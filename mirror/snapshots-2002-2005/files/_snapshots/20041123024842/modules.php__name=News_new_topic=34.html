Annual Auction for the American Way.

Madonna is one of the celebrities that have donated one-of-a-kind memorabilia to People for the American Way’s (PFAW) annual "Auction for the American Way." The auction will benefit PFAW’s ongoing efforts to promote free speech, civil rights, and religious liberty.

Madonna item is her Ray of Light Platinum album customized with the winner’s name.

The first items will be available on Sunday, November 28. New items will be added Daily until the auction closes on Sunday, December 5. Visit www.pfaw.org to check out the items available; link straight to the Auction for the American Way eBay site starting November 28.

"Artists, authors and entertainers rely on freedom of speech every day," said People For the American Way President Ralph G. Neas. "That’s why so many of them are supporting our work to protect freedom of artistic expression and the right to dissent, precious liberties that are in severe danger of being eroded in the current political climate. We are deeply grateful for everyone who is helping us promote and protect the constitutional principles of privacy, equality under the law, and freedom of thought, expression, and religion."

Thanks to Vincy for bringing this to our attention.

Some more on the Charity Auction of Miniature Books.

FIRST PUBLISHED ON A MADONNA WEBSITE

Think big by thinking small. This was the idea behind a plan to raise money for some of the poorest people in Britain. Take 25 miniature books (just one inch tall), send them to some of the greatest artists, writers and statesmen of our time and ask them to fill the books with their words or pictures. The results were spectacular.
As we already reported more than once, the books will be auctioned at Sotheby’s on Monday 1 November 2004 for the 999 Club, a charity based in Deptford, south-east London, which helps the homeless and helpless.

Madonna has written out the lyrics to her song "American Life" and drawn her version of the Stars and Stripes. "I’d like to express... my extreme point of view. I’m not a Christian and I’m not a Jew... I’m just living out... the American Dream... and I just realised that nothing is what it seems." 
Madonna's miniature book is estimate £1,200–£1,500.

[BREAKING NEWS] Christie's Pop Memorabilia auction results.

The Christie's Pop Memorabilia auction took place yesterday in London (South Kensington). We presented an ample treatment of the this last week.

The Madonna outfit consisting of a black leather backless bra and thong, joined together with a network of straps, worn by Madonna for her 1992 book Sex, sold for 4,500 pounds. It was valued at as much as 4,000 - 6,000 British pounds.

Four out of nine Madonna items failed to sell at the Christie’s music memorabilia auction. Two black-and- white publicity photos, one showing the star in black stockings with her legs crossed to form a T-shape, took in 2,400 pounds after a round of bidding, compared with an estimate of 350 pounds.

A London collector, Peter Dita, paid 200 pounds for a presentation award for the album Music, a 33 percent discount from Christie’s low estimate.

More on exotic black leather Madonna outfit.


Pop Memorabilia
29 September 2004, 2:00 pm
85 Old Brompton Road, London (South Kensington) 

An exotic black leather one-piece outfit worn by Madonna in a photo shoot for her 1992 book Sex will be one of the lots to be auctioned at the Pop Memorabilia Christie's auction next week in London (09/29/2004).

This outfit comprises a backless bra and thong connected via a network of straps from a choker fastening at the neck with chrome poppers. It's signed on the crotch in silver felt pen Madonna xoxox.

It's accompanied by a book Sex, London: Martin Secker & Warburg, 1992, with original aluminium covers, numbered 0858594, featuring a photograph of Madonna wearing an identical outfit on page 9 (2)... as you can see on the pic above.

It has been previously auctioned at Christie’s South Kensington on Thursday 26th August, 1993. 

It's estimated 4,000 - 6,000 British pounds.

More items to be auctioned: a hat worn by Elton John, a typescript letter, signed, from John Lennon, many Beatles' itmes, and so on.

Madonna's naughty ensemble up for grabs.

Taken from Hello Magazine:

22 SEPTEMBER 2004

 
A sexy piece of pop history, once worn by none other than Madonna, is one of the many items fans can pick up at a Christie's music memorabilia auction next week.

The exotic black leather one-piece outfit, sported by Madonna in her 1992 book Sex, is one of the prize lots up for grabs, expected to fetch upwards of £6,000. Comprised of a backless bra and thong connected with a network of straps, the naughty ensemble is signed by the pop star – along with an affectionate XOXOX – in a not-so-discreet spot.

Madonna's risqué leather costume joins decidedly less racy items going under the hammer on September 29 in London. Other standouts for sale include an elaborate Black Sabbath stage cape worn by frontman Ozzy Osbourne in 1974 – estimated to bring in £4,000 – as well as a 1960s-era striped satin jacket owned by Rolling Stone Keith Richards, a steal at £500-700.

Some of the priciest lots up for bid include a Keith Moon drum kit from the late Sixties emblazoned with The Who insignia, estimated at £10,000-15,000, and a rare set of cartoon sketches drawn by John Lennon around 1957. That Beatles-related item, one of many, including letters, concert posters and other keepsakes, is expected to fetch at least £15,000-20,000.

[Read It First Here] Ex-Madonna Mercedes appeared on Deeper And Deeper.
Ex-Madonna 1969 Mercedes-Benz 280SE Cabriolet - white with black leather interior and black top - will be on sale on August 12 at a Los Angeles Christie's International auction.

The 280SE offered at this auction was formerly the personal property of Madonna, and also appeared in her 1992 music video Deeper And Deeper. Since purchased at auction from Madonna in 1995, this vehicle has been sparingly used and garage kept and shows approximately 70,000 miles. Most recently it has undergone a thorough servicing by one of the country's largest Mercedes-Benz dealerships and is reported to be an excellent driver, but currently the windshield wipers and air conditioner are out of commission. This is an extraordinary opportunity to purchase a sleek, classic automobile with a celebrity history.

Estimate: 40,000 - 60,000 U.S. dollars.
