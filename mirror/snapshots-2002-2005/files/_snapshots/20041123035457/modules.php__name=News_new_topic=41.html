[Gossip] Madonna in Love Match.

Sports newspaper Olè is running an absurd "love gossip" about Madonna and Argentine Rugby player Ignacio Corletto.

According to the paper Madonna met Ignacio in a Club in Paris in September during the French leg of  the re-Invention Tour. Last night, in the TV program "Intrusive", the journalist Luis Fortune assured that when he called Ignacio asking him the truth about this story , "he  cut the communication on the phone". 

Ignacio is currently playing with Stade Francais, the French Rugby Team.

[BREAKING NEWS] The Scoop on Caresse' Farewell.

Madonna’s manager moves on
By Jeannette Walls / The Scoop
MSNBC


Madonna’s longtime manager is quitting.

Caresse Henry, who has been Madonna’s manager for 13 years, is leaving. “As I understand it, she’s not thrilled about the direction that Madonna’s interests have been taking her,” says a source. “Besides, 13 years is a long time and Madonna’s a demanding boss.”

When called for comment, Madonna’s spokeswoman, Liz Rosenberg, emailed a statement that Madonna and Henry “have mutually agreed to go their separate ways” but said that both Madonna and Henry would decline further comment.

Some more on Madonna's brand new fitness regime.
Every UK newspaper reports today that Madonna has signed up to the latest exercise fad - and it involves being stretched on a rack.

Gyrotonics is normally used by ballet dancers and gymnasts, but Madonna has begun visiting a North London gym once a week for her own 90-minute session, which sees her strapped down with legs in stirrups and pulleys stretching her limbs.

A member of the gym told the Daily Mirror: “It may resemble an instrument of medieval torture but it works miracles.

“When your feet are strapped in the stirrups it’s a bit like getting ready for a gynaecological examination.

“But it’s great for your posture and exercises parts of your body other methods can’t reach.”

The paper claims the mum-of-two pays £90 for her sessions with expert James D’Silva, who she first started seeing for Pilates classes. 

Madonna particularly likes the fact that the £3,500 machine develops longer limbs and a more elegant look.

The system was originally developed by a Romanian ballet dancer to help prepare dancers for the stage.

Madonna to adopt and annoyed with Eminem?

Gossip from Star magazine:

While Madonna is healthy enough to get pregnant, it just hasn’t happened, so she’s considering adopting a baby in early 2005, says a source. The insider stresses that even with talk of adoption, Madonna and Guy Ritchie haven’t given up on having a child of their own. In fact, they would like to have another baby and adopt a child.

Meanwhile, she has less than good feelings for Eminem these days. The source says that "she has been trying to get him to do something for her next CD," but is now annoyed with him because "he dissed her in his new video." In "Just Lose It," Eminem dresses in drag as a "Vogue"- ing Madonna, whose coneshaped bra catches fire.
