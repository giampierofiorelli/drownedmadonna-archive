[BREAKING NEWS] About the Revolver shooting.

Our insiders revealed us the location in Clerkenwell (London) where "Revolver" is being shot. At this stage we cannot publish the exact address. However, we can say that last week they filmed 3 days inside and outside an old-style house. The shooting was mainly based on action moves between Jason Statham’s character (Jack) and his jailors. All these scenes will be featured in central part of the movie.

The crew will fly to the Isle of Man in middle of November, with the shooting scheduled by the end of November (tentatively) till Christmas. Guy Ritchie and some of the crew have flown today to the isle to scout the locations; they will fly back to London after the weekend.

While the casting calls for the leading roles took place in Los Angeles just when Madonna was going to hit the road with the re-Invention Tour, the production is still looking for extras to be cast for the shooting on the Isle. [BREAKING NEWS] Elton Ready to Apologize to Madonna.

Associated Press Oct. 28, 2004 12:01 PM NEW YORK

 - Elton John is ready to apologize to Madonna after recently accusing the pop star of lip-synching. "I don't want to escalate it because I like Madonna," the 57-year-old singer-songwriter tells Entertainment Weekly. "She's been to my house for dinner. It was something that was said in the heat of the moment, and probably should not have been said."

 "Would I apologize to her if I saw her?" said John. "Yeah, because I don't want to hurt any artist's feelings. It was my fault. I instigated the whole thing. But it applies to all those bloody teenage singers."

John made the comment when presenting the Best Live Act award during the Q awards in London earlier this month. In EW's Nov. 6 issue, John described the event as "a very drunken lunch."

"The reaction to it was so hysterical," he said. "It was like I said, 'I think all gays should be killed or I think Hitler was right.' I just said someone was lip-synching. I'm not afraid to speak my mind. I'm not going to mellow with age. I get more enraged about things as I get older because you see that these injustices go on."

(Madonna's spokeswoman, Liz Rosenberg, has said the singer doesn't lip-synch and indicated that she hadn't taken John's remarks too seriously.)

John, whose new CD, "Peachtree Road," hits stores Nov. 9, wasn't remiss about recently calling photographers in Taipei, Taiwan, "rude, vile pigs."

"The thing in Taiwan was unfortunate," he told the magazine. "We arrived at 12:15 at night, we were going through the terminal, and we were just ambushed. (The photographers) were pretty hostile. They were allowed to stay in immigration and photograph us."

In the article, John also revealed he loves Eminem's new album, "Encore"; might collaborate with the Scissor Sisters; doesn't see himself stopping recording; and wouldn't have a "Jennifer Lopez wedding."

Condomania's Madonna Condom Giveaway Winners.
FIRST PUBLISHED ON A MADONNA WEBSITE


LOS ANGELES, Oct. 27 /PRNewswire/ -- Condomania (http://www.condomania.com), America's First Condom Store, announced today the winners of its Madonna Condom Giveaway. Condomania produced the contest to encourage the creative promotion of safer sex awareness. CEO Adam Glickman explains, "Over the last four years the Bush administration has distorted scientific knowledge to further a political and ideological agenda. Consequently, many young people have been denied access to honest sexual education and the information needed to protect them. For our part, we wanted to provide a fun way for health agencies to fight back and increase awareness about safer sex, STDs and the importance of condoms."
The contest was open to nonprofit organizations that were interested developing a fun and unique way to use the condoms in their outreach activities. Every entry received 100 free Condomania Condoms just for sending in a proposal.
The grand prize of 21,000 Madonna Condoms (7,000 3-packs featuring the pop singer's licensed image from her early days) went to Douglas County AIDS Project (DCAP) of Lawrence, Kansas. They specialize in HIV/AIDS services in a three county area in northeast Kansas. They plan to put the Madonna Condoms to use during Pride Week at the University of Kansas where they will host a Madonna look-alike drag show that will entertain and educate about safer sex and HIV/AIDS prevention.
Upon hearing the news, DCAP's Executive Director Geri Summer exclaimed, "We are so thrilled! It came at a perfect time, too, as we had to reduce our budget this year due to cuts in funding."
The second place prize of 6,000 Madonna Condoms went to Vox: Voices for Planned Parenthood of Franklin & Marshall College in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. They plan to throw an 80s Safer Sex Party on campus with plenty of music by Madonna, sexy food, and games that educate about safer sex.
The third place prize of 3,000 Madonna Condoms went to the Planned Parenthood Association of Pennsylvania. They plan to hand out the Madonna Condoms during a "Pub Crawl" which they use to train their volunteers in the art of grassroots campaigning. They walk the streets and visit bars, wearing shirts proclaiming their support for safer sex, handing out supplies, and registering supporters to vote and get involved with Planned Parenthood.

Some more on Madonna's brand new fitness regime.
Every UK newspaper reports today that Madonna has signed up to the latest exercise fad - and it involves being stretched on a rack.

Gyrotonics is normally used by ballet dancers and gymnasts, but Madonna has begun visiting a North London gym once a week for her own 90-minute session, which sees her strapped down with legs in stirrups and pulleys stretching her limbs.

A member of the gym told the Daily Mirror: “It may resemble an instrument of medieval torture but it works miracles.

“When your feet are strapped in the stirrups it’s a bit like getting ready for a gynaecological examination.

“But it’s great for your posture and exercises parts of your body other methods can’t reach.”

The paper claims the mum-of-two pays £90 for her sessions with expert James D’Silva, who she first started seeing for Pilates classes. 

Madonna particularly likes the fact that the £3,500 machine develops longer limbs and a more elegant look.

The system was originally developed by a Romanian ballet dancer to help prepare dancers for the stage.

A little More on The Rack.
Oct 27 2004-
POP queen Madonna has embraced another torturous celebrity fitness fad.

The 46-year-old is raving about a muscle-stretching exercise machine dubbed 'The Rack', after the medieval torture device.

Clients are strapped on to the £3500 wooden contraption and then pulleys and winches give their limbs a tough workout.

The proper name for The Rack is the Gyrotonic Expansion System, developed by Romanian ballet dancer Juliu Hovath and popular with top dancers and Olympic gymnasts.

Madonna is said to be delighted with the results because the machine helps create long, lean muscles that look toned, rather than masculine bulges.

A member of the exclusive London gym Madonna visits once a week said: 'It might resemble an instrument of medieval torture but it works miracles.

'It's great for your posture and exercises parts of your body other methods can't reach.'

Mum-of-two Madonna, who lives in Marylebone with husband Guy Ritchie, 35, and children, Lourdes, eight, and Rocco, three, pays £90 for her 90-minute sessions with fitness guru James D'Silva.

Friends say she started visiting him for Pilates but soon became hooked on the new apparatus.

The singer has never been slow to try unconventional techniques and alternative therapies, including Ashtanga yoga and reiki.

She also follows a strict macro-biotic diet, which involves adding wholegrain cereals,pulses and soy products to meals.

Some more on the Charity Auction of Miniature Books.

FIRST PUBLISHED ON A MADONNA WEBSITE

Think big by thinking small. This was the idea behind a plan to raise money for some of the poorest people in Britain. Take 25 miniature books (just one inch tall), send them to some of the greatest artists, writers and statesmen of our time and ask them to fill the books with their words or pictures. The results were spectacular.
As we already reported more than once, the books will be auctioned at Sotheby’s on Monday 1 November 2004 for the 999 Club, a charity based in Deptford, south-east London, which helps the homeless and helpless.

Madonna has written out the lyrics to her song "American Life" and drawn her version of the Stars and Stripes. "I’d like to express... my extreme point of view. I’m not a Christian and I’m not a Jew... I’m just living out... the American Dream... and I just realised that nothing is what it seems." 
Madonna's miniature book is estimate £1,200–£1,500.
