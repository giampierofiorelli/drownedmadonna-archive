Regarding producers or collaborators, we have some exciting news. Andrew Barlow (Andy), amazing mastermind and musician behind Lamb, is working in a demo for Madonna. Madonna is a very big fan of this British duo. Andrew Barlow worked with Guy Sigsworth in Lamb "What Sound" album featuring the incredible track "Gabriel". Guy introduced Andy to Madonna and Madonna is waiting for a demo that Andy is doing for her, with a trip-hop pop feeling to it. Madonna is thrilled to work with Andy.

Regarding the Martin Gore demo that he did for Madonna, we can inform that Madonna is going to rewrite some of the lyrics of that song and record some raw vocals for Martin. This demo has yet not title. So we called it "Martin Track".

But now she wants to re-work the music with Orbit.

Again this is all in the process of writing and creating new stuff, so this is purely demos only. We have no certainty if we ever going to be able to hear these tunes. Hopefully we will. Again this is all subject to change and nothing is official. As usual, Drowned Madonna is honoured to promote new artists who are related somehow  to Madonna. Singer/actor/model Eric West gave us a brief interview in which he told us about his meeting with Madonna.

Eric is actually working on the untitled "Lindsay Lohan" flick that is due for 2006. Eric's debut album "Half | Life" will be release in early 2005, while the first single from the album will be out in December 2004. For more info check his official website, www.eric-west.com.

Drowned Madonna: How many times have you met Madonna?

Eric: I was lucky enough to meet the Material Girl twice.

DM: Can you tell us something of the first time?

E: At last years MTV Video Music Awards I sat about 4 rows behind her, as soon as I got a chance I made sure that I introduced myself to her.

DM: And what about the second time...

E: I met her at the Forum Club in Los Angeles in May or June when I attended her world tour after party. I went to the party... just being there was enough, but my manager introduced us (again). I explained to her how much I was a fan, and that I met her last year at the MTV Awards, but didn’t really get a chance to say much to her at the awards. Then I went on to tell her that one day it would be an honor to work with her, whether it was writing, dancing, acting with her.

DM: Did you talk about your upcoming album "Half | Life"?

E: I told her about my album... she seemed interested in hearing it and said send her a copy. Of course I said I will, though I don’t know where she lives.

DM: Did she make a good impression on you?

E: She was extremely pleasant, it will always be one of the standout moments of my career. At lot of people make her out to be arrogant, and not fan friendly, but I can say otherwise, for such a superstar, she makes you feel as if you’re talking to an old friend.

DM: Thank you Eric. It was a pleasure chatting with you...

E: Thank you for supporting my music as a new artist, and my upcoming movies. Ya’ll rock!!!

Claudio Camaione and Paolo Cilione migrated to New York City at the turn of the century then further on the Southern California to build their studio for recording and film editing in a Villa overlooking Silver Lake.

At the start of their collaboration they scored a few short films and started directing indie pieces of their own featuring Clifton Collins from Traffic, NYC "it girl" Debi Mazar and Italian starlet Claudia Gerini.

In 2003, under the name Blow-Up, they wrote, produced, performed and recorded their debut American LP Exploding Plastic Pleasure. Packed with rhythmic feuled compositions, club remixes and songs in no less than 4 different languages, the LP features guess performances by Debbie Harry, Lydia Lunch and Dee Dee Ramone.

This gained them a reputation resulting in offers for Blow-Up's involvement in writing, producing and remixing for up and coming acts as well as some of the top names in music. The Flaming Lips chose Blow-Up to remix Ego Tripping and Madonna commissioned them to lead off her single Love Profusion with a Blow-Up mix after hearing what they did for Hollywood.

Keep an eye out for Blow-Up remixes by Yoko Ono, Esthero, Blondie and a 25 year anniversary release of  Do Ya Think I'm Sexy by Rod Stewart.

THEY WENT on tour with Blondie again this year starting August 26 and are doing so many projects including their new video for their album "Exploding Plastic Pleasure" that will come out in Europe and Italy around October with some new songs in it.

They just completed a single for Robert De Niro's daughter Drena De Niro. It's a new version of Pensiero Stupendo, the old Patty Pravo song. It'll be up on the www.blowup.ws soon.
