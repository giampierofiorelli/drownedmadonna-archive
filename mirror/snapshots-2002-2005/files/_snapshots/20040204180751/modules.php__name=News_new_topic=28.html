Madonna will attend the Grammy Awards.
So it's official: Madonna will attend the Grammy Awards. American TV stations are broadcasting a Grammy advert which announces that Madonna and Britney Spears are going to be there.
As we have reported, an advert published on People Magazine showed Madonna's name on the list of the Grammy performers.
We'll know soon if she is going to perform and, in this case, which song she'll sing. Rumour has it that Madonna will perform "Die Another Day", because this song is nominated and because it's a Grammy "rule" that singers perform a nominated song. But there's another rumour too: Madonna and Britney could sing "Me Against The Music."

Madonna has received the Lifetime Achievement Award.
Madonna received the Lifetime Achievement Award - an award commemorating her hits-filled career - at the NRJ Music Awards. Britney Spears presented Madonna with the award. Clutching the award, Madonna said: "My ambition may be American, and I may have married a Brit, but my heart belongs to France."
Madonna didn't win the other awards.
Madonna didn't sing - as it was rumoured - but she bewitched everyone with her beauty and charisma.

Christina Aguilera, who was also at the music awards, said she regretted not sharing in the kiss between Madonna and Britney in August. "I would have been open and up for anything, but the music wouldn't allow it," she told reporters backstage.

Madonna scores lifetime achievement award at NRJ
Dido Scores in France as Madonna and Britney Look On
Sat January 24, 2004 06:07 PM ET

CANNES, France (Reuters) - Dido on Saturday added two awards to a bulging trophy case, scoring best international female artist and international artist of the year at France's biggest music awards, hosted by European radio group NRJ.

"It's all a bit too good to be true. I've had a great time tonight," the notoriously publicity-shy singer said upon receiving her awards.

The night's biggest winner was French rock group Kyo, who cleaned up four awards, including best French album, French song and French group of the year.

The award show got off to a hip-shaking start when Britney Spears, fresh from her rapid-fire marriage/divorce in Las Vegas this month, performed a crotch-grabbing rendition of her new single "Toxic."

Moments later, Britney met Madonna on stage -- their first joint appearance since sharing an infamous public kiss in August.

Britney presented Madonna with an award commemorating her hits-filled career. Clutching the award, Madonna said: "My ambition may be American, and I may have married a Brit, but my heart belongs to France."

Christina Aguilera, who was also at the music awards, said she regretted not sharing in the kiss between Madonna and Britney in August.

"I would have been open and up for anything, but the music wouldn't allow it," she told reporters backstage.

Other winners on Saturday night included goth-inspired rockers Evanescence, who claimed international breakthrough artists of the year; Justin Timberlake, who scored best international male artist; and Blue and Elton John, who took the prize for international song of the year for "Sorry Seems to be the Hardest Word."

    Madonna nominated at the NRJ Music Awards.
    Madonna has been nominated at the NRJ Music Awards 2004, organized by French radio station NRJ. Madonna has a nomination in the "Female Artist of the Year" category; the other candidates are Kylie Minogue, Dido, Pink and the Italian Laura Pausini. "American Life" is nominated as "International Album of the Year"; the other nominations are Placebo's "Sleeping With Ghosts", Justin Timberlake's "Justified", Dido's "Life For Rent" and Shania Twain's "Up".

    Madonna has introduced the VMALAs.
    The second annual "MTV Video Music Awards Latin America" (VMALAs) was broadcast live from Miami Beach's Jackie Gleason Theater on Thursday, October 23rd. The event is organized by MTV Networks Latin America.
    The "MTV Video Music Awards Latin America" nominees in 16 categories (only 3 categories are international) were selected by a "Music and Video Academy" comprised of music industry professionals and MTV viewers. Ballots were cast via Internet. The winner for each category was chosen by viewers who casted their votes exclusively online at MTV Latin America's website MTVla.com between September 11th and October 8th 2003.

    Madonna has introduced the show. They has aired a pre-recorded clip where she was speaking in Spanish. Madonna was nominated for the "Best Pop International Act" award. The other candidates were Avril Lavigne, Christina Aguilera, Justin Timberlake and Robbie Williams. It was rumoured that she has won the award. Unfortunately, the award went to Avril Lavigne.
