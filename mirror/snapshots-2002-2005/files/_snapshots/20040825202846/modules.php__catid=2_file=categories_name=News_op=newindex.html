live



Just in from the 2nd nite at Earls Court London and WOW what a show, gets better even 3rd time round!!!

Tonight she again forgot the words to Material Girl, this must be coming pretty standard now.

She also said how when she first came to England 20 years ago how much she hated it and hated the cold, didn't understand all the drinking, and generally just hated it and then said how now she lives in London which got the crowd going, and that her band are all British and she has a British husband and then said ’Come on, I'm wearing a fucking kilt for christs sake!’.

She also said how she invited the Queen to the gig, and then said how it probably wouldn't be too hard to find a queen in the audience!!

The crowd were ok but i heard louder cheering in NYC in june. And again she asked the crowd to get up and dance for ’Like A Prayer’ because it's a non-sit-down song!!! Other than that the night was fantastic.

Can I also say, well done to the crowd for the best Mexican wave I've ever seen at any gig!!! Amazing!

I managed to get video clips of Vogue and Nobody Knows Me, a very short clip of Lament and the chorus of Music. I will post these video files as soon as possible!!! I also take some pics from ’Into The Groove’ onwards (the pics published above).

Written by Giampiero on Thursday, July 22 @ W. Europe Daylight Time EXCLUSIVE: What went behind the scenes in Toronto. [20 reads].
live



I was in the building around 4:30pm waiting to see Madonna's escort motorcade to arrive but got called to a security meeting. When I was done it by 5:30pm a supervisor asked me to come into the bowl of the arena, and there was Madonna standing on the stage like 20 feet from me... the guy in the Toronto Sun pic on sunday, opening the door for her from the Kabbahal Centre is her personal bodyguard, and I met him in the bowl and he asked me and one other person go around inside the bowl to make sure none of the staff there where peeking in (closed sound check)... After she was done I walked over about 20 feet from the stage waiting for her to exit the stage to give the all clear and she looked over to me, I nervously waved hi, and she waved back with a smile and then with her bodyguards and dancers exited the stage so we could open the arena for the fans. For the sound check she wore a hot bright pink summer top and white pants, shes very small in person.
During the show she was giggling like a school child and talked ot theaudience and she said "let's stay here all night, we could have a sit in protest and i could sing all my songs, and lets see  them try to arrest me for that". When she started to sing "Do have have ot change my name" she then yelled humourly "Don't call me esther!!" "Should I loose some weight?" Well maybe and then "Am i gonna be a star" she said "Well duh yeah i am one.." and start laughing and enjoying heself."
Special thanks to Mr Kevin Young for this story.
