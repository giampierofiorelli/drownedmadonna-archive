[Rumours] Ever more details on two new songs..
Madonna discusses her relationship with her father in the song “Haunted”. The track which is a string sweeping ballad mixed with a slight Moroccan percussion. The tracks lyrics address her perception of her father while she was a child and now. It is almost as if the track Mother and Father from American Life was simply a jumping point for Madonna’s theme of speaking about her father. Some of the lyrics are:

“And I’m haunted/by the visions in the grieving room/daddies working hard/never saw the clouded doom/eclipsing his broken heart”

The song “If You Were Here” is a hard bass driven dance track unlike anything Madge has recorded. The track is very techno acid but with a trip hop twist.

“If you were here/everything would be much easier/If you were here/Damn I’d be happier/If you/If you/If you were here”

Again mind you lyrics and songs are subject to change and these are only rumored info, nothing official has been said about them.

Upcoming Projects
[BREAKING NEWS] More details on what we already Told You.

Today DrownedMadonna and fellow fansite madonnalicious  received the same news about Madonna's rumored projects for 2005 - of which DrownedMadonna already talked about last week (link) and even this week in some exclusive info.

Madonna has completed two tracks with Stuart Price they are titled "Haunted" and "If You Were Here". This is in no way confirmation that these tracks will appear on her pending album currently under the working title "Project 05", simply that the songs have been written and printed.

The collaboration with Missy Elliot was on a track titled  "Cali Dreams" and is about the cost of fame. If the track is included, Madonna's only contribution are background vocals....this will NOT be a "Me Against The Music" type thing. It was rumoured to be called "Liquid Dreams".

Madonna has also reached out to an AMERICAN producer for her next album, tapping Emilio Estefan Jr. to crank out a demo for her. Madonna wants a latin track for her next album and is a big fan of Estefans work.

Also...talks for "Streets of Paris" are warming up some. However, word is that Cate Blanchett is also interested in the role Madonna has her eyeson...and Mondino is no longer in the running to be director.

[EXCLUSIVE] Latest Exclusive Info.

Warner Music Group is planning to re-release Madonna's 1989 album "Like A Prayer" in early 05 (March). The newly remastered CD should include bonus remix tracks (Like A Prayer - Shep Pettibone remix, Express Yourself - Stop and Go remix, Keep it Together remix). If the project gets a green light from Madonna herself, it will be released towards the end of March.

As far as her remix album is concerned, the CD is tentively put on the back burner. It was to be titled "American Dream" but it's not certain if it will be released commercially.

In January Madonna will resume work on her documentary whose working title has changed to "Nobody Knows Me", but probably it shall change again.

Upcoming Projects
[Exclusive Rumours] WHAT'S BEING COOKED UP?

Insiders informed DrownedMadonna that the musical film project could be put off till the end of next year.

As far as the musical is concerned, "Hello Sucker" is now in a lull because Madonna owes Warner Bros. a new studio album and she is working on it....primarily... as well as on some new music to be possibly a soundtrack for her documentary which is due to premiere at Cannes in May and have a world wide theatrical release in June/July.

Madonna’s documentary (to which insiders refer to with the working title of "Reinvented Process") has yet to be officially picked up by a distributor,but is most likely going to be Warner Bros. subsidiary New Line that will release the film. Madonna has a development deal with New Line and it would seem to be the easiest route. Things with Miramax have not been that good (nor that bad) since Madonna pulled out of “Fiddlefest” and “Chicago” . Besides as you know Madonna is now looking to adapting The English Roses into a movie. And last but not least,Madonna is also up for a role in a film titled 'The Streets Of Paris" wich apparently has attracted also the attention of sir Anthony Hopkins and Jude Law. The screenwriter for "Streets Of Paris" is Michael Cunningham the writer of "The Hours" and "A Home At The End Of The World"...Madonna, again is only in talks with the film, but she does seem very interested. A recent insider with Fine Line films, the company that now wants to produce/distribute the movie, has also mentioned Clint Eastwood as a director.

Jean Baptiste Mondino has been looking to get into film for a long time and though he has done music videos, this film “The streets of Paris” is a definite low budget art house film, that could represents his debut as filmaker. There was also talk of Sofia Coppola directing but that has fizzled out.

Anyway if Madonna gets the part for "Paris",  "Hello Sucker" could be postponed.

Again this is all subject to change and nothing is official since it's all at the early stage.

Upcoming Projects
[EXCLUSIVE] How the future could be.

Now that the Caresse Henry news has been officially released we have been authorized to publish the following info:

We have been informed that we are probably months and months away from a DVD release of the re-Invention Tour.

One plan has the new documentary debuting at Cannes 2005 then rolling out to the rest of the world in the following months. If it hits US by summer then a DVD can be expected next November and it might be combined with the entire re-Invention Tour Video Super Special Directors Pack. Or it might just be the documentary with re-Invention Tour to be scheduled some time after.

And talking about the Cannes Film Festival, we have been told that it could be the Ritchies Show since Europa Corp. is thinking of premiering Guy Ritchie's Revolver at Cannes.

Madonna is writing and recording as she always does. A possible album release is still tentative for next November to coincide with the documentary DVD. It might be an extension of Warner deal with same royalties for one album. If it does well than Madonna can get a better deal and prove "American Life" was a fluke.

Madonna also wants to film a new movie (we are not talking about Revolver), but it won't be till summer 2005 with an 2006 release.
