Upcoming Projects
[EXCLUSIVE] Madonna And Warner in Music Match.

Last week, our insiders told us (and our readers too) that Madonna is still very unhappy with all the changes at WB. We asked then to let us know more about the current relationship between Madonna and her label.

Apparently we have to look back at the American Life era. The album was originally to sound much different than what was released. If you remember Madonna had made comments that it was hard and very heavy. This is true. Warner and/or someone on Madonna's camp did not like the idea of Madonna coming out with a harder look and sound, and she was asked to "soften" the sound of the album. Madonna's original instincts were correct as American Life was her least selling album. The tracks originally sounded much more like they do on the Remixed And Revisited Ep.

This was the beginning of Madges dissapointment for Warner.

As for her new album it sounds very hard, yet still Madonna and the tracks are similar again to Remixed And Revisited. Several tracks are floating around to friends, family and associates.

Once again there is no word, currently, on a possible release for the album though it is 95% certain it will be released on Warners.

[BREAKING NEWS] Some More About The ReInvention DVD.

Optimus, a good insider is providing the Madonna's community with the latest about the DVD/Documentary project.

We decided to publish his info (which matches our inside exclusives published back in Autumn 2004) because he is absolutely realible.

"There is no scheduled release date at the moment but the DVD will be released. In fact the DVD is near completed last I heard. I repeat, the RIT will be released.

No one at Warner including Liz Rosenburg will give a straight answer because there is no confirmed date yet. Until a solid 100% release date is confirmed they will deflect all inquiries with generic vague statements.

The reason for the delay is that they want to focus on the documentary. They want to delay the release of the RIT so as to draw more people to the doc. The doc is Madonna's passion and focus at the moment.

As it stands it seems the concert will be released on either sometime after the release of the documentary or at the same time that the documentary itself is released on DVD in order to maximize exposure to both.

It was at one point planned for Spring 2005 but then they started playing with the idea of releasing it after the documentary. There is a chance they could stick with their original release of Spring 2005 but at this point it is unlikely. This is why there is so much confusion among retailers because they did receive advance release info only to have it retracted and left obscure.

Last bit I can throw in is that there might be a single released at the same time as the documentary. Right now the idea of releasing Imagine is being toyed with. Part of the reason Madonna sang it at Tsunami Aid was as a test.

Just remember that with Madonna everything is done for a reason. She's a very smart business woman."

A special thanks to Optimus

[Exclusive Rumours] WHAT'S BEING COOKED UP?

Insiders informed DrownedMadonna that the musical film project could be put off till the end of next year.

As far as the musical is concerned, "Hello Sucker" is now in a lull because Madonna owes Warner Bros. a new studio album and she is working on it....primarily... as well as on some new music to be possibly a soundtrack for Guy's "Revolver" or  her documentary which is due to premiere at Cannes in May and have a world wide theatrical release in June/July.

Madonna’s documentary (to which insiders refer to with the working title of "Reinvented Process") has yet to be officially picked up by a distributor,but is most likely going to be Warner Bros. subsidiary New Line that will release the film. Madonna has a development deal with New Line and it would seem to be the easiest route. Things with Miramax have not been that good (nor that bad) since Madonna pulled out of “Fiddlefest” and “Chicago” . Besides as you know Madonna is now looking to adapting The English Roses into a movie. And last but not least,Madonna is also up for a role in a film titled 'The Streets Of Paris" wich apparently has attracted also the attention of sir Anthony Hopkins and Jude Law. The screenwriter for "Streets Of Paris" is Michael Cunningham the writer of "The Hours" and "A Home At The End Of The World"...Madonna, again is only in talks with the film, but she does seem very interested. A recent insider with Fine Line films, the company that now wants to produce/distribute the movie, has also mentioned Clint Eastwood as a director.

Jean Baptiste Mondino has been looking to get into film for a long time and though he has done music videos, this film “The streets of Paris” is a definite low budget art house film, that could represents his debut as filmaker. There was also talk of Sofia Coppola directing but that has fizzled out.

Anyway if Madonna gets the part for "Paris",  "Hello Sucker" could be postponed.

Again this is all subject to change and nothing is official since it's all at the early stage.
