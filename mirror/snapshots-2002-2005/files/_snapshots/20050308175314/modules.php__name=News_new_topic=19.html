Drowned Madonna
A Possible Explanation On The 'Cloud' Word.

Madonna's fans are questioning about the word "Cloud" which appeared on Madonna's left hand during her visit at the Mercer Hotel in NYC last week. Drownedmadonna thanks to the French correspondent Claudine published for the first time on a Madonna website those "Cloud" pics taken from French magazine Public.

One of the most qualified hypothesis says that Madonna signed on her hand the name of a Mirwais song which will be featured on Fischerspooner new album Odyssey, to be released on April 11th.

Here is an excerpt from an article on Fischerspooner out some minutes ago:

"FISCHERSPOONER, ‘ODYSSEY’
TRACK-BY-TRACK

“JUST LET GO”
The propulsive lead single picks up neatly where #1 left off and notably features psychedelic-sounding percussion instrument the Vibraslap, made famous by Jimi Hendrix’s “All Along the Watchtower.”

The vocals, in contrast to most other tracks, were written and recorded on the spot in one day, an apt reflection of the lyrical idea. Casey explains: “This song is about battles -- battles between the mental and the physical, between the rational and the intuitive, between mortality and greatness, timelessness and relevance.”

“CLOUD”
Casey: “The ideas of the Romantics became a huge influence on the album because they were basically the roots of rock ‘n’ roll. The Romantic Hero led directly to the modern idea of a rock star. ‘Cloud‘ relates to that as the a tale of a mad, obsessive genius who gets so caught up in his creation that he completely loses himself in it.”

Co-produced by Mirwais, the track was based on a song idea by NY artist John Wolfington and influenced heavily by Warren’s love of the Cure’s unique chorus/post chorus song structures.

Drowned Madonna
[BREAKING NEWS] KRAMM DOES IT AGAIN!

Do you remember Kramm?

Our friend Mark , aka Kramm, designed a simple blue T-shirt with a red line saying MadonnaSummer, for a store in San Francisco called "Body".  Surprisingly, Madonna was spotted in NYCity wearing it!!!!
Mrs Henry, Madonna's manager, said to Mark "Someone bought it for her (Madonna) when we were in San Francisco and she loved it - so there you have it!  I will pass this along!" More fans wanted this shirt and were disappointed when the San Francisco store sold out....

Only 2 days ago Mark discovered that his t-shirts have been bootlegged...So Mark decided to send out this statement to us and to all Madonna's fans:


".I am the designer of the original "Madonnasummer" t-shirt. I have
recently noticed some individuals bootlegging this shirt on various
internet sites. These people have stolen my design and produced it
without my permission. Their shirts are copies and in no way similar to
the ones originally offered by me. The actual blank shirt that the logo
is printed on cannot be obtained by them. They are exclusive to the
printing company I use.

I understand that there were many more fans that wanted this shirt and were disappointed when the San Francisco store sold out. I have now decided to re-print the ORIGINAL t-shirt, that Madonna herself wore, and offer them as a fixed price auction on ebay. I'm working as fast as I can to get them ready for sale as soon as possible. I will notify Drowned Madonna the moment they are ready to go, so check back within the next few weeks.

Thanks for everything,
Mark "

WE are very pleased that Mark chose our website to make this statement and we wish this kind of problem will be solved in the best way wich means as soon as his new t-shirts will be done..Stay Tuned for more on this story!
