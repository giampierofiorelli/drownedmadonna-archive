Into The Chart Grooves
Madonna Sales

Ol' standby The Immaculate Collection increased sales a bit this week, to 3,800 units. That's not enough to rank among the top 50 catalog chart, but have no fear- I'm certina it will return there in a matter of weeks.

GHV2 also saw an increase, to more than 900 copies scanned last week. Expect it- like TIC- to see sales increases as the re-Invention Tour nears.

On the Billboard scene, Usher's Confessions album continues to sell fabulously, as he holds down the top slot on the Hot 100 again. D12 (Eminem's rap project) crashes in at #1 on the Billboard 200, with 544,000 units sold last week. On the "divas" front, Janet Jackson's Damita Jo continued its tumble, down 15-25 in its 5th week.

More later.

Into The Chart Grooves
Welcome to Into the Chart Grooves!

Hello y'all. Welcome to the new Into the Chart Grooves feature, which is about- well, as you probably can tell- the charts (as well as sales stats, and the like).

Madonna info will be the primary discussion, but also other goings-on in the U.S. chart world. More comprehensive info will come a bit later, but, as usual, The Immaculate Collection album again was Madonna's top seller in the USA last week, scanning more than 3,600 units. Whether it holds in the catalog top 50 reamins to be seen, though it had re-entered last week at #46, when sales were 3,900+. Regardless, the album continues to sell at impressive rates.

And R&B/pop phenom of the moment Usher hangs onto the top of the Billboard 200 for a 5th consecutive week, as Confessions scans another 255,000+ units- that's a 5-week tally of 2.6 million+ albums- not all that shabby, eh?

More to come later, but I hope the chart fans out there enjoy this new feature. If there is any info or suggestions to pass along, please feel free to do so.
