Einstein image denied to Madonna.

A bid by Madonna to incorporate Einstein’s image in one of her concert performances failed. “It was just inappropriate,” Professor Hanoch Gutfreund, a former president of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, said without elaborating.

Anyone wanting to use a picture of Albert Einstein, the 20th century’s most celebrated scientist, must get permission from the Israeli university he helped found and which owns all the rights to his legacy.

“We have one objective — perpetuating Einstein’s memory with the proper dignity,” said Gutfreund.

[BREAKING NEWS] Madonna Heads List Of Year's Top Tours.

First published on a Madonna website

 Madonna's blockbuster Re-Invention tour took all of the drama out of the top tour of the year race, ringing up a year's best $125 million in total box office gross by the time it wrapped in September. According to Billboard Boxscore, Madonna sold out 55 of 56 performances worldwide, with an average nightly take of $2.23 million.

"My Re-Invention tour was by far the most creatively satisfying experience I have ever had," Madonna tells Billboard. "I was able to put everything I love into one entertaining event: film, music and dance." The tour was produced worldwide by Clear Channel Entertainment

Prince's Musicology tour, produced by AEG Live, drew nearly 1.5 million people, more than any other artist, and grossed $90.2 million, second only to Madonna.

Shania Twain was third, reporting grosses totaling $62.5 million and playing to nearly 950,000 fans. The rest of the top 10 includes Simon & Garfunkel ($59 million), Metallica ($53.8 million), Bette Midler ($53.3 million), Sting ($52.4 million), Kenny Chesney ($49.3 million), David Bowie ($46 million) and Toby Keith ($44.3 million).

This year, Celine Dion reported more than $77 million from her residency at the Colosseum at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas. If Dion's numbers reflected a "tour" per se, she would rank third among all acts. The Red Hot Chili Peppers, with James Brown and Chicks On Speed, headlined the top engagement at the year, three nights at London's Hyde Park that grossed $17,187,324 and drew more than 258,000 June 19, 20 and 25.

For the fifth consecutive year, Madison Square Garden in New York City tops the list of arenas, reporting music-event grosses totaling just under $73 million. For the second consecutive year, Tweeter Center in Mansfield, Mass., is the top amphitheater, reporting more than $23 million from 32 concerts.

-- Ray Waddell, Nashville -  BILLBOARD

What an incredible experience in Holland!

Yesterday, our friend Jorge (aka jor-g) from Spain sent to us some photos he took in Arnhem (Holland) for the re-Invention Tour.

We were too busy due to the Vogue backdrop video for the re-Invention Tour to post them... but finally we can show the pics, as well as Jorge story.

"We are from Spain, and decided to go to Holland to see the shows, a country we’d never been before and wanted to visit. We were 10 days there, and visited cities as Amsterdam, Rotterdam, La Haya.. and of course Arnhem! It was a very good idea. We found there quiet and beautiful people. We were front row, and could see the show and Madonna better than we could have ever imagined. We had Madonna so close that we went mad, and enjoyed the concert at its best.

Here I enclose some pictures we did:


The Gelredome in Arnhem.


An advertisement near the Gelredome. A huge one, there were several like this near the stadium.


Jorge in the streets of Amsterdam, beside another advertisement.


Jorge, David and Bruno waiting before the show. Only a few hours waiting, and any push. It was great!

 
Jorge front row. We got the best place to see the show!


Photos from 8th September in Arnhem, made by Bruno. These are not very good pictures (he used a very bad disposable camera, with no zoom). Security was very high in this show, and all cameras seen front line were requisitioned. So he had to hide his camera behind of me, and he couln’t use the flash either, so the pictures are dark.

 
A picture of the stage once the show finished.


Madonna made front page in every local newspaper the following day. Here David and Bruno with 2 of them.


Bruno in the streets of Amsterdam with Madonna’s front page newspaper.


10 - Jorge and Arjan again in Arnhem, beside another advertisement.


Elena, Jorge and Sara in our own spanish after party!!


We had a experience in Holland we’ll remember for the rest of our lives. Thanks to our friends there, Arjan and Gé!! You are so great. Also to Johan and his colleagues, and all the people we meet.

Can’t wait to see the show again in the official DVD release and documentary."
