[Gossip] More on DONATELLA VERSACE’s party.

The loos!


Closed shop ... Madonna not one to share

MADGE insisted no one else used the ladies’ toilet when she did.

The Queen Of Pop’s minders even stood outside to ensure guests didn’t wander in and accidentally overhear the royal wee.

One desperate guest forced to queue said: “What on Earth did they think I was going to do to her?”

Perhaps she wanted to make it a Kabba-loo...

What they ate and drank


Water hoot ... Madge prefers special Kabbalah brand

GUESTS had a real food frenzy, but MADONNA obviously didn’t partake – she once told me she never eats anything when she goes to restaurants.

And look at what she missed: oysters, tuna tartare, fillet steak with green peppercorn sauce, corn-fed roast chicken, veal Milanese, spaghetti Bolognese, Dover sole and cod.

Sweet treats included chocolate cake, apple tart, pavlova and ice cream.

To wash all that down the guests could choose from Cipriani’s famous Bellini cocktails, Veuve Cliquot champagne and fine Italian wines.

MADONNA had her army of rabbis in tow who had all dressed down for the evening. And they all clearly had a very good time –  they each handed the doormen very large tips at the end of the night. Who says Kabbalah's no fun?

Something's Coming Over The Night Before on August 17th.

We remind you for the third time to join this party/charity event "Something's Coming Over the Night Before". 

The Bloomsbury Theatre Education Programme is one of the most successful education programmes of its kind, having been accessed by over 130,000 young people since its launch four years’ ago. The aim of the Programme is to help under-represented groups such as young people with disabilities, mature students and those from ethnic minorities. Receiving no outside grants or subsidy, the Programme relies solely on generous sponsorship and charitable donations, which is why, on August 17, they are raising funds with a special Madonna Night entitled Something’s Coming Over The Night Before.

The two-hour long extravaganza will look back over Madonna’s distinguished career, showing highlights from her greatest live performances from her sex vixen days of Like A Virgin to her politically charged American Life, along with rare footage from her earlier live performances.

For all true fanatics there’ll also be a Madonna look alike competition with great pries from Warner Music whilst the theatre bar will be pumping out Madonna Remixed and Revisited tunes from her career which has spanned two decades, reworked with fabulous Madonna Cocktails especially concocted on the night.

The Material Girl will be the tribute performer on the night. The Material Girl is recreated by Alison, a professionally trained singer, dancer and choreographer, with performances at prestigious venues such as; ‘The Royal Festival Hall’, ‘The Royal Albert Hall’, ‘Privilege’ (Ibiza) and various ‘Civic Halls’.

Here are a few reactions from people who’ve already experienced The Material Girl...
"The Material Girl is incredibly professional, very authentic really sounds like her." Martin Kennedy Head of Full Time School, Academy of Contemporary Music.
