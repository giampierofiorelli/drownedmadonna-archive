[The First Look] Donatella talks to Vanity Fair Italy: 'On The Set: No Pray and No Lunch'.

Italian edition of Vanity Fair features an exclusive interview with Italian designer Donatella Versace, who talks about her new ad campaign and her long-time friend Madonna.

Take the first look.

"Madonna is the most professional artist in the world. The meeting was at 10 am and she was there at 10 o'clock"

"She brought the hairdresser Henry Lecompte from LA, which was also on Tour with her, and I chose the make-up expert. After 2 hours she was platinum and ready!"

"She's not a secretary in the photos. This is a CEO (Chief Executive Office) look as Madonna herself calls it.
 Madonna worked on defining it"

"There was no break for praying during the shoot. Kabbalah is a spiritual philosophy, it needs the right moment to practice it."

"There was no break for lunch..Madonna usually does an abundant breakfast and then straight to supper. Everyone on the set was starving and  fainting but she didn't care. You would not call her a docile person."

"The only stop came in the afternoon when Madonna's kids arrived on the set. She is incredible with them..Very sweet but also strict. She tried to explain to Lola she was going to represent a character in the photos, but Lola was only interested in wearing the dresses, she already acts like a little Diva and said to me - I will be your next model -"

"Rocco is a very sweet baby, he is deeply attached to her mother. Madonna’s children are friends of with mine. Since they have been living in London, I meet them more often"

"There was an amusing atmosphere on the set. Mario has got a strong personality too. Madonna and Mario joked with squabbles all the time - Not that way I look like a witch - she said and Mario - Shut up you , strike a pose - "

"We were looking for a futuristic place as location for this photoshoot...but this kind of office exists only in Los Angeles..that's why we recreated it at Mario Testino's West Way Studio."

"All I read about her use of our villa in Como and all the million dollars we would have given her is not true at all. I don’t want to say anything else but we made an agreement I like better to remain private. It also includes charity."

"We have known each other for thirteen years. She was a friend of my brother Gianni. We met in Miami where we both lived. We used to meet at the parties until one night we started speaking, we didn’t stop anymore…!"

"We talk about anything. Madonna has a superior intelligence, a great culture and a very interesting point of view about the world. But you can also talk about gossips with her..."

Vanity Fair Italy also published a photo of Madonna when she was a waitress in New York City.

Make a note of Madonna the secretary.

By Hilary Alexander - The Telegraph
(Filed: 08/01/2005)
Madonna has played many roles in her long and lucrative career: virgin, pop star, actress, music mogul, sex symbol, fashion icon, yoga expert, feudal landlord, model-mother and tweeds-and-wellies country wife.

But a secretary? Even in the most up-market and stylish office, you are unlikely ever to hear the instruction: "Take a letter, Miss Ciccone."

Secretarial chic, however, is the latest image that Madonna – worth a conservative £227 million at last count – has chosen to promote for the new Versace advertising campaign.

As might be expected, she is no ordinary secretary. The Versace clothes she wears in just three of the photographs add up to £7,000 – not counting the watch – which probably represents a few months' take-home pay for a secretary in a fairly senior position.

Madonna's fee, by all accounts, is a "sizeable chunk" of the £10.6 million cost of the campaign – which may ease her disappointment over the sluggish performance of her most recent album, American Life.

Donatella Versace said yesterday: "She's the best in her field and she relates to women of all ages. She's got extraordinary personal style and has been a friend of mine and the house for many years."

[Rumours] Only Four Pictures to Be Used for Versace Campaign?

PARIS, Jan 6 (AFP) - US rock star Madonna is to be the new face of an advertising campaign for the Italian fashion house Versace, a spokeswoman for the house said Thursday, confirming a report in the magazine Women's Wear Daily.

The 46-year-old singer is a long-standing friend of Donatella Versace, artistic director of the couture and luxury read-to-wear label.

The campaign for spring-summer 2005 will be shot by the fashion photographer Mario Testino and will centre on four pictures of Madonna, wearing day and evening wear, on the theme of women in the office.

"She's an icon," Donatella told WWD. "She is an innovator, a groundbreaker and the best in her field. She relates to women of all ages. She's got extraordinary personal style and has been a friend of mine and the house for many years."

Madonna is only the latest in a long list of celebrities pressed into service to promote perfumes or fashion brands, which includes Nicole Kidman, Charlize Theron, Catherine Zeta-Jones and, most recently, Uma Thurman.

Madonna and Donatella: the aura of cool also in a video?

by Jenny Beckam /MediaWeek

"If I cast my memory back just a few days, this aura of cool, calm and collected media types is blown well clear of the water, when word spread around the office that Madonna and Donatella Versace were filming a video just yards from us.

Not only were two of the world’s most famous women within our immediate vicinity, but Madonna was complete with both children and a fleet of black Range Rovers.

The drama was all too much and the cool, calm and collected among us soon dissolved into a swarming stampede, swirling down the stairs to the gated studio car park, which had become a celebrity utopia.

It was only at this point I observed another phenomenon – the instant return of the cool, the calm and the collected.

Faced with possible eye contact from one of today’s true showbiz icons, the stampede – like a well choreographed stage troupe – struck a pose, with body language emphasising how entirely natural it was for a group of 15 adults to be standing at the end of a dead-end road, in the dark on a freezing cold Friday.

True to form, one knowing glance from a member of the stars’ protection team saw the rabble drawing mobile phones from pockets quicker than John Wayne in his heyday.

No time to consider exactly why 15 individuals would, all of a sudden, choose to make outdoor phone calls a group activity.

Needless to say, time spent huddling in the cold afternoon air desperately trying to look discreet paid no dividends and neither Madonna nor Donatella satisfied our “cool curiosity” with an appearance.

There are, of course, circumstances when the “human swarm” simply won't do.

My industry comrades would find it both personally and practically impossible (and entirely unacceptable) to act in such a manner should yet another A-lister be spotted scoffing at The Ivy.

It’s times like these when the cool, calm and collected come into their own with a series of rapid gesturing eye movements, altering their dining partner to the exact location of the celebrity without so much as a word uttered.

So future dining partners take note – I’m not “making eyes at you”, I'm simply telling you that Madonna is sitting behind you!"

Jenny Beckman is head of marketing at LBC97.3FM and LBC1152AM
