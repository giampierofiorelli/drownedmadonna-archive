Denim secrets unzipped .

Dec 6:
THE raunchy television commercial promoting Gap jeans featured rock icon Madonna smiling sexily from beneath a tweed cap.

A pair of low-slung blue denims clung to her narrow hips, showing off a perfect body.

The camera captured exactly the sort of look that thousands of other women immediately wanted to copy.

The Madonna jeans campaign was a brilliant marketing success for the High Street store in the autumn of last year.

The fun and funky hipsters modelled by the 44-year-old singer flew off the shelves, boosting the already booming multi-million pound jeans market in Britain and America.

Yet a few months later, in the spring of this year, a very different image of Gap jeans was revealed to the world.

[BREAKING NEWS] Madonna to do LIVE AID II?

SUNDAY MIRROR:

I'M MADGE FOR IT

Nov 21 2004

Madonna joins Robbie and Quo in Live Aid II line-up 20 years after first concert

By Ben Todd, Showbiz Editor

MADONNA wants to repeat her Live Aid performance 20 years on.

The superstar, who was one of the main US attractions first time around at the gig that "changed the world", has backed plans for the new extravaganza.

Madonna - who introduced the Band Aid II video on TV on Thursday ahead of its November 29 release date - told the Sunday Mirror: "If I was asked I would absolutely do it again."

Talking of her performance in Philadelphia on July 13, 1985, Madonna said: "My memories were that it was a very heady experience. I had never played to that many people before."

Madonna, 46, is the latest celeb to line up for Live Aid II. Status Quo and Robbie Williams have already said they will play.

Meanwhile, Band Aid 20's version of Do They Know It's Christmas? has run into problems in the United States because the song can't get a record deal.

Sir Bob Geldof jetted to New York last week to try to persuade a record company to release the song there. But despite intense pressure from Sir Bob and Universal UK record firm boss Lucian Grange, a deal had not been signed by the end of the week. An insider said: "Sir Bob and Lucian have been doing their nut. But there's just not the same interest in the US.

"Some of the names on the record just don't register. People like Busted and Keane just aren't stars - even Robbie Williams is a virtual nobody here."

Madge fear for Lourdes.
Today's edition of The Sun:

Convert ... Madonna

From ANTONELLA LAZZERI in Los Angeles

RELIGIOUS convert Madonna admits she would not like her daughter Lourdes to follow in her raunchy footsteps.

The singer, 46, said in a TV interview she would not object to eight-year-old Lourdes making pop her career.

But Madonna, who has followed the Kabbalah faith for eight years, was pressed: “If Lourdes decided to prance around the stage in her underwear, singing and hollering, would you approve?

“If she behaved like you, as a mother who’s found a spiritual way of life, would you approve?”

The star, who also has a son Rocco, four, replied on CNN in America: “Probably not.”

She added that she would love US President George Bush to convert to Kabbalah, a mystical Jewish religion. Madonna said: “To get George Bush to study Kabbalah. Yes. It would be amazing.”

[BREAKING NEWS] Mirror: Madonna's speech for BAND AID!

FIRST PUBLISHED ON A MADONNA WEBSITE


Nov 15 2004
EXCLUSIVE
By Daniel Boffey, Caroline Hedley And Nicola Methven

MADONNA will spearhead the launch of the new Band Aid single Do They Know It’s Christmas, 20 years after the original rocked the world.

The Material Girl will introduce the new version of the 1984 charity hit when it is played on all five main TV channels at 6.05pm on Thursday.

Mother-of-two Madonna agreed to take part after Live Aid hero Sir Bob Geldof phoned her as she travelled to the UK Hall of Fame ceremony last week.

A source revealed: "Twenty years ago Geldof asked Bowie, because he was the coolest man on the planet, to introduce the video before its first showing on Top of The Pops. This time he wanted the reigning Queen of Pop and she agreed immediately."

Madonna will tell viewers: "Twenty years ago I performed at Live Aid.

"You saw me and my generation demanding a change.

"Once again, here we are 20 years later and more people die of hunger in Africa than war and Aids put together.

"In a world of plenty it is hard to imagine that there are African children going to bed tonight hungry.

"Bob Geldof and his friends are here to remind you that none of us can forget. Not ever. Feed the world. I am honoured to introduce Band Aid 20."

Sir Bob said: "Madonna was just a kid when she played at Live Aid 20 years ago. What I needed was somebody with resonance who goes beyond the normal pop world and reaches everyone. When people buy this record they are making a political demand.

"Madonna understands that."

Super-rich kids on £50k.

From The Sun:

By MICHAEL LEA

SUPER-RICH kids are getting £50,000 a year or more lavished on them by doting parents, a survey revealed yesterday.

The pampered group numbers a staggering 18,000 youngsters in Britain.

Another 180,000 cost mums and dads £20,000 or more a year.

Those in the elite brat pack have been dubbed Mass Affluent Kids by internet bank Egg, which carried out the research.

The vast sums go on holidays, hobbies, school trips and the latest fashion clothes. But the figures do NOT include school fees.

Famous jet-set kids include Madonna and Guy Ritchie’s children Lourdes and Rocco.

Parents who splash out £50,000 a year would spend £850,000 before the child was 18.



The study also found that 600,000 off-spring still get financial help beyond the age of 30 — and 150,000 into their 40s.

Average parents spend £2,916 a year on a girl, excluding education. Boys are cheaper at £2,790.

Egg’s Andy Deller said: “Raising a child is a significant financial commitment — typically more than 14 per cent of income. With private education it’s 55 per cent.”
