Merchandising
More on ''The English Roses'' merchandising.

On 08/08/2004 we revealed that The English Roses merchandising program will feature:

1. Dolls from The Alexander Doll Company

2. Tea from Harney and Sons

3. Juvenile girls' apparel from Lipstik

Now we can reveal more details.  The licensing program will also feature hot chocolate (along with tea), pajamas, slippers, calendars, jewelry boxes, charm bracelets and necklaces, diaries, school supplies,  journals and so on.

[BREAKING NEWS] Introducing the Madonna Nesting Doll.



Madonna Nesting Doll
Price: $35.00

Dear Madonna Fan,

Introducing the most unique Madonna collectible yet! This authentic wooden Russian Nesting Doll is a 4-in-1 treat, featuring a different phase of Madonna's career on each smaller doll. Each doll fits within each other to make one whole unit. Each doll is also handmade and hand painted. Only 5,000 of these are being made in total, so don't miss your chance to get one while they are still available.

Please note that these are currently being made by hand and we expect them to be ready to ship by early October. We will not charge any cards until we know the exact ship date; we will then send an email to notify you of the charge and the ship date.
