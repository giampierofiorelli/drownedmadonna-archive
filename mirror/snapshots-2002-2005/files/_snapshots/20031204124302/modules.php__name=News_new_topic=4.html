Madonna interviewed by ''Extra''.
"Extra" interviewed Madonna after her book-reading. Madonna confessed that her daughter Lourdes serves as her secret editor and stylist. Madonna says, "She didn’t like my hair dark. She was like, 'Mom, ew.' Actually, my children rule my life."
Then, Madonna reveals that she and Britney Spears have become fast friends. She says, "I found her to be very vulnerable and very open and searching. She started asking me for advice." And Spears says she may move to London and live closer to her role model. Madonna says, "She is definitely looking for a place there. Maybe we'll be neighbors there too."
Madonna talked about her first job. "I was a jelly squirter. I got fired. I was also the coat check girl at the Russian Tea Room. I got fired for wearing fishnets. I’ve been a housekeeper, a house cleaner, and I was never really good as a waitress."
And Madonna also revealed her secret addiction. She says, "I started to become obsessed with Krispy Kreme. I got a little carried away with those, and there had to be an intervention. I used to leave my yoga class, which is total hypocrisy, and go the Krispy Kreme and get two hot, fresh, glazed donuts and have a sugar rush, and then crash and be depressed the rest of the day."

Greek TV station fined for two men kissing, but Spears/Madonna smooch is OK.
A private Greek TV station has been fined 100,000 Euros ($151,000 Cdn) for broadcasting a scene of two men kissing, reports said Wednesday.
The action brought strong criticism from media commentators. Mega TV was fined by the independent licensing body, the National Radio and Television Council, for the Oct. 6 scene showing two male actors kissing in the weekly drama "Close Your Eyes".
The council - usually tolerant of the frequent nudity and sexual content of Greek television - described the scene as "vulgar and unacceptable."
TV critic Popi Diamandakou called the decision "hypocritical."
"The council tells us... that it's OK to be tolerant but we shouldn't go too far," she wrote in the daily Ta Nea newspaper.
Greek TV stations repeatedly showed clips of Britney Spears kissing Madonna during the MTV Video Music Awards in August without facing penalties.

What if Madonna had kissed Pink? It almost happened.
It was a moment that captivated the nation, but what would it have been like if Madonna had picked different kissmates for her VMA performance? As it turns out, Pink was supposed to be there too.

"It was originally supposed to be different people involved," Pink told MTV News Europe, "but I didn't even know [what was going to happen during the performance]. The kiss wasn't even talked about."

Rumors had been rampant before the Video Music Awards that Madonna would be taking the stage with representatives of the younger pop generation, but talk had centered mostly on Britney Spears and Jennifer Lopez. Pink escaped the rumor mill because she backed out before rehearsals began.

"I had one vacation all year," Pink explained, "and I was not leaving my vacation to go into a rehearsal studio, which sounds ridiculous, but you know, I just said, 'I can't be at those rehearsals, but I can learn it for that day, and I'm sure that I'll be more than capable of doing it.' "

That proposal, however, wasn't acceptable, and Pink was taken out of the running. But since she was only apprised of the singing aspect of the performance, not the kissing, it begs the question: Had scheduling not been a problem, would she have been willing to make out with Madonna? She says yes and insists she's amused that the same-sex liplock generated so many headlines.

"I'm very surprised that kisses, or girl-on-girl, or guy-on-guy, is that interesting still, to this day," she laughed.

Advertising campaigns and 'Rolling Stone' cover.
Robster, one of the moderators of Madonna-Mad Forum, stated that "Love Profusion" is going to be in the background of the next Estée Lauder's advertising campaign, which will run for a whole year. The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. is one of the world’s leading manufacturers and marketers of quality skin care, makeup, fragrance and hair care products. It was founded in 1946 in New York City.

Then, he said that "Rolling Stone" cover with Madonna is pushed back to October. Initially, it should be published in August.

Finally, Robster confirmed that GAP advertising campaign, featuring Madonna and Missy Elliott, will hit TV stations on August the first. This commercial will feature "Hollywood" and Missy Elliott's remix of "Into The Groove". This is not the first time that GAP uses a Madonna song.
