Warner aims to change the mix.

EDGAR BRONFMAN, head of Warner Music, has begun shopping for music management companies as he prepares the business for a possible flotation.
Sources close to Bronfman say he wants to expand revenues beyond music sales and add more publishing, merchandising and music management to the business mix.

Bronfman bought Warner Music from Time Warner last November for $2.6 billion, beating Britain’s EMI. Warner’s artists include Madonna, Led Zeppelin and REM.

Bronfman, heir to the Seagram whisky fortune, was backed in the bid by venture- capital companies including Thomas H Lee Partners and Bain Capital. The group has been linked to a possible merger with EMI but a flotation is now believed to be its preferred first option.

Music-industry executives have named Britain’s Sanctuary as a possible target but sources close to both sides say no talks have taken place. Another potential target is Hollywood talent agency The Firm.

Thomas H Lee and Bain recently took a stake in The Firm whose clients include Limp Bizkit, the Dixie Chicks and Robert De Niro.

Since the takeover Bronfman has cut $250m in costs and axed over 1,000 jobs. Warner’s labels have been consolidated and 25% of acts dropped. Analysts reckon the group could now be worth in excess of $3 billion.

Source: The Times.

News of today in Side-Line Magazine.

Thu Nov 18, 2004

Depeche Mode songwriter pens tracks for new Madonna album
 


Early September Side-Line was informed of a meeting between the Depeche Mode songwriter Martin Gore, Madonna and Stuart Price during Madonna's Re-invention tour. At that meeting Gore was given 2 songs to work on. At the time we had no extra details so decided to give it a rest until we were informed a few days ago by our dear friends at www.DrownedMadonna.com that Madonna had just joined up again with Martin Gore in London. Apparently she was impressed by one of the tracks. Madonna is now going to rewrite some of the lyrics and she will also record some raw vocals for Martin. Officially the info has been dissed as not true but this morning it was confirmed by someone from the Depeche Mode innercircle who stressed "it is not sure whether the tracks Martin wrote will be used, but it looks like Madonna is under the impression of at least one of the submitted tracks". Still according to this source unless the recording is really used, there will be no official announcement made. Recently Gore has been co-writing music with Claudia Brucken for her OneTwo project and with No Doubt's Gwen Stefani for her debut solo album. Only the track written together with Brucken, "Cloud 9", has been released so far. (You can read an extensive interview with Claudia Brucken on this matter in Side-Line 49, which you can order below.) Madonna is currently also solliciting material from Lamb's Andy Barlow (apparently a track with a trip-hop feeling to it) and Goldfrapp. Also William Orbit is back in the picture for a re-work of an old song holding the working title "Liquid Love". According to a source close to the singer, Madonna has opted for this collaboration approach after her 2003 album "American Life" didn't exactly receive rave reviews. In related news, Michael Colombier, the composer who composed the string arrangements for Madonna on "Die Another Day", "Don't Tell Me" and "Easy Ride" as well as composing the soundtrack for "Swept Away" has died. More news as we get it.

Caresse Henry seeking new clients.

Here's another article about Caresse Henry's future:

LOS ANGELES (Billboard) - Following her parting with longtime management client Madonna, Caresse Henry says she is still in discussions with Sanctuary Group to join the diversified music company, and is seeking other artists.
"Management is what I do best," she told Billboard, "and hope in a short time I will be fortunate to find other brilliant people to work with and endeavor to make the music business more profitable and interesting, culminating in success for all."

She declined to comment on Madonna other than "we had a remarkable span" together.

Henry, Madonna’s former assistant, began managing the artist after Madonna split with Freddy DeMann in 1997. Henry co-managed Madonna with Q-Prime from 1997 through 1999, and then assumed full-time managerial duties. She has also briefly managed Paulina Rubio and co-managed Jessica Simpson.

London-based Sanctuary’s management roster includes Destiny’s Child, Mary J. Blige, Nelly, Slipknot and the Von Bondies.

Music
Laura Pausini inteviewed by Mexican EXA FM.

Laura Pausini is currently on tour in Latin America promoting her upcoming album "Resta In Ascolto". After being in Puerto Rico, she has visited Mexico and she has been interviewed by Mexican EXA FM. Here's an interesting excerpt regarding Madonna and the song - "Mi Abbandono A Te" / "Mi Abandono A Ti" (originally titled "Like A Flower") - she gave her:

Madonna wrote the last track of my disk that is called "Mi Abbandono A Te" /"Mi Abandono A Ti". It's a song that I received about a year and a half ago. She gave it to me in English, but she wanted it to be sung by an interpreter that could sing it in another language... and I believe that she chose me because, aside from being able to sing it in Spanish, I can sing it in Italian, which is a language that she loves so much because of her Italian origins. 

Working with her was very interesting, and even if I enterely worked on the song with the producer of The Power Of Good-Bye and some other songs from her Ray Of Light album, it has been completely recorded and arranged in London while she was on tour. However, she wanted to know every single thing. Madonna took care of what I wrote, because, translating it, I changed the whole meaning of the song.

It's not about the love-affair between a man and a woman, but it's about the inner peace of the people, just like if we are sat one in front of the other. It's a very spiritual song, and, compared to the first demo she sent me, it's quite different.

I like to work with someone this way, because only the biggest artists like her can respect contributions... leave you free... a little bit of her and a little of me... I think this is the true way to work... when two persons are united and at the same time very different musically, it's interesting to listen to their meeting... which is not necessarily one the adaptation of the other.

If I don't identify myself, I don't sing a song... even if it's written by a very important artist. I don't accept songs written by important people that don't let you change anything... don't let me feel myself identifing with the song.

Is Elton John Jealous?
By ABC Network

(New York-WABC, October 5, 2004) — Elton John is letting his fangs show, in what music lovers say is a wicked case of professional jealousy.

The aging singer songwriter stunned a crowd at the "Q" music awards in London.

That's where, out of nowhere, he launched into an attack on fellow nominee Madonna. John says Madonna "ought to be shot" for lip-synching in concert.

Madonna fans say, the bizarre rants by the singer of "Crocodile Rock" are a crock and that "he" is the one whose voice gives out in concert.
