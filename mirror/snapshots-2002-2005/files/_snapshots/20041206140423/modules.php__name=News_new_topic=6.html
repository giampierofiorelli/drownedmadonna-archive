MADONNA - the rolemodel of the modern woman.

Our German correspondent Martin (aka singysingysingy) sent us the scans of a new article from Germany’s magazine "Maxi" on today's female rolemodels. Of course Madonna is one of them. Below we show you the trhanslation of the article and the scans.

MADONNA - the rolemodel of the modern woman

MADONNA’s way of life sounds perfect: Children, husband, career. But this works not completely in real life. No matter which haircolour, which role she’s playing-the bitch, the enlightened-MADONNA is always credible, always on the winning side, started from dildos to Kabbalah she’s always ahead of the rest! What’s the reason for her success? It’s her obstinacy, her calculated provocation and her authenticity. MADONNA wasn’t even beautiful when she was young-thick, black eyebrows and too thick legs. But she had one aim: Fame! Over the years MADONNA was fighting the way for the modern women of today, for extravaganzas of all kind: From provocating to boredom everything is allowed.

Elton apologises to Madonna again.

Tuesday, November 9 2004, 14:19 GMT -- by Daniel Saney


Elton John has turned down the opportunity to present Madonna with her UK Hall of Fame Award due to his commitment to appearing on Friday Night With Jonathan Ross which films at the same time.

Elton recently gave a qualified apology to Madge following his rant about her (and others') lipsynching when performing live, when he was presenting the Best Live Act award at the Q Awards. He has since claimed that she is talented.

A spokesperson for Madonna denied reports that she sent Elton a big box of chocolates with a note reading "Obviously you have too much time on your hands," after his negative criticism.

"If Madonna were to respond to Elton, you know it would be way way more clever than candy and that pathetic line," he said.
