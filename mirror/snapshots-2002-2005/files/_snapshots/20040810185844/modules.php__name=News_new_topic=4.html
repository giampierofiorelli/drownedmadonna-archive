Rumours: RECORD NUMBER OF DIGNITARIES IN THE ATHENS OLYMPICS
Athens, 30 July 2004


A record number of foreign dignitaries are expected to watch the Athens Olympic Games given the fact that the number of applications by personalities from around the world is unprecedented.

According to the Athens newspaper “Apogevmatini”, some of the foreign politicians who will come to Athens to watch the Games will be former US President George Bush, Russian President Vladimir Putin, French President Jacques Chirac, Romanian President Ion Iliescu, British Prime Minister Tony Blair, Turkish Prime Minister Recep Tayyip Erdogan, the French Finance Minister, new European Commission President Jose Manuel Durao Baroso and former FRG Foreign Minister Mr. Gensher

Also, present will be former king of Greece Konstantinos, who is a member of the International Olympic Committee, with all his family, Prince Charles and Belgian crown prince Philip with Princess Matilde.

The games will attend the Mayors of New York, Rome, Paris, London and Istanbul invited by Athens Mayor Dora Bakoyianni.

Among the Hollywood stars to watch the games will be Brad Pitt, Jennifer Aniston, Sylvester Stalone, Tom Hanks and film director Steven Spielberg. World famous singer Madonna will be in Greece as the guest of one of the Olympic Games sponsors

Madonna talks to contestants of new reality show
By Hal Bodeker | Sentinel Television Critic
Posted July 23, 2004 

LOS ANGELES -- Hip-hop star Missy Elliott says she's a fan of American Idol, but her UPN reality series will be quite different. After all, Madonna stops by to talk to the contestants.
"You get a chance to see what it takes to be an artist and not just someone getting onstage and performing," Elliott says. "Every singer can't get onstage and keep a crowd's attention. . . . You have to have drive, you have to have personality, all of that. You get a chance to see all of those wrapped in one."

Elliott, 33, serves as co-executive producer and judge on The Road to Stardom With Missy Elliott. UPN plans the talent show for midseason, although it may turn up sooner.

In the show, 13 performers live together on a tour bus and travel cross-country while performing for a deal with Elliott's record label. Elliott and her judges drop a contestant each week.

Madonna speaks to the aspiring performers as a favor to Elliott. "We are friends, and that's what we owe to each other," Elliott says. "We both did the Gap commercial, so we have a friendship there."
Elliott watched thousands of audition tapes for the show. "Some people, I think, have lost their mind," she says.

She had to tell other hopefuls that they were auditioning for the wrong show. "Some people gave me very bad migraines," she says. "Some people really think that they have what it takes. They must not have any friends."

Elliott says she has never dropped Misdemeanor as her middle name. But UPN doesn't have it in the show title or publicity materials.

"I think the press might have dropped it," she says. "That name is like a tattoo. It's with me forever."

BREAKING NEWS: MADONNA TO VISIT HERTS SHOW?
Wednesday, April 28, 2004: HARDMAN Vinnie Jones plans to bring fellow-star Madonna to this year's Herts County Show.

Footballer turned Hollywood star Vinnie couldn't resist a little name-dropping when he turned out yesterday in his new respectable role as Hertfordshire Agricultural Society's new deputy president.
Vinnie will be taking on a variety of jobs including making high-profile appearances at the two-day County Show on May 29 and 30.
And he even hinted that he may bring some wellknown faces with him.
"I might bring Madonna and Guy they like their country sports. I might just shock everyone by bringing them along," he quipped.
Vinnie's Jones appointment has been met with mixed reactions from the public who have accused him of trying to renew his image.
Secretary of the Agricultural Society, Mike Harman, said that it made sense to have Vinnie on board because he raises the profile and is a Hertfordshire man.
"He's Hertfordshire through and through. It's our county show and it's his county and we have a mutual interest in how we view country life," he said.
The Jones family have been regular visitors to the in the past because he thinks it is a great day out for the kids.
