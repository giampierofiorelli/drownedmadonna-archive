Elton apologises to Madonna again.

Tuesday, November 9 2004, 14:19 GMT -- by Daniel Saney


Elton John has turned down the opportunity to present Madonna with her UK Hall of Fame Award due to his commitment to appearing on Friday Night With Jonathan Ross which films at the same time.

Elton recently gave a qualified apology to Madge following his rant about her (and others') lipsynching when performing live, when he was presenting the Best Live Act award at the Q Awards. He has since claimed that she is talented.

A spokesperson for Madonna denied reports that she sent Elton a big box of chocolates with a note reading "Obviously you have too much time on your hands," after his negative criticism.

"If Madonna were to respond to Elton, you know it would be way way more clever than candy and that pathetic line," he said.

Magazines
German Max on S.Klein/MADONNA exhibition in Berlin.

German "in-magazine" Max is reporting on the upcoming Steven Klein/MADONNA exhibition in Berlin.

What do Beckham, MADONNA, Schiffer and Brad Pitt have in common?

They adore one man- Steven Klein!

The 38 year old is at the moment the most wanted photographer of the world. Now his work is coming to Berlin: The gallery
Camera Work is showing a performance project, dedicated exclusively to the pop Icon MADONNA. Originally Klein was an educated classic painter, but since a long time he's earning his money by shooting photos. Thanks to this we got erotic-provocant pics of stars like MADONNA, Pitt or Beckham. From 6th November his work is shown for the 1st time in Berlin. In cooperation  with MADONNA he created a performance project, which connects performances, videos and animation: X-STaTIC PRO=CeSS".

To be honest - if MADONNA would call you and ask you to do a photo shoot, you'd be feeling honored. But Klein's reaction is reserved, when being asked, why MADONNA choose him to do the promo-shooting for her  "American life" album , he tells:"I don't know. I guess MADONNA knows  the answer more than I do." He hates to speak about his work as well as he hates to get photographed himself. Instead he's pushing limits with sensational campaigns. So he did for the Apri issue of "W" with MADONNA on the cover, where he's presenting breathtaking pics of MADONNA and which is the most sold issue of "W".
"We didn't want to show her new haircolour or create a new fashion trend", Klein says.

The exhibition and "making of "video were a huge success at NY Deitch Projects Galery. Now he landed a new coup: He was said to shoot a video for MADONNA's  "Nothing fails".
Asked about his work with MADONNA he says: "It isn't true that stars always want to look pretty and beautiful. MADONNA didn't have any problems not to look like a model in my pics. She only didn't like 4 of them and didn't want to release them. That's ok for me. It was something special to work with her. Althogh she's called a control freak - the reality is that she wants to bring herself into the creative process and this is brilliant. She is full of incredible energy and intuition. To work out the concept for the shoot took months. We didn't want to do a trendy fashion shoot, but something more deep! We wanted to show the different world in her, beyond the surface of the glamour MADONNA! For me MADONNA was always more a performance artist than a popstar. The shooting took 10 hours in a studio in LA. "

Special thanks to our german correspondent Martin.
