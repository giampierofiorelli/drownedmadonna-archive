Madonna - Berlusconi controversy is making the headlines around the world

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

And here we go. After arousing controversies in Italy, provoking a 'rubbish' reaction of Silvio Berlusconi's Party, Popolo delle Libertà (PDL), that we reported previously, Madonna's comment on Italian Prime Minister is now making the headlines around the world.

 

NME and The Telegraph are some of the international papers reporting about the controversy.

 

PDL's reaction was predictable, since they are doing their best to hide international opinions on Silvio Berlusconi and every Italian person, who has a different opinion, is pointed as a Comminist. But, like Madonna and the Economist, most of Italians are actually thiking that Berlusconi is unsuited to govern the country.

 

Just have a look at a report broadcast by TG3, the TV News of the third Italian National Channel, RAI3.
