Gossip: Reportedly Lourdes judged the London's dance auditions and will be one of the dancers on Madonna's Next Tour

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

We at DrownedMadonna.com were the first to break the news of dance auditions in London's Pineapple Dance Studios for Madonna's next tour, as part of Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project.

Besides uploading their one-minute video online, applicants had the chance to audition in New York and London. New York's dance audition took place with choreographers Tony and Rich Talauega as judges on September 8, while London casting call took place last Friday and no judges were announced.

But according to a gossip published on today's Daily Star, hordes of dancers in London were surprised to find out that Madonna's daugher, Lourdes, was there to judge them. According to the British tabloid, Lola has been a pupil at the Pineapple Studios for years and she has perfected a mix of styles now.

 

Reportedly, Lourdes will join the team of dancers on Madonna's next tour and promo-tour. "Lourdes does routines every day in her mum’s studios as well as taking classes," a source told the Star. "She can now do jazz, hip-hop, popping, contemporary and even tap. That's why she is qualified to judge who would be the best back-up dancers for her mum."

 

Madonna is confirmed by Live Nation for the final dance competition on November 12 in New York City, when one dancer will be chosen for her next tour, in collaboration with Smirnoff as part of a multi-deal.

William Orbit: 'Expect the unexpected!' tweeting about his last days in the recording studio with Madonna and Jean Baptiste Kuoame

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

William Orbit is really in a very good mood these days and he is very open to tweet comments about his last days in the USA and the recording studio with Madonna and producer Jean Baptiste Kuoame. Orbit will be back in London in October.

"Couple more days in the studio with the awesome Jean Baptiste Kuoame. Expect the unexpected!" William Orbit tweeted less than two days ago.

 

And a few hours ago he tweeted some more: "Another ZEUS day in the studio. Done recording."

 

William Orbit is very happy with what he is being recording with Madonna for her upcoming album, due next Spring.

 

Let us further remark that Orbit tweeted about St. Vincent, calling the singer his "favourite guitarist right now", and he invited to watch the Marrow video, paying attention to the sound from 2:11. Could it be a sigh of what Orbit is into right now and some kind of what he is creating with Madonna? We would not be disappointed... it's absolutely amazing. We agree with William!

Madonna learning French for her lover Brahim Zaibat?

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

It is time to post some succulent gossip. As usual, take it with a grain of salt.

 

According to the Mirror, Madonna has hired a tutor to teach her French, the so-called language of love, besides being the first language of her partner, dancer Brahim Zaibat.

"Madonna is working on her album, but she is squeezing in French lessons too," a source is alleged to have told to the Mirror. "Her boyfriend is impressed, he thinks it's sweet."

 

"She's looking at properties in France," the source allegedly added.

 

We remind you that Madonna lived in Paris for a few time before she became a super-star, while working with Patrick Hernandez, and she has always been fascinated by French and French artists. In her latest documentary, I'm Going to Tell You a Secret, Madonna was filmed asking her daughter Lourdes to help her to learn some French lines for her show in Paris. Lola attended a French school in London and she is an astonishingly good French speaker.
