Madonna's next tour to stop in Milan, Italy, on June 14, 2012

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

According to always accurate and reliable Italian newspaper La Repubblica, there is already the Italian date for Madonna's next tour.

 

The daily paper reports that Madonna will perform in Milan on June 14, 2012, after 3 years from her Sticky & Sweet Tour. Reportedly, the concert is taking place at Milan's San Siro stadium. The stadium has been already booked.

Madonna's W.E. to be released in UK cinemas on January 12, 2012

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

The UK release date for Madonna's second directoral film W.E. has been finally announced today.

W.E. will open in UK cinemas on January 12, 2012. As we earlier reported, the movie will be distributed in the Great Britain by StudioCanal.

 

Don't forget that W.E. will get its UK premiere at BFI London Film Festival on October 23. The gala screening will take place at London Empire Leicester Square. BFI told DrownedMadonna.com that Madonna is expected to attend the premiere, but "guests lists for London Film Festival events are not 100% confirmed until the day of the event and are even at that point subject the change."

Win tickets to New York for Madonna Smirnoff Final Dance Audition on November 12

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

Smirnoff gives you the chance to win an all-expense paid trip to New York to party with Madonna and her choreographers Rich and Tone Talauega on November 12, when Madonna in collaboration with the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchance project will select a dancer for her next world tour. Just visit your country Smirnoff Facebook page and sign up. The more people you can get to sign up, the better your chances of winning tickets to attend the event and see Madonna. The competition ends on November 7.

Madonna's W.E. is a 'visual and stylistic masterpiece'. A revival of classic heritage brands as Vionnet and Mainbocher

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli

Madonna's W.E. "most definitely has the fashion world clamoring and bracing itself for a revival of classic and elegant heritage luxury brands such as Vionnet and Mainbocher," Huffinghton Post Style columnist Malcolm Harris writes.

"Madonna's film is a visual and stylistic masterpiece," a dear friend of Malcolm Harris and fashion editor said, after having the chance to see the world premiere of W.E. in Venice. "She described in great detail every pin-tuck, corset, pencil skirt and shade of Wallis blue she witnessed dance across the screen," Harris writes. "The movie will definitely herald a turning point in the direction of fashion and it's relationship with film," he adds.

 

"With all the renewed interest in these classic heritage brands and the successful relaunch of Vionnet (currently sold in over 200 stores worldwide), it is no wonder the fashion rumor mill is churning about the revitalizing of the ultimate style sleeping beauty -- Mainbocher," Harris writes. He hopes Madonna will fund or even co-design the collection.

 

Finally, Harris adds that "thanks to the Material Girl there is one thing we know for sure -- the world is about to get a lot more elegant, sophisticated and yes -- very, very romantic."
