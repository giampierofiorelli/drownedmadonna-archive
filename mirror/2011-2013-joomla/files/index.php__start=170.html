Madonna MDNA review by BoyCulture

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Our friend Matthew at BoyCulture has been invited to a Madonna's MDNA listening party at Liz Rosenberg's office. His favourite track so far from the first listening is Falling Free, an amazing and intimate ballad - written with Joe Henry and produced with William Orbit - which close the standard edition.

 

"I genuinely loved most of what I heard," Matt writes. "There are definitely songs on here that are personal, uplifting and inspiring mixed among the far louder "I'm still hot and young, fuck me in any hole you can!" club crushers."

 

Matt revealed that Lourdes is singing back vocals in Superstar, described as "A charming and fluffy song ("ooooh, la la!") that felt for me like Little Star had grown up and was having fun."

 

Turn Up the Radio is one of Matthew's favourite post-Ray of Light songs, described as "a sweeping, joyous pop-dance song as anthemic as Get Together."

 

"The suite of songs Love Spent, Masterpiece and Falling Free is probably going to please any Madonna fan I've ever met," writes Matt. "They're beautifully sung and are not cursed with oversimplified lyrics, nor do they sound like they care much if asinine radio programmers will like them."

 

Matthew can not understand why Beautiful Killer is not included in the standard edition of MDNA. "It is a f*cking great Orbit-sounding Solveig song," he says.Talking about the other bonus tracks, Matt adds: "Even the cringe-inducing title I Fucked Up turns out to be a really pretty, plaintive pop song. B-day Song didn't seem to have its guest star—M.I.A. was M.I.A."

 

There are 5 MDNA configurations: STANDARD EXPLICIT ALBUM/ALL ACCOUNTS/PHYSICAL & DIGITAL (with Gang Bang), STANDARD EDITED ALBUM/ALL ACCOUNTS/PHYSICAL & DIGITAL (without Gang Bang), DELUXE EXPLICIT ALBUM/ALL ACCOUNTS/PHYSICAL & DIGITAL (with Bang Bang), DELUXE EDITED ALBUM/WALMART EXCLUSIVE/PHYSICAL EDITION (without Bang Bang) and SUPER BOWL DELUXE EXPLICIT/ITUNES EXCLUSIVE/DIGITAL VERSION (including an ACOUSTIC version of Love Spent).

Madonna Gang Bang review

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


EXCLUSIVE - A few months ago we exclusively revealed the lyrics of Madonna's Bang Bang demo. That song has completely changed and brought to life Gang Bang, co-written with Mika and co-produced with the Demolition Crew (Demacio Demo Castellon, Mike Anthony, Square Boogie and Jax) and William Orbit. We can now reveal juicy details on Gang Bang.

 

Both Bang Bang and the final version now titled Gang Bang are very different from Give Me All Your Luvin' and Girl Gone Wild. Bang Bang was a mid-tempo song featuring a sample synth piano, which is now disappeared on Gang Bang. If the demo was someway similar to some Mika songs, without the typical happiness, now Gang Bang is very theatrical and electronic. Now the lyrics are presented in a new manner in Gang Bang. Part of them are completely new and the chorus has changed. But, if some of the lyrics are pretty the same, the whole thing is very different and absolutely more dramatic and violent. The song is now very dark, also thanks to the new beat. One of the biggest differences, is that Madonna is now mostly speaking gently over the track in a Kill Bill like fashion. "Bang Bang, shot you dead, my lover in the head. Bang Bang, shot you dead and I never regret ," Madonna says in the chorus.

 

As the song progressives, so does Madonna. Her voice gets much more aggressive, and it's clear she is out to take revenge on her past lover. "I'm going straight to hell, and I've got a lot of friends there," Madonna says. "If I see that bitch in hell, I'm gonna shoot him in the head again, 'cause I wanna see him die. Over and Over and Over and Over and Over and Over and Over and Over."
The end is terrific, and it may cause controversies, especially because it is a woman to express this way. Someway, it may be compared to the video of What It Feels Like For A Girl . "I said drive bitch. And while you're at it, die bitch. Now if you're gonna act like a bitch, then you're gonna die like a bitch."

 

The production is very good. Gang Bang is very different from everything Madonna ever done before. Be ready to be completely blown away!!

Madonna MDNA: Mika, Joe Henry and Klas Ahlund collaborated on her new album

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Universal Music Italy revealed some more interesting news-bits about Madonna's MDNA album to be released on March 26.

 

Mika co-wrote one of the tracks on MDNA. A brand new song has been co-written with Madonna's brother-in-law Joe Henry (who co-wrote Don't Tell, Jump and Devil Wouldn't Recognize You). Madonna's new album credits also include Klas Åhlund, who apparently co-wrote a track titled Girls on Film. But probably Åhlund is mentioned for his re-work of Duran Duran's Girls on Film.

 

Universal Music Italy also revealed that Nicky Minaj is featured in I don't Give A.
