Madonna's W.E. press conference at TIFF Toronto Film Festival 2011

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


We at DrownedMadonna.com are finally glad to reply to the abundant emails we are receiving.

We have been given official confirmation that there will be a press conference for W.E. at this year's TIFF Toronto Film Festival. Director Madonna, and actresses Abbie Cornish and Andrea Riseborough will reply to press questions on September 12 at 12:00 PM. Richard Crouse will be the moderator.

We remind you that the same day there will be the gala screening as well, with Madonna, Abbie Cornish and Andrea Riseborough on the red carpet.

 

The press conference will take place in Malaparte on the 6th floor of TIFF Bell Lightbox. Admission will be granted to accredited media on a first-come, first-served basis. Photo calls will occur 10 minutes prior to the start of press conferences; weather and schedule permitting.


The schedule is still subject to change.

Dance Auditions for Madonna's Next Tour in collaboration with Smirnoff

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Last August Madonna joined Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project to find out a new dancer for her next tour, which will start on 2012.

 

The first event will take place on September 8 2011 in New York City. Madonna's long-time choreographers Rich & Tone Talauega will judge the auditioners. The selected dancer will attend the final Smirnoff Nighlife Exchange Project in front of Madonna which will take place in on November 12 2011 in New York as well. The final winner will dance on tour with Madonna in 2012.

 

Here are the details for September 8 audition, as you can also see on the flyer below:

 

  • Location: Broadway Dance Center 322 West 45th Street New York City
  • Doors open at 7am
  • Auditions from 8am to 1pm
  • Auditions only for resident in New York and Connecticut
  • Auditioners must be 21 or over
  • Bring proper identification showing current address
  • Auditioners must come dressed to dance
  • Auditioners must NOT wear clothing with visible logos/brands, since all auditions may be filmed for use in future promotional content

 

 

 

New York event is the only one confirmed at this point, but there could be others.

 

Don't forget you can also apply online. Submit a 60-second dance video until October 10. For details on the dance submission process and terms and conditions, visit www.smirnoff.com.

 

 

The ten Finalists will be contacted via email by 17 October 2011 and must be eligible and able to travel to New York within the period of 7 to 12 November 2011 to take part to the final Smirnoff Nightlife Exchage Project event.

 

Click here and read all details of Madonna's next world tour available at this point.

 

 

"What attracted me to the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project is the combination of participating in the celebration of nightlife around the world and the opportunity to discover the world's best unknown dancers," said Madonna. "The best dancers are always in the clubs looking for the most original experience, just like Smirnoff is doing."

 

 

The 50 countries participating in the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project include: Argentina, Australia, Belgium, Bolivia, Brazil, Bulgaria, Cameroon, Canada, Chile, China, Colombia, Costa Rica, Cyprus, Dominican Republic, Dubai, Ecuador, El Salvador, Germany, Ghana, Great Britain, Greece, Guatemala, India, Indonesia, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Jamaica, Japan, Kenya, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Nigeria, Norway, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Poland, Romania, Serbia, South Africa, South Korea, Thailand, Trinidad, Turkey, Uruguay, United States, Venezuela and Vietnam.

 

Keep on checking this page for updates.

'Madonna is entitled to like any flower she wants,' says her Spokesperson

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Ok, she is Madonna, but it's incredible that she is able to arouse such viral controversy about a flower she doesn't like.

The episode was unintentionally funny, and in fact we gave prominence to it.

Anyway some media - this is not the case of CNN - used it as a mere excuse to sully Madonna and made people think that she offended one of her fans. Madonna was not rude at all with her Italian fan. She thanked him for the present, then she confessed with one of her actors that she doesn't like hydrangeas and added that "he [the fan] doesn't know it obviously." We also remind that Madonna didn't know her microphone was on.

There is a funny reportage broadcast by CNN, where it's said that Madonna insulted hydrangeas. Madonna's spokesperson Liz Rosenberg responds with a statement to CNN.
"She's entitled to like any flower she wants and she didn't want to hurt the feeling of the hydrangeas of the world," says Liz Rosenberg. "No disrespect to the hydrangeas lovers of the world but she prefers different types of flowers."



A lot of fuss about nothing. This is the evidence that she is still Madonna!

Madonna W.E. to premiere at BFI London Film Festival in October 2011

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


BREAKING NEWS - Madonna's W.E. will receive UK premiere at the 55th BFI London Film Festival.

The UK premiere of W.E. will take place at Empire Leicester Square on Sunday October 23 2011 at 7 PM. It's not clear wheter Madonna will attend the gala screening.

The LFF 2011, which runs from October 12 to 27, will feature 204 films, including 13 world premieres.

 

W.E. will screen again the day after, October 24 (12:15), at Vue Screen 5.

 

The movie will be distributed in the U.K. by STUDIOCANAL.

 

BFI Members can book festival tickets online from 19 September. Public booking opens 26 September.

 

Besides Madonna's W.E., there are other movies premiered at Venice Film Festival 2011 to screen at LFF, among them George Clooney's The Ides of March and Roman Polanski's Carnage.

Madonna denies saying Lady Gaga is obsessed with her to Belgian magazine Le Soir

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


There is a formal denial to what Madonna reportedly said about Lady Gaga during an interview she gave to Belgian magazine Le Soir, while Madonna was in Venice for the Film Festival.

 

Madonna's spokesperson Liz Rosenberg denies the quote about Lady Gaga. "This is not true. Madonna did not say anything negative about Lady Gaga during her press conference," she told the Daily Mail. But, unfortunately this false news already went global.

Madonna: 'Berlusconi is unsuited to govern Italy'

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


While she was in Venice for the world premiere of W.E. at this year's Film Festival, Madonna gave a lot of interviews to international media. One with Swedish SVT television made the headlines for the release dates of her new single and upcoming album.

 

And now we are sure that the interview Madonna gave to Italian magazine Oggi - out now - will raise a lot of discussion and controversies. In this cover interview, Madonna has also been asked to reply to a question regarding Italian Prime Minister Silvio Berlusconi.

"What do I think about Berlusconi? I wouldn't like to talk about it now. But English weekly magazine The Economist already said everything, isn't it?" And then Madonna laconically added that Berlusconi "is unsuited to govern Italy."

 

In this interview Madonna also talk about love: "Loving means giving". But, we will be back on this later.
