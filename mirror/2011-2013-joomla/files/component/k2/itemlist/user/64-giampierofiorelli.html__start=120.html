Madonna expected on September 12 for the W.E. Red Carpet at the Toronto International Film Festival

As announced, after being world premiered at the Venice Film Festival 2011, Madonna's W.E. will be screened at this year's TIFF - Toronto International Film Festival.

Madonna met and congratulated with James Franco for his new movie 'Sal' premiered in Venice

Actually there is no media coverage, but Just Jared got the scoop of Madonna congratulating with James Franco last Saturday at Venice's Palazzo del Cinema for his new movie, Sal, which was premiere at Venice Film Festival 2011.

Sal - directed, wrote and starred in by James Franco - is the story of the last days of homosexual actor Sal Mineo.

Homosexuality is a topic Madonna has always been very keen on.

A few years ago James Franco starred in Milk with and directed by Sean Penn, Madonna's ex husband. A movie that, reportedly, she really loved.

Madonna: 'Being a mother is harder than being a director'

Interviewed by Italian magazine Gente, out next Monday, Madonna confesses that being a director is a strenuous work, "but not as much as being a mother."

"Children tie you down for your whole life," says Madonna. "While a movie, sooner or later, ends."

Madonna also talks about her second movie W.E., "I started thinking about this storyline when I married Guy Ritchie in 2000 and I moved in England. As Wallis, I also felt alone and I felt like an outsider. Then she has been treated basically as a bitch. Just like all the women that over the centuries dared to defy man rules."

Love is also part of the interview, with Madonna saying that "women keep on thinking that happiness depends on finding and marrying the Prince Charming. I do not believe it. I think happiness is in our own hands, and only when we understand it we can find completeness in a partner."

What happened inside and outside the Gucci Award for Women in Cinema: promotional footage

This promotional footage courtesy of Gucci shows what happened outside and inside the very first Gucci Award for Women in Cinema.

As you can see from the clip, among the guests there was someone well known to Madonna: Adriano Giannini, co-star of Swept Away directed by her ex-husband Guy Ritchie, and brother of Madonna's new friend Frida Giannini.



Also the actresses of W.E. were attending the party: Abbie Cornish, Andrea Riseborough and Natalie Dormer.

Other guests were: Luca Argentero, Myriam Catania, Bianca Brandolini d’Adda, Carolina Crescentini, Ginevra Elkann, Sarah Felberbaum, Giulia Bevilacqua and Valentina Lodovini.

All guests were Gucci dressed.
