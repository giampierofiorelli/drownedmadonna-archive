Arianne Phillips ready to work for Madonna's New Album on 2012.

Arianne Phillips has just finished working on Madonna's second directoral film W.E. and is already thinking about her next adventure with her Madgesty.

 

"Madonna has a lot of new stuff coming out in the New Year as well because she’s recording an album, so we’re going to have so much to do," Arianne Phillips told WWD.

 

This includes a promo tour in Spring 2012 as well as a world tour on summer 2012. We can't wait to see what Madonna and talented Arianne will come with next year!

Friday, 23 September 2011 11:30

Harvey Nichols showcasing Vionnet pieces worn in Madonna's W.E.

London's Harvey Nichols is showcasing a selection of Vionnet pieces worn in Madonna's second director fatigue, W.E., as seen on Andrea Riseborough.

 

"The film W.E. gave Vionnet such a wonderful opportunity to showcase the exquisite craftsmanship and skill of the house," the label's ceo Matteo Marzotto told Vogue. "We were thrilled to be considered for the project and it was so exciting to be part of such a lavish production that portrayed one of history's most intriguing women. The film, W.E., challenged us to utilise our extensive knowledge and craftsmanship to re-create the original pieces designed by Madeleine Vionnet for Wallis Simpson with precision and accuracy. Extensive research was carried out on fabrics and construction using original pieces from the archives at the Louvre."

 

The chosen pieces will be shown on London's Harvey Nichols' first floor, alongside the current autumn/winter 2011-12 collection.

 

"Harvey Nichols is such a visionary retailer with a world renowned reputation so it is such a pleasure that Vionnet can work with them on this installation," said Marzotto. "The store is the perfect place to showcase the intricate and exquisite pieces and will allow customers to truly appreciate the attention to detail and craftsmanship of the house, we hope that the installation provides a greater awareness of the unique heritage and vision of Madeleine Vionnet."

 

The installation will be on display until Friday September 30.
