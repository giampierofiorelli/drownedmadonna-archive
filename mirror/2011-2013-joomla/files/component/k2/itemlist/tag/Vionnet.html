Madonna's W.E. Vionnet installation at Harvey Nichols London: Photographs of the Showcasing

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


A few days ago we reported about the exclusive Vionnet installation at Harvey Nichols London, showcasing specially commisioned pieces by Vionnet from the film W.E.

 

We can finally show you a few photographs of the installations thanks to Harvey Nichols London and Vionnet Paris. Enjoy!

 

 

Harvey Nichols London is hosting the special Vionnet installation till September 30 in a dedicated space on the First Floor of the store. The installation is providing customers with the unique opportunity to view the special archive pieces from the film.

"Harvey Nichols is the perfect place to showcase the intricate and exquisite pieces and will allow customers to truly appreciate the attention to detail and craftsmanship of the House" explains Matteo Marzotto, CEO of Vionnet. Vionnet worked alongside director Madonna and costume designer Arianne Phillips to re-create original Vionnet pieces from Wallis Simpsons wardrobe.

The installation is providing customers with a greater awareness of the unique heritage and vision of Madeleine Vionnet.

 

Vionnet Installation,

14th- 30th September 2011

Harvey Nichols showcasing Vionnet pieces worn in Madonna's W.E.

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


London's Harvey Nichols is showcasing a selection of Vionnet pieces worn in Madonna's second director fatigue, W.E., as seen on Andrea Riseborough.

 

"The film W.E. gave Vionnet such a wonderful opportunity to showcase the exquisite craftsmanship and skill of the house," the label's ceo Matteo Marzotto told Vogue. "We were thrilled to be considered for the project and it was so exciting to be part of such a lavish production that portrayed one of history's most intriguing women. The film, W.E., challenged us to utilise our extensive knowledge and craftsmanship to re-create the original pieces designed by Madeleine Vionnet for Wallis Simpson with precision and accuracy. Extensive research was carried out on fabrics and construction using original pieces from the archives at the Louvre."

 

The chosen pieces will be shown on London's Harvey Nichols' first floor, alongside the current autumn/winter 2011-12 collection.

 

"Harvey Nichols is such a visionary retailer with a world renowned reputation so it is such a pleasure that Vionnet can work with them on this installation," said Marzotto. "The store is the perfect place to showcase the intricate and exquisite pieces and will allow customers to truly appreciate the attention to detail and craftsmanship of the house, we hope that the installation provides a greater awareness of the unique heritage and vision of Madeleine Vionnet."

 

The installation will be on display until Friday September 30.

Unpleasant incident on the W.E. red carpet in Venice for Matteo and Marta Marzotto

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


At first, he thought it was a joke when Venice Film Festival Security told him "you cannot enter."

This unpleasant incident happened to the Vionnet Chairman Matteo Marzotto and his mother, the fashion designer Marta Marzotto.

What made this episode more embarrassing and paradoxical is the fact that Madonna was wearing one of Vionnet exclusive clothes that night!

Matteo Marzotto burst into a furious rage with the Venice Film Festival team, and he talked about "dilettantism."

According to the important entrepreneur and chairman, Venice Film Festival decided who was allowed to enter the theatre at their discretion. They didn't care if Marzotto and his mother hold a ticket. The service was "disgraceful," he said.

"Walking the red carpet I just wanted to say 'look how pretty is Madonna with her cloth designed by Vionnet'," said Matteo Marzotto. "It was a source of pride, not only for me, but also for Italians. But it was not possible."
