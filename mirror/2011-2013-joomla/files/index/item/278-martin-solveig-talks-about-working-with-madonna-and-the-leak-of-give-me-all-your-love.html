Martin Solveig talks about working with Madonna and the leak of 'Give Me All Your Love'

"We had a terrible leak ... and everyone is very upset about what happened," Martin Solveig tells the Boston Herald.

 

Even thought Solveig cannot talk about "Give Me All Your Love", he says that working with Madonna is a dream come true. "I would have considered this as impossible," Solveig says. "People ask who you would dream to work with. To me working with Madonna was not really something I could do. When I got the call, first I thought it was a joke. And then when I realized it wasn't, I was real-ly overwhelmed."

 

"The collaboration has been an enormous joy and an enormous surprise," Solveig adds.
