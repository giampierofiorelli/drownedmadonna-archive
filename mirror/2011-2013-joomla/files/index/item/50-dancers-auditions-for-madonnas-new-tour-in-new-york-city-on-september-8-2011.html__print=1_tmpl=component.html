Perez Hilton has got cool news! There will be a live casting call in New York City for dancers who wish to join Madonna on her next tour.The casting will take place at Broadway Dance Center on Septeber 8 2011.

Madonna long-time choreographers Rich & Tone Talauega will judge the auditioners.

It should be part of the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange project.

 

Here are the details:

 

LIVE CASTING CALL, 21+
BROADWAY DANCE CENTER
SEPTEMBER 8 2011
DOORS OPEN AT 7AM
AUDITIONS FROM 8 AM TO 1 PM
MADONNA'S CELEBRITY CHOREOGRAPHERS RICH & TONE WILL JUDGE YOUR AUDITION!
