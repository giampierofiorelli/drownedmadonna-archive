The official trailer of W.E. is doing justice to the movie directed by Madonna. A few minutes were enough to make general public and some media open up their eyes and realize that critics were factious and specious.

The second Madonna's fatigue had a never-ending standing ovation in Venice at the end of the screening gala, but press omitted to report it.

 

"The movie may have received mixed reviews, but when the very first trailer debuted online this morning, we must admit we were pleasantly surprised," writes Marie Claire. "Stunningly beautiful and uber-stylish, we'll certianly be first in line to see this one," they add.

 

"The trailer shows a beautiful cinematography by Hagen Bogdanski," AceShowbiz reports.
