On Wednesday, 18 January photographer Curtis Knapp will have an iPad app on sale on iTunes / a coffee table book for the iPad, titled: ICONS – THE PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY OF CURTIS KNAPP.

Curtis Knapp's portrait of Madonna for the cover of ISLAND magazine is the cover of the app. Over 50 people plus extras and Madonna has 4 or 5 extras as well as some other people such as Andy Warhol. Steve Buscemi wrote a fun introduction. Also it has some small audios.

 

Thanks to Curtis Knapp.
