DrownedMadonna.com collaborator Tony Albanese are doing his best to cover live tonight exclusive Madonna danceevent from New York's Roseland Ballroom.

 

"Everyone keep checking the DrownedMadonna Facebook page tonight (http://www.facebook.com/drownedmadonna)," Tony says. "I will be doing real time updates and posting photos and videos throughout the evening. It promises to be a fun, unique night and I will take you along for the experience!" Please join our Facebook page.

 

We recommend to stay tuned also to DrownedMadonna newspage.

 

People are about to enter the Roseland Ballroom now. Tonight Madonna is going to choose a dancer who will tour with her as well as performing with her at next year Super Bowl. Martin Solveig is attending and playing at the party.

 

"The VIP line is insane," reports Tony. "But the GA line is even crazier!"
