Madonna & Smirnoff Global Dance Party to take place at Roseland Ballroom on November 12: The Finalists and The Details!

The big final Madonna's dance event in collaboration with Smirnoff is getting closer. DrownedMadonna.com just received interesting information on the event, part of the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project.

 

First of all the winner will not have a chance to tour with Madonna, but he/she will become for sure an official Member of Madonna's Dance Crew! Madonna will PERSONALLY select the best dancer at Global Dance Party out of 11 global dance contest finalists.

 

WHEN: Saturday, November 12th from 9:00 pm - Late.

WHERE: Roseland Ballroom, 239 W. 52ND St. NYC - where Madonna performed to promote her "Music" and "Hard Candy" albums.

 

The eleven dancers competing for this life-changing opportunity are: Lil Buck (US), Skorpion (Belgium), Medhi (GB), Deydey (Netherlands), Kyubee (Belgium), Sarah Bee (US), Maya (Japan), Manuela (Belgium), Neik (Netherlands), Princess Lockerooo (US), and Hurrikane (US).

 

The 11 finalists have been personally selected by Madonna’s long-time choreographers Rich & Tone Talauega and will rehearse as a group for one full week with Rich & Tone leading up to their individual final performance in front of Madonna.
