Madonna's W.E. not postponed in the UK. Tentative release date for the Soundtrack

As already reported, the release date of Madonna's movie W.E. has been postponed in the USA from December 9, 2011 to February 3, 2012.

While this postponement has an effect in the Netherlands, where the movie will be released on March 15, 2012 instead of December 22, 2011, this does not effect the UK release date at all, DrownedMadonna.com learnt.

 

The W.E. original soundtrack will most likely be released nearer to the release date of the movie in the UK DrownedMadonna.com heard. But no definitive news are available since they are still working on it these days.
