It was strongly rumoured, and now will.i.am has confirmed that L.M.F.A.O. will duet with Madonna during the Super Bowl on February 5.

 

 

 

 

Talking to Capital FM, will.i.am said: "I'm going to the Super Bowl this year to see my group L.M.F.A.O. perform with Madonna."

"Check that out, will.i.am Music Group is pretty freaking two for two. One year the Super Bowl the next year another group part of the Super Bowl in collaboration with Madonna. That's still happening."
