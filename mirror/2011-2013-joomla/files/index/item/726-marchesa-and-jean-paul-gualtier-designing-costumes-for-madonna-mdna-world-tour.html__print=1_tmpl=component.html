As our Facebook and Twitter likers and followers already know, Jean-Paul Gualtier confirmed today that he is designing costumes for Madonna's MDNA World Tour. "Well, yes. Yes I am," Gualtier confesses to fashion editor Melanie Rickey.

 

DrownedMadonna.com alse learnt that Marchesa is designing clothes for the MDNA World Tour as well. You probably remember that Madonna worn a Marchesa dress for the New York Premiere of her W.E. movie.

 

More designers are working on Madonna 2012 World Tour.
