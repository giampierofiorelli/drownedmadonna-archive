The annual Bid 2 Beat AIDS auction has launched on eBay. Proceeds benefit Lifebeat - Music Fights HIV.  There are 427 lots of signed celebrity items and experiences, among them there are two Madonna lots in the auction:

  • The Ultimate Madonna Fan Pack - with 27 promotional items, including a numbered limited edition lithograph, a limited edition t-shirt, vinyls, CDs, promo flats, DVDs, and more
  • A copy of Lotsa de Casha, signed by Madonna
