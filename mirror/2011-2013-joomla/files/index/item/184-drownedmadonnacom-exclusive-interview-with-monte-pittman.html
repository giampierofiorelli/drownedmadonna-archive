EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW - Madonna's fans knows him for being her guitar teacher as well as her guitarist since the promotion for the "Music" album back on 2000. But Monte Pittman first of all is a great talent, a musician and singer who has just released his second own album, "Pain, Love & Destiny" that you can buy here.

 

 

Talking about the writing sessions of "Easy Ride," one of DrownedMadonna.com Team favourite songs, Monte Pittman said that "it came about by being a chord progression you can play the Harmonic Minor scale over."

 

We asked Monte if he is planning to tour with Madonna next year. "If she’s playing somewhere and asks me to join her – I’m there," he said. "She gave me my break. She comes first."

 

Stay tuned to DrownedMadonna.com to read the whole EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW with the one and only Monte Pittman. And do not forget to buy a copy of is new album out now!
