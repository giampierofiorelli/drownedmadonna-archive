Guy Ritchie on his marriage with Madonna

This is probably the very first time director Guy Ritchie openly talks about his marrige with Madonna in an interview.

 

"I enjoyed my first marriage," Guy Ritchie told Details. "It's definitely not something I regret. The experience was ultimately very positive."

"I love the kids that came out of it, and I could see no other route to take," Guy added. But he moved on and claimed that he "stepped into a soap opera, and I lived in it for quite a long period of my life. I'll probably be more eloquent on it 10 years from now."

 

Asked about what he learnt from his marriage with Madonna, Guy Ritchie said that "when you end up with a lot of the things you set out to chase and find that you've stumbled into all sorts of hollow victories, then you become deeply philosophical. I'm quite happy that that experience was accelerated for me. I'm glad I made money, in other words. And I'm glad I got married."
