DrownedMadonna.com is attending the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project in New York on November 12th. But the great news we have for all Madonna's fans is that we can give away:

  • 1 PAIR of VIP tickets
  • 4 PAIRS of General Admission (GA) tickets


VIP = complimentary food and drinks all night; GA = two complimentary cocktails per ticket.

It is a stunning chance to live an Exclusive Dance Night with Madonna celebrating her upcoming album and next tour. The Dance Event in collaboration with Smirnoff is taking place at Roseland Ballroom in New York City. One of the eleven world-wide contestants will became an official member of Madonna's Dance Crew and, besides other project, he/she will tour with her next year.

To enter DrownedMadonna.com CONTEST for this Exclusive Madonna Dance Event you must be at least 21 years old.

How can you enter the CONTEST to win tickets for the November 12th Dance Party? You have 3 different choices:

  1. The Dance of Life Photo Contest: To enter, became a fan of DrownedMadonna Facebook Page, and post your best Photograph doing one of Madonna's classic dance moves from any tour, video or performance. The photo with more "Likes" will win a PAIR of tickets.
  2. The Dance of Life Music Contest: To enter, register at DrownedMadonna, and post on Our Forum a Story regarding a Madonna's Dance Song which is important for your life and explain why. We will post a link to this story on our Facebook Page. The story with more "Likes" will a PAIR of tickets.
  3. The Followers Hunter Contest: To enter, invite as more friends as possible to became a fan of DrownedMadonna Facebook Page, then ask them to post your FB name. Alternatively, Invite as more friends as possible to register at DrownedMadonna, then ask them to post your name on Our Forum. Those who will bring more "Followers" to DrownedMadonna will win a PAIR of tickets.


The Overall Winner will get the best prize: 1 PAIR of VIP tickets. There are 4 PAIRS of General Admission tickets for the other 4 winners.

To have more chances to win, ask all of your friends to VOTE FOR YOU. We suggest you also to post about it on your Facebook Wall or tweet about it.

Hurry up. Our CONTEST ends on November 10th at 12AM (Central European Time). The winners will be announced later on November 11th. So come on, if it makes you feel good, we say do it!
