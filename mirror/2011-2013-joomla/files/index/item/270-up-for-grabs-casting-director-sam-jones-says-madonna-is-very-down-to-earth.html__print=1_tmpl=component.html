Casting director Sam Jones, who casted Madonna's West End debut Up For Grabs, says Madonna was very down-to-earth.

"Madonna was lovely, she didn't behave like a diva," Sam Jones told Wales Online. "She really enjoyed it and threw herself into it. It was quite an event."

 

While Madonna had already been named as the lead, Sam Jones had to cast her co-stars. "When you have a big name, you have to think about the qualities the other actors need to complement them. You always have to make the most interesting combination possible."
