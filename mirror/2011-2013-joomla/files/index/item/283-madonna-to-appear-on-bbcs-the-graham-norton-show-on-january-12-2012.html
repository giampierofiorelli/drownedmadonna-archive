BREAKING NEWS - Madonna is set to appear on BBC's The Graham Norton Show for the first time on Saturday, January 14 2012 to promote her second directoral movie W.E. She will be joined by actors Andrea Riseborough and James D'Arcy.

 

"I couldn't be more excited," Graham Norton says. "This is my real Christmas present. I may have to retire, but that’s not a promise."
