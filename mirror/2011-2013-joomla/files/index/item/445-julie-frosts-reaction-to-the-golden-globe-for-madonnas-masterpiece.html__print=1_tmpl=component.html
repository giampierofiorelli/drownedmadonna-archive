"Being acknowledged by the Hollywood Foreign Press amongst a room full of so many people I admire and accepting the award next to Madonna, was such an honor," Julie Frost to CBSNews.com. She co-wrote award-winning Masterpiece with Madonna and Jimmy Harry. The Southern Californian songwriter has also co-written songs for Beyonce, the Black Eyed Peas and Pitbull.

 

"As Adam Levine and Jimmy Fallon announced the award for best original song, I thought, no matter what happens I'm proud of our song and to be included in the same category as so many of my musical heroes," adds Julie Frost.
