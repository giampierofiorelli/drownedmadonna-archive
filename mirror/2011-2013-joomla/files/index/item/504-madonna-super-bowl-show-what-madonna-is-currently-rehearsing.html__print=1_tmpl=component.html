DrownedMadonna.com learnt what Madonna is currently rehearsing for the Super Bowl Half Time Show this Sunday, February 5. As we told you on our latest news about the Super Bowl, we do not want to ruin the surprise. Therefore, we took several hours to decide if we had to post it or not. Since things are still subject to changes and the setlist is partially leaking anyway, we finally decided to share what we heard.

 

If you do not want to ruin the surprise, do not read what follow.

 

Here are the songs that Madonna is currently rehearsing and planning to perform as part of a MASH-UP, with Give Me All Your Luvin' taking more time:

 

Give Me All Your Luvin'
Hung Up
Vogue
Like a Prayer
Ray of Light
Music

Holiday was also mixed and rehearsed, but now we may just get some element of this 80's classic throughout another song, like Into The Groove had bits of Jump for the Sticky & Sweet Tour. But, keep in mind that Madonna could change her mind and actually perform Holiday as part of the MASH-UP.
