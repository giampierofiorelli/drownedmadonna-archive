The former Oasis guitarrist Noel Gallagher latest target is Lady Gaga.

 

"Is it about the music? Really? She's got the publicity side sorted out, but where's the f****** music?, Noel Gallagher told Q magazine. "Paparazzi? It's Madonna lite. Madonna was hardcore. She took it to the edge musically as well as everything else."
