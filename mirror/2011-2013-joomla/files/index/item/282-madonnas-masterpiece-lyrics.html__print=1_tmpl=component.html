A man who saw Madonna's W.E. last night at a private screening in Los Angeles was able to write down some of the lyrics of Masterpiece, which plays over the end credits.

 

It hurts so much to be in love with a masterpiece

Nothing is indestructable.

When I first saw you,

all the darkness turned to light.

It seems to me it's what you like.

And honestly,

it's not fun to always be the chosen one.

I'm right by your side.

Like a thief in the night.

 

He says that Madonna's voice "sounds stunning! A very pretty song and the movie is much better than people are giving it credit."
