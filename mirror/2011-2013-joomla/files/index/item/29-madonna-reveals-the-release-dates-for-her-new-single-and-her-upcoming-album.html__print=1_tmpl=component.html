In this interview with Swedish Television SVT, besides talking about W.E., Madonna was also very open to talk about her upcoming album.

Actually she confirmed that she is currently working with William Orbit again. But, most important, she said that her new single will be out next Febraury or March, while the new album will be out Spring 2012.
