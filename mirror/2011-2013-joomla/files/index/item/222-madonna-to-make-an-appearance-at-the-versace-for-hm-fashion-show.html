RUMOURS - According to Billboard, there are unconfirmed reports that Madonna will make an appearance at the Versace for H&M fashion show in New York this evening. The magazine also mentions rumours that Madonna's Give Me All Your Love might be introduced in full at this event as well as at another New York event this week: the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Party.

 

Billboard magazine also report about today's leak of Madonna's Give Me All Your Love and point out that within a few hours, it was among one of the top 10 trending topics worldwide on Twitter. Contacted by Billboard Madonna's publicist, Liz Rosenberg, did not immediately respond.
