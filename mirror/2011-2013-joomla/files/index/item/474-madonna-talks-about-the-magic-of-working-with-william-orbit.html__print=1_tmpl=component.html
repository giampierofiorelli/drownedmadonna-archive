Madonna and William Orbit worked together for so many times that when Madonna was asked about their latest collaboration, words weren't needed.

"With William, I didn't really have a discussion," Madonna told MTV News at the New York premiere of W.E. "We've worked on stuff for so many years that we kind of finish each other's sentences. He knows my taste and what I like."

 

"Magic happens when we get into a recording studio together," Madonna added.
