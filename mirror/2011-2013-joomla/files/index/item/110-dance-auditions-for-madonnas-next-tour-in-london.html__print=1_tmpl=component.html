BREAKING NEWS - We at DrownedMadonna.com has just been informed that there will be dance auditions in London on Friday 23 September 2011 for Madonna's Next Tour. You can comment this news below, directly with your facebook account.

"We thought this would be a great opportunity for any of fans of Drowned Madonna to get involved," we have been told.

 

To kick-off the competition in Great Britain, Madonna and Smirnoff are teaming up with well known Pineapple Dance Studios to host open audition filming on Friday 23 September 2011 between 12:00PM – 9:00PM at Pineapple Dance Studios, 7 Langley Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JA.

Whether it’s krumping, salsa, pointe, pop and locking, or lyrical, dancers are invited to put their skills to the test for the ultimate prize.

 

We remind you that, for those hopefuls unable to make the open audition filming, aspiring dancers are encouraged to upload a 60-second video of their most original routine by October 10 to be selected for one of the ten spots at the November live auditions. Entrants must be 21 years old and over. For details on the dance submission process and terms and conditions, visit www.smirnoff.com.

 

Madonna will attend the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange Project event in New York City on November 12, it was confirmed by Live Nation Entertainment. The event will include the search to find the most unique, and innovative dancer who performs in front of Madonna and her long-time choreographers, Rich + Tone Talauega. The last dancer standing at the end of the epic dance-off has the chance of winning the coveted prize and becoming part of her official dance crew.
