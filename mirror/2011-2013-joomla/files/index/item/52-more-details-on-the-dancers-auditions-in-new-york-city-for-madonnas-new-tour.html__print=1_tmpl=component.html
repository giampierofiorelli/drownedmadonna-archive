Smirnoff just gave us more details regarding the dancers auditions on September 8 in New York City for Madonna's new tour.

 

This casting call is for New York and Connecticut residents only.

One dancer from this audition will be chosen by Madonna's choreographers Rich and Tone Talauega to attend the November 12 dance event. The Smirnoff - Madonna Nightlife Exchange final event will take place in New York as well.

 

Come dressed to dance. You must not wear clothing with visible logos/brands. Please bring proper identification showing current address. All auditons may be filmed for use in future promotional content.
