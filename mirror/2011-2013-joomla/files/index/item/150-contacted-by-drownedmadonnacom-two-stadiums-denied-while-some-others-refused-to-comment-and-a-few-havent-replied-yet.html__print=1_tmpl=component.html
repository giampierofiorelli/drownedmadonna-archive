Yesterday night, while we were debating if sharing the document we received by an unconfirmed source or not, we also contacted the stadiums mentioned on the unconfirmed itinerary.

 

At the moment only two stadiums decided to comment. Contacted by DrownedMadonna.com, both ANZ Stadium in Sydney, Australia, and Mt Smart Stadium in Auckland, New Zeland, denied that they have been booked by Live Nation on those dates.

Some other stadiums refused to comment, while a few didn't reply yet.

 

We remind you that at this point we cannot confirm or deny the authenticity of the hypothetical Madonna's next tour itinery we received by an unconfirmed and anonymous source. We still invite you to take it with a grain of salt. We keep on not denying our scepticism, most of all because it contrasts with what we heard a few months ago. But, we leave it up to you to decide.

 

We will keep you up-to-date.
