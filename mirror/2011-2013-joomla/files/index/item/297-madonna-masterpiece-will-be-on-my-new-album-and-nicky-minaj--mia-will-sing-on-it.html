Interviewed last night during the screening of W.E. at MoMa in New York, Madonna also talked about her new album.

Madonna confirmed that Masterpiece will be included on her new album. She also said that Nicky Minaj and M.I.A. "are both singing on my record."

 

It's funny to see that asked about the Super Bowl, Madonna was not sure if she was allowed to confirm it. The official announcement was being broadcast on those minutes.

 

Madonna also showed to be proud to represent the Italians with her Roberto Cavalli clothes.
