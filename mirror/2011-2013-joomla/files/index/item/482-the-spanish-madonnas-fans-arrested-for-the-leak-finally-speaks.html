The Spanish Madonna's Fans arrested for the leak finally speaks!

The news of the Spanish Madonna's fans arrested after the leak of the demo of Madonna's Give Me All Your Luvin made the headline around the globe. Jorge - this is his name - has finally talked with a Spanish newspaper, El Pais.

We at DrownedMadonna do not know if Jorge was the one to upload the song online, but most Madonna's fans online know that he is not a hacker. Also every fan heard that someone is making money with Madonna's unreleased demos, still-to-be-released songs, and pictures. It is well know that there is someone within Madonna's circle selling this stuff for years. His/her identity is apparently unknown. We really hope Madonna and her camp can finally found out who is the original leaker.

 

You can read the translation in English of the whole article by El Pais thanks to Carlos Garcia

 

It’s midday when Jorge is prepared to open his small appliances store. Nothing makes suspect that this young 31 years old guy with any criminal record and well-knowed in his neighbourhood will be mentioned by Madonna as “her hacker”.
What happened to this guy to make him feel deeply disappointed with his idol? Operation Madonnaleaks – triggered by the leak of Gimme All Your Love – has blown his fan-love down. He used to go to all Madonna’s concerts in the last 20 years and to collect press trimmings and other rarities as only a fan could do.
Maybe behind his life there’s the “attraction law”, with dangerous and not always predictable consequences summarized in the sentence “be careful with what you desire”. Or maybe it’s the last marketing operation of Her Madgesty, Madonna Ciccone.
Let’s remind the acts. Last 20th December, Jorge Moro Ruiz, born in Zaragoza and a professional freelance in the inoffensive world of electric material, became suddenly in J.M.R., arrested for a criminal attempt for revealing secrets in the Internet. “I still astonished for what has happened. They arrested a very normal guy. I’m not a hacker and, of course, not a criminal.” He has reason to be angry. Instead of being innocent, as it has been displayed in his judicial appearance last 10th January, when Garrigues’ lawyers, legal representatives of Madonna, didn’t present as accusation. The media, from the corrosive The Sun to the prestigious The New York Times, defended a very different version.
It all began a day before detention, in 19th December, when the complaint by Madonna Ciccone triggered an operation against her new song diffusion. Only a day after he was arrested: “Is completely surrealist to imagine something like that, especially if you are not involved”, he confesses. “I even thought that it was a TV joke, one week before the Fool’s Day” (In Spain it’s in December 28th).
To put some sense in this leak story, we only have to write in any web searcher “Madonna Chile”, the most international active forum in the world. “I remember how surprised I was when I saw in the download area the new single by Madonna, uploaded by someone called “mjco”. As other registered members, I only had to click the link and download the song.”
Javier Notivoli – the mediatic Spanish lawyer known by the corruption case of a little town in Spain and also the murder of a Spanish major – was the responsible to close this “nonsense” case. As he told, “my defended didn’t upload the song, he only downloaded it as other 3.133 people did. He neither could be accused for a copyright attempt because it was a demo that hadn’t been released. And of course, the guilty for a criminal attempt for revealing secrets in the Internet was who uploaded the demo, not him.” “Police reacted to a Madonna’s complaint. Because of the good behavior of my client, who was up to collaborate in every moment, it was not necessary to present a court order.”
Even when the case has been overridden for lack of evidence, who would restore his honor to this citizen addicted to anxiolytics since the case was revealed? His lawyer doesn’t answer. Meanwhile, Madonna continues joking about her hacker as she did in Graham Norton’s Show. “Hear her talking about me as a criminal injury is shameful”, complains Jorge. “Or her lawyers aren’t telling her the truth or she’s pretending to seem stupid.”

The “Robin Hood” of collectors
Since the suspicious leaking in forum Madonna Chile, the catchy cheerleader’s melody can be enjoyed without any legal restriction in Youtube. More contradictions: only one day after the leak, in 9th November, the song jumped to Twitter’s forum, the new press office of celebs. As Jorge Moro reminds, “Guy Oseary, Madonna’s manager, twitted he complained the demo’s leak, but he was satisfied with the positive reaction of people. I call it double standard.” Now, we only have to wait her comeback at the Super Bowl.
No so much time ago, musicians had to promote in a lot of traditional media: TV, radio and music magazines like Rolling Stones. Then, the internet irrupted with illegal downloads. “Today, fans are doing what disc companies used to do”. As police could prove, Jorge’s behavior was not exactly a collector’s one, obsessed with his rarities. And, of course, wasn’t a hacker’s one (he’s computer skills are intermediate). “I’ve been sharing my entire Madonna’s collection all my life, without any economic return. “
Maybe, his only error was to break the game’s rules, uploading to the Internet old demos, instrumental versions and other curiosities by Madonna. Behind nicknames as Dboy or Johnny Pearson – one of his favorite musicians – this Spanish guy earned the hatred of a lot of collectors because they wanted to win money with leakings and he does not. “I have paid a lot of money”, he confesses without regrets.
In other order of things, the case was closed with a picture capture of the mentioned forum. Who knows a little bit of computers, may ask about IP’s (a personal number which identifies a computer) of who leaked the song. A clue that certainly would have made the truth closer to us.
This story is not new. Ten years ago, Madonna suffered the sweet consequences of the leaking of her single Music in Napster, shot to number one. It also happened with American life, Hung Up and, as Jorge says, “with Hard Candy in 2008”. It’s really surprising how easy it is to leak songs from the music companies. Now, we have to suspect of that easy permeable environment from where Madonna’s songs leak.
