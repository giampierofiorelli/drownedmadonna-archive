As earlier reported,  people at Graham Norton Show are planning to release a limited number exclusively for fans of Madonna, and these will be allocated on Monday 9th January at 6pm. We got more details on how to apply. Send an email now to This e-mail address is being protected from spambots. You need JavaScript enabled to view it - with the subject GN SHOW – MADONNA - and  include a contact telephone number and let them know what area you are from.

It is greatly appreciated if you could answer all 3 questions below with as much detail as possible:

  1. If you could ask Madonna any question in the world, what would it be? (Please submit a few if you like!) Be as original as you like or keep it simple. Either way, they’d love to hear your questions.
  2. What makes you a big fan of Madonna?
  3. Do you have a collection of Madonna merchandise? If you have an interesting collection, please let them know what you have. Feel free to attach any pictures you have of your collection.

 

Remember that there are only a limited number of tickets available.
