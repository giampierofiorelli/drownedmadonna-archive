Kris Thykier: 'Madonna works harder than anyone I've met'

Film producer Kris Thykier is behind two of the year's biggest Brit flicks: The Debt and Madonna's W.E.

 

"Madonna works harder than anyone I've met," Kris Thykier told GQ. "You don't always know what you're getting with a big name, but she was the last one on set [of her film W.E.] every night and kept the crew going when they were exhausted."

 

Being into the film industry, Thykier handled the marketing of some fantastic films, such as Four Weddings And A Funeral and Lock, Stock And Two Smoking Barrels directed by Madonna's former husband Guy Ritchie.
