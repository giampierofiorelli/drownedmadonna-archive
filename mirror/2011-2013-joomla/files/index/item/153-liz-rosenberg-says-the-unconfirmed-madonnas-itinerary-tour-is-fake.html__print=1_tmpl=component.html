Finally there is an official statement. Contacted by Billboard, Madonna's spokeswoman Liz Rosenberg said the tour dates and press release are "fake." At the same time, we have no additional news to bring to you from stadiums we contacted.

 

What we originally heard about the next Madonna's world tour - which made us be very sceptical about the unconfirmed itinerary sent by an anonymous source - is that Madonna will start the rehearsals around March and the tour will begin in June.

It's very interesting to see how much attention this news caused worldwide. Every major media reported it. Even if we were sceptical, since we were not able to deny the document, it was worthy to share it. And this is proved by the coverage it got. What an excitement every new Madonna's tour is causing! She is always the Queen!
