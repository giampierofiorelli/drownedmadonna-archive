BREAKING NEWS - Talking with Ryan Seacrest at 102.7 KIIS-FM, Madonna announced that the second single from MDNA will be Girls Gone Wild, co-produced with Benny Benassi.

 

Madonna added that she will film a music video for Girls Gone Wild in a couple of weeks. She is going to have auditions for her 2012 World Tour next week, and she will shoot the video for Girls Gone Wild after that. Asked about the rumour about Britney Spears being featured on the track, Madonna said it's not true. "No, it's just all me," she said.
