BREAKING NEWS - It's not 100% confirmed yet, but Madonna is interested in recording one of NERVO songs for her new album. It has just been revealed by the Australian Nervo twins themself in an exclusive interview with Jon ALi.

 

"I actually have some enormous news for you. We've just started telling people. Madonna has expressed a great interest in recording one of our songs and William Orbit wants to produce it," Mim and Liv Nervo said. "That's like wow, we honestly are so lucky. We couldn't be more excited."

 

"It's funny, we speak to our parents quite a bit. We always tell them what we're doing and who we're working with and they don't really know of them. I told them Madonna and they jumped for joy so we're so excited," they added.

"It's not 100% confirmed but she has expressed an interest," they admitted.

 

We are sure that this will make fans pick up the rumours regarding another Australian act, SIA, who has been reportedly asked to work with Madonna and wizard William Orbit as well.
