Madonna and her daughter Lola are looking for a brand new model for their Material Girl collection. Be their next Lucky Star! Watch this official clip where Madonna is absolutely Stunning!

 

 

If you want to be the next Material Girl, you can apply for one of 5 live castings in New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, San Francisco and Miami. The first open casting is on November 2nd (5 PM) at Macy's in New York. The second casting is on November 4th (6 PM) at Macy's in Miami. The third casting is on November 8th at Macy's in Chicago. The forth casting is on November 12th (2 PM) at Macy's in San Francisco. And the final casting is on November 19 at Macy's in Los Angeles.

 

Prizes includes: a trip to New York for a custom photo-shoot, your Material Girl ad in the coolest magazines, you'll be the face of Material Girl in stores and online, a billboard in Times Square, $5,000 in cash and much more.

 

Enter online at Materialgirlcollection.com.
