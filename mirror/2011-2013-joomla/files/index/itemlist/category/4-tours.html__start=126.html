More details on the Dancers Auditions in New York City for Madonna's New Tour

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Smirnoff just gave us more details regarding the dancers auditions on September 8 in New York City for Madonna's new tour.

 

This casting call is for New York and Connecticut residents only.

One dancer from this audition will be chosen by Madonna's choreographers Rich and Tone Talauega to attend the November 12 dance event. The Smirnoff - Madonna Nightlife Exchange final event will take place in New York as well.

 

Come dressed to dance. You must not wear clothing with visible logos/brands. Please bring proper identification showing current address. All auditons may be filmed for use in future promotional content.

Dancers auditions for Madonna's New Tour in New York City on September 8 2011

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Perez Hilton has got cool news! There will be a live casting call in New York City for dancers who wish to join Madonna on her next tour.The casting will take place at Broadway Dance Center on Septeber 8 2011.

Madonna long-time choreographers Rich & Tone Talauega will judge the auditioners.

It should be part of the Smirnoff Nightlife Exchange project.

 

Here are the details:

 

LIVE CASTING CALL, 21+
BROADWAY DANCE CENTER
SEPTEMBER 8 2011
DOORS OPEN AT 7AM
AUDITIONS FROM 8 AM TO 1 PM
MADONNA'S CELEBRITY CHOREOGRAPHERS RICH & TONE WILL JUDGE YOUR AUDITION!
