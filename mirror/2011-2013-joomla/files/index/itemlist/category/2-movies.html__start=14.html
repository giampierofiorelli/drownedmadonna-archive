Andy LeCompte tells how he dressed Madonna's hair for the Golden Globes

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Los Angeles Times gave us in interesting eye on Madonna’s easy, beachy waves last night at the Golden Globes ceremony. The newspaper talked with Madonna's long-time hair dresser Andy LeCompte, who dressed her hair for the Golden Globes as well.

 

“She wanted easy, natural texture,” says Andy LeCompte. He started by rough drying the hair, manipulating and emphasizing her natural texture. He applied a mist of Wella Thermal Heat Protection spray to protect the hair from heat as well as add even more texture. For the loose waves, LeCompte used his Phi platform 1-inch flat iron to make soft, “s” formations throughout the hair. Finally, to create some piecey-ness, he used a mist of Wella’s Ocean Spritz to break up the waves and give the hair an overall tousled, relaxed look.

Elton John's husband David Furnish lashed out at Madonna following her victory speech at the Golden Globes last night. Moments after Madonna won the award for Masterpiece as Best Original Song, David Furnish went to his Facebook page and wrote:

 

"Madonna. Best song???? F**k off!!!"

 

And then he went on, posting:

This mention of Lady Gaga, when Madonna actually never criticized her, is the proof that there's something else besides the wickedness of Elton John and his husband.
