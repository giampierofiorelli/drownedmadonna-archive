Latest News on Madonna's New Album and First Single, the Super Bowl performance and a ballad for W.E.

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


The first single, described similar to a cheerleader song, from the upcoming Madonna's album has already been chosen, as MadonnaTribe.com reports. The track features two vocal guests, Nicki Minaj, who expressed her wishes to work with Madonna, and MIA, who lately attended the UK premiere of W.E. Some other tracks on the new album are featuring them. The main producers are William Orbit, Benni Benassi, who introduced Jean Baptiste Kouame to Orbit, and Martin Solveig.

According to this accurate news, the first single will be one of the five songs that Madonna will perform at next year's Super Bowl, which is now definitely a go and will give so much attention to Madonna and her new musical fatigue. Madonna is expected to perform with three music guests, and MIA and Nicki Minaj are supposedly two of them.

More news on Madonna's new album is that photographers Mart and Marcus will shoot the cover. The photoshot is apparently happening towards the end of the year. They recently did the campaign for Madonna's True or Dare perfume out next year.

We are going to have a brand new Madonna's ballad for W.E. An additional reason to go and watch this beautiful movie! The track may also be included on Madonna's new album, but it is not sure at this stage.
