Madonna talks about the creative process of her Super Bowl performance

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


In an interview with ABC's Cynthia McFadden, Madonna said she's in the process of planning her Super Bowl Half Time show and auditioning dancers to perform with her at Indianapolis Lucas Oil Stadium.

"It's quite a daunting task because I have, you know, in the middle of this very important event in Americans' lives, I have to put  on the greatest show on Earth," Madonna said.

Madonna said she's had a lot of creative ideas about how to make her show unique, but not all are feasible. "I actually wanted to have 100 drummers come from the ceiling, (but) it can't take the weight," she said.

"I have ideas. I get like, 'Oh my God, this is amazing.' And then like, 'No, that's not going to work,'" Madonna said. "So it's forcing me to be creative with a lot of various restrictions."
