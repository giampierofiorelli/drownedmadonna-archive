Nile Rodgers: 'I love Madonna! I apologized for the drug accident in her house'

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


To promote his new book, Nile Rodgers are giving a few interviews to the press. Nile Rodger worked with Madonna on her great second album, Like a Virgin.

 

US Weekly asked Nile to says how was Madonna before she was big... how is the real Madonna. "I love Madonna! If you want to see the Madonna I know, just go on YouTube and you'll see those early interviews before the record came out," Nile says. "She was giddy and wonderful and giggly and happy and so excited looking towards the future. Every now and them she'd put on the puff mask of the super businesswoman, and most of the time we were laughing and joking and making fun of each other. We were as close as I've ever been to any woman, without being romantically involved. We were really close and I think that it's because we finished the record in a relatively short period of time but we had to wait for months before the record was put out. And that's when Madonna and I really spent more time, was after."

 

US Weekly also asked Nile about the incident where he was in Madonna's house and he's at the big drug breakdown in her bathroom. "She's notoriously anti-drug. Did you ever discuss it with her? Did you ever apologize?" asks Us. "Yeah I did," confesses Nile. "The incident that you are referring to is when she had a birthday party at her house in Miami Beach. I was on a three-day binge and up all the time partying, South Beach was just so amazing to me I didn't even want to go to sleep. I ran into Madonna at a club called Liquid and she invited me over for her birthday party, which was completely by accident. My behavior, which I don't remember at all, was not so cool. I am almost positive that I was the last person to leave. And I was carried home and it was really ugly. I was so embarrassed I didn't even talk to Madonna for a long time. And then when I saw her and I apologized, she looked at me and she said, "Oh no, you were just a little drunk." I was like, "Here I was ready to have a big apology. A little drunk? Do you know I was so embarrassed I haven't had another drink or drug in my life? It was hardly a little drunk." But to her it was nothing. She's probably seen a lot worse. But thank god it all turned out just fine and when I look back upon it, it was completely embarrassing, way out of character for me and it was a resetting of my artistic and moral compass. Honestly, I haven't touched drink or drug again and that was like 17 years ago."

Kris Thykier: 'Madonna works harder than anyone I've met'

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Film producer Kris Thykier is behind two of the year's biggest Brit flicks: The Debt and Madonna's W.E.

 

"Madonna works harder than anyone I've met," Kris Thykier told GQ. "You don't always know what you're getting with a big name, but she was the last one on set [of her film W.E.] every night and kept the crew going when they were exhausted."

 

Being into the film industry, Thykier handled the marketing of some fantastic films, such as Four Weddings And A Funeral and Lock, Stock And Two Smoking Barrels directed by Madonna's former husband Guy Ritchie.

Oscar Isaac on Madonna and the W.E. Premiere at the Venice Film Festival

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Interviewed by Theater Mania, W.E. actor Oscar Isaac talks about Madonna and the premiere of W.E. in Venice for the Film Festival.

 

"It was great. I had never been to Venice," says Oscar. "The Italians and Madonna are really a cool mix, and it was great to see the film for the first time with such a warm audience."

Oscar also talks about having Madonna as a director. "She was intense, she was really determined, and she has an amazing wealth of knowledge about art and aesthetics, so it was easy to trust her," he says. "What's really great about her is that she expects the best of everyone and won't settle for mediocrity. I had to learn to play piano for this role, and she made sure I went to rehearsal every day."

"I found it refreshing to work with someone who's into every minute detail," Oscar adds.

Lourdes Leon and Kelly Osbourne talk about Madonna and Material Girl Brand

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


While celebrating the first birthday of Material Girl brand at Macy's in New York, Madonna's daughter Lourdes Leon and the brand's face Kelly Osbourne talked with People magazine.

 

"I never thought it would get this big and it makes me really proud, and my mom [Madonna] is really proud, too," Lourdes said.

When asked about her style icons, "definitely Kelly [Osbourne], and my mom definitely inspires me," Lourdes told People. "She’s just crazy when it comes to her fashion sense."

"My mom doesn't fit into my shoes, so sucks for her," Lourdes joked. "She's like, 'Oh damn, this doesn't fit on my feet.' I'm like, 'Oh really? That's too bad!'"

But Lola is happy to take advice from Madonna. "We sort of balance each other out. I feel like I could work on it, and she does the same for me. We both give each other fashion tips."

 

Kelly Osbourne talked about Madonna as well. "I actually learned a lot from my mother and growing up watching Madonna."

"[Lourdes] and Madonna do everything and that really reflects in the styles you see coming down the catwalk … it makes me really proud as well, because the line just keeps getting better and better," Kelly Osbourne told People.

Liza Minelli: 'Madonna is terrific, smart at hell, always interesting and interested!'

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Liza Minelli - one of the biggest Divas and best voices of all times - expresses her excellent opinion of Madonna.

 

In a interview given to the Daily Mail, Liza says that she thinks that Madonna is "terrific. When I'm chatting to her, she's just normal, the opposite of grand. We'll meet in a restaurant with friends. Or hang out at a party. She's always interesting and interested."

"She's smart as hell and keeps her eyes peeled," Liza adds. "She misses nothing."

 

Talking about Lady Gaga, the "New York, New York" singer says that "she's into the Madonna thing: shock value. And it works. But the girl has a big talent."

Even though we pointed out that Gaga is used to re-invent what Madonna has already done, we agree with Liza Minelli and think Miss Germanotta is talented and smart, as well as she is the best Madonna's 'wannabie' of all times. Lady Gaga is the only singer that, besides Madonna, is not standardized nowadays. The problem is that Madonna's fans saw it all before and in years when it was harder, pregnant and extraordinary.

 

"Gaga and Madonna have realised the value of shock," goes on Liza Minelli. "But why not? You need to do anything you can in this business to get noticed. And they’ve both done it brilliantly."

 

Like Madonna and Lady Gaga, Liza Minelli is of Italian origin.
