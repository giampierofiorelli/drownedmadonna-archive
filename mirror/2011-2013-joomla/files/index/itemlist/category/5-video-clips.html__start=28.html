More Details on the Music Video of Madonna's Give Me All Your Love

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


BREAKING EXCLUSIVE NEWS - DrownedMadonna.com heard more information in detail on the music video of Give Me All Your Love, the first single from Madonna's new album.

 

There were dance auditions for Madonna's Give Me All Your Love on Weds November 30, Thurs December 1th and December 2nd. Dancers rehearsed on last Saturday, December 3 and they will rehearse again on December 5 and 6. Dancers will shoots will take place in New York City on December 7 and 8th.

 

The Production Company is The Director's Bureau, which manages Megaforce, the French collective that will direct Madonna's new video, as we previously revealed.

 

They were looking for:

  • MALE DANCERS, 20-30 years old, any ethnicity, who can portray dancing FOOTBALL PLAYERS.
  • MALE EXTRAS, 20-30 y/o, any ethnicity, who can portray FOOTBALL PLAYERS extras.
  • STUNTMEN, 20-30, any ethnicity, to do football playing stunts, dives and jumps.
  • MALE BODY BUILDERS, 20-30 y/o, who are real life BODY-BUILDERS.
  • GANGSTER MALES, 20-30 y/o, Caucasian.
  • FEMALE DANCERS, 20-30 y/o, any ethnicity who can portray dancing CHEERLEADERS.
  • FEMALE BODY DOUBLE, Caucasian, 20-40 y/o, Fit, 5 feet 4, dress 2-4, shoe 8 prefer light, honey, or strawberry blond long hair.
  • FEMALE STUNT DOUBLE, Caucasian, 20-40 y/O, Fit, 5 feet 4, dress 2-4, shoe 8 prefer light, honey, or strawberry blond long hair.

 

Thanks to Julian.

Megaforce to direct Madonna Give Me All Your Love music video

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


EXCLUSIVE BREAKING NEWS - DrownedMadonna.com heard some great news about the music video of Give Me All Your Love, the first single from Madonna's upcoming album.

It's very likely now that Give Me All Your Love video is going to be directed by Megaforce, a very talented and cutting-edge French collective with a wide variety of backgrounds in graphic arts, design and even advertising. They are not very popular yet, but they already directed a few award winning music videos and stunning commercials. Kid Cudi's Pursuit of Happiness is their most famous music video.

Megaforce trademark is their surreal, zero gravity camera trickery. They mostly use hand held camera.


Give Me All Your Love video to be filmed in New York is going to be very funny and will involve 50 and more dancers. Megaforce profound talent will give us a stunning video! We remind you that Nicki Minaj and M.I.A. will be on the video, as we at DrownedMadonna.com lately revealed.


We remind you that it's not 100% confirmed yet, and things can change till the last minute with Madonna!

 

Watch their controversial video for Is Tropical's The Greeks, which went viral last summer. Also have a look at Kid Cudi's Pursuit of Happiness.
