Madonna: 'I will direct more movies, but now I need to make a record!'

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


International press is absolutely enthusiastic about last night screening of W.E. at MoMa - Museum of Modern Art - in New York. Madonna's beauty and timeless charm - marvellously Roberto Cavalli dressed complete with Van Cleef & Arpels white gold and diamond earrings - bewitched everybody. We have to confess that the Italian fashion designer created her best cloth ever of the W.E. era so far. Madonna was gorgeous!

 

The Associated Press asked Madonna is she wants to direct more movies. "Yes I do, but I need to make a record," said Madonna. "I will, I will!" Watch the clip.

 

 

Have a look at this nice Hollywood.tv coverage from the red carpet.

 

 

During his introduction, Harvey Weinstein said, "If Joe Smith had made the film it would still be heralded," to which Madonna politely replied, "Thank you, Harvey, but I would never want to be Joe Smith." Not hardly.

Later, as MoMA PS1's Klaus Biesenbach walked into the Piaget-sponsored after-party at Crown, he asked Weinstein, "Are you happy?" Weinstein's response: "Yes, Patti Smith got up and gave her a standing ovation."

 

if Madonna could have owned any of Wallis Simpson's accessories, she would have "the cross bracelet," she told FashionEtc.

 

Andrea Riseborough keeps on praising Madonna as a director. "She was wonderful to work with," said Andrea.

Madonna's W.E. not postponed in the UK. Tentative release date for the Soundtrack

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


As already reported, the release date of Madonna's movie W.E. has been postponed in the USA from December 9, 2011 to February 3, 2012.

While this postponement has an effect in the Netherlands, where the movie will be released on March 15, 2012 instead of December 22, 2011, this does not effect the UK release date at all, DrownedMadonna.com learnt.

 

The W.E. original soundtrack will most likely be released nearer to the release date of the movie in the UK DrownedMadonna.com heard. But no definitive news are available since they are still working on it these days.

New Official W.E. photographs to be exhibited in London

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


New photographs of Madonna's film W.E. are to go on show at Chelsea's Little Black Gallery. The stills by young Brazilian photographer Anthony Souza show actors Andrea Riseborough and James D'Arcy playing the famous couple.

"I was visually inspired by the sets and costumes, and the romanticism and visual elegance of the scenes, which made me approach the photos as artistic works and not just publicity stills," Sousa says.

 

Anthony Souza is the official W.E. still photographer. He was born in Rio de Janeiro, and grew up in Italy and England. He studied photography at Cambridge Arts & Sciences and Camberwell University.
