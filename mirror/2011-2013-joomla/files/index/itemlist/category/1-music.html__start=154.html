Madonna New Album and Single: More details

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


DrownedMadonna.com heard some more juicy news on Madonna's new album and the first single.

 

Martin Solveig did an alternate single mix, a dub and a club mix of Madonna's Gimme All Your Luvin. While both Stuart Price and William Orbit have mixes with Madonna-only vocals, though no confirmation of release has been given yet.

 

We also learnt that William Orbit has several mixes of the tracks from Madonna's new album, but it is still unknown which ones will be released.

 

The album is definitely her best according to our sources. It's described as a combination of Ray of Light and Music, only better. There are lots of beats in every track, even the ballads have bass!

 

A press release with album and single info - including remixes and single artwork - will be out soon, tentatively on the week of the 16th.

 

The Digital Download of the "Single Version" of Gimme All Your Luvin will be made available the same day as radio date.

Last but not least, as for now, DrownedMadonna.com has been told that William Orbit has a new remix of  Madonna's Ray Of Light, but we can not say to what purpose.

Madonna furious with her Staff for all the leaks

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


According to The Sun, Madonna is conducting her own Sherlock Holmes-style investigation into a costly "leak" in her camp. Some of her associates are under the spotlight for flogging music, video footage and sensitive pictures behind her back. It all kicked off before Christmas, when a Spanish super-fan was arrested for leaking the demo version of Gimme All Your Luvin. "Her camp were aware of the Spanish character because he was an annoying super-fan," A source told The Sun. "They were on to him for actively leaking and selling new and old unreleased tracks online."

 

But now that he has been arrested "there's a much bigger picture," adds The Sun. "The source of these leaks comes from within Madonna's camp. Single tracks have been exchanging hands for well over $1,000. Hundreds of hours of Madonna footage have been obtained illegally for tens of thousand of dollars."

 

 

The UK newspaper writes that "it would have to be someone close to get access to that kind of material. It's clear a serious betrayal has been going on."
Hundreds of unflattering, un-Photoshopped pics of her also leaked in the last year. The source added: "It is all part of the same deal made by a few unscrupulous characters to line their pockets."

Madonna topped the Top 100 World's Greatest Party Classics!

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


UK radio chanel Magic 105.4 launched an online survey to find the world's greatest party classics and the final Top 100 was revealed yesterday night to celebrate the New Year's Eve.

It's impressive to see that three Madonna hits spotted at number 1, number 2 and even number 3!

 

  1. Madonna - Holiday
  2. Madonna - Hung Up
  3. Madonna - Into The Groove
Madonna is followed by Bee Gees with Stayin' Alive, Chaka Khan, popping up twice with Ain't Nobody and I'm Every Woman, and Michael Jackson with Billie Jean. Bananarama's cover of Venus, and Kylie Minogue's Can't Get You Out of My Head also make the list. The list also features tracks by Britney Spears to Queen, Abba and Shania Twain.
