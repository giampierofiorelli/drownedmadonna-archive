Madonna interested to buy a penthouse in Verona

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


While Madonna was about to arrive in Venice for this year's film festival, several Italian newspapers and magazine claimed that she was interested in one of the Palladian Villas of the Veneto on the surrounding of Vicenza, or a house in Venice. But, talking with WWD, Madonna said that these rumours are "not true. Every time I go somewhere, they say that, but enough houses."

 

Today new facts have emerged. Reportedly, Madonna would be very interested in an amazing old penthouse at Piazza Erbe in Verona, Italy, the city of Romeo and Juliet. "I confirm that the American popstar is interested in it, but I cannot add anything else," Real Estate Entrepreneur Alessandro Proto told Verona's paper L'Arena, who also sold a house to Leonardo di Caprio and the celebrity couple Pitt-Jolie.

 

Business negotiations are still under way, according to the Italian entrepreneur. At this point, Madonna would have seen only pictures of the prestigious house, while her entourage is expected to visit it soon.

 

The grand penthouse is a five-hundred-square-metre duplex apartment with a loft, 6 bedrooms with fireplace, 5 bathrooms, a kitchen, a study, and a large hall with a panoramic view. It is furnished with antique furniture and modern design items. It has a back entrance perfect for Madonna's privacy.

Madonna learning French for her lover Brahim Zaibat?

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


It is time to post some succulent gossip. As usual, take it with a grain of salt.

 

According to the Mirror, Madonna has hired a tutor to teach her French, the so-called language of love, besides being the first language of her partner, dancer Brahim Zaibat.

"Madonna is working on her album, but she is squeezing in French lessons too," a source is alleged to have told to the Mirror. "Her boyfriend is impressed, he thinks it's sweet."

 

"She's looking at properties in France," the source allegedly added.

 

We remind you that Madonna lived in Paris for a few time before she became a super-star, while working with Patrick Hernandez, and she has always been fascinated by French and French artists. In her latest documentary, I'm Going to Tell You a Secret, Madonna was filmed asking her daughter Lourdes to help her to learn some French lines for her show in Paris. Lola attended a French school in London and she is an astonishingly good French speaker.

Madonna feels fat

Written by Vincenzo Ruocco
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Speaking at the Met Ball in New York this week Madonna, wearing this figure-hugging dress by British designer Stella McCartney, told astonished red carpet reporters: "I feel fat.”

The 52-year-old star has always taken pride in her looks and follows a strict diet to keep her in top shape for her rigorous touring schedule but her admission that she’s sometimes unhappy with her body image shows that she could be human after all.
