Martin Solveig to play at Madonna & Smirnoff Dance Competition Party

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Madonna has hand picked DJ and producer Martin Solveig - who has been collaborating with Madonna in the studio for her upcoming album - to headline the Dance Competition Party in New York on November 12th in collaboration with Smirnoff. As we lately revealed, the event will take place at Roseland Ballroom.

Hardy "Indigo" Muanza and Vikter Duplaix will also be spinning custom mixes. While Shay Normann is the artistic director and choreographer for the event, and the eleven finalists have also been working with Madonna's long-time choreographers Rich and Tone Talauega.

 

"Even with my crazy schedule, any chance I get to be on a dance floor in New York with fabulous music and incredible dancers is still my idea of a perfect evening. I can't wait for November 12th," said Madonna.

 

Stay tuned to DrownedMadonna.com for more.
