Latest on Madonna's Give Me All Your Luvin', the album, the tour and the Super Bowl

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


The title of Madonna's upcoming single has changed again. It will be definitely called Give Me All Your Luvin', as reported by Madonnatribe. The single will debut to the radio and will be available as digital download on February 3, as already reported. But there will be a special preview the day before.

 

The same day (February 2), Madonna will attend a special Super Bowl Press Conference where she will discuss detail of the biggest show of the year. This will be just the start of a month full of surprises and exciting announcements, as also William Orbit teased on his Facebook.

 

M.D.N.A. is currently set to be the album in Madonna's career with the longest running time. It will feature a dozen of songs on the regular CD with a few more to be added on the special edition packaging. On the promo side, Madonnatribe reports that Madonna will make the cover story of The Advocate and will be featured in People magazine with an extensive article, even although she won't be the cover girl.

 

The official announcement of Madonna's next world tour is taking place two days after the Super Bowl, on February 7, with tickets for the first announced concerts going on sale immediately afterwards. We remind you that Madonna will launch her tour in Israel at the end of May, as DrownedMadonna.com revealed a few months ago. Expect it to be her longest tour ever.
