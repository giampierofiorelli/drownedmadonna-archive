Madonna stalker escapes hospital

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Los Angeles police are asking for the public's help in locating a man convicted of threatening to kill Madonna who walked away from a Los Angeles-area mental hospital last week. The LAPD say Robert Dewey Hoskins left the state hospital Friday. He had been committed there last year.

The 54-year-old is "highly psychotic" and can be extremely violent and should not be approached by members of the public, police said in a news release today (February 9). They urged anyone who sees him to call authorities immediately.

Hoskins served a 10-year prison sentence for stalking and threatening Madonna after being convicted by a jury in 1996. The singer reluctantly testified against the man, who was shot by her personal security after he jumped the fence of her Hollywood Hills home.

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Sources at the Tel Aviv Kabballah Center claim that Madonna is due to land in Israel this weekend for a short and personal visit. The reason is thatshe wants to take part in the celebrations of her favorite Rabbi, Michael Berg, whose son is having a Bar Mitzvah ceremony in Beit Shmuel, located in the heart of Jerusalem. Apparently, Madonna is planning to stay for a few hours and come back to the US, where she is rehearsing for the Super Bowl. The source at the Kabbalah Center told Israeli news site Walla that they are already preparing for Madonna’s arrival. "It’s still not 100% sure that she’s arriving, but by the looks of the preparation of the Kabbalah Center’s stuff, it looks like it’s going to happen."

"It’s going to be a last minute decision," a Los Angeles told AbbaNibi.com, "Madonna is very busy with her Super Bowl performance, which occurs a week after. Madonna DOES feel very close to Rabbi Berg and wishes him Mazal Tov whether she’ll be there in person or not."

 

Whether Madonna arrives in Israel or not, at least one member of the Berg family will watch Madonna's Super Bowl performance from up close, as Rabbi Yehuda Berg tweeted that he's going to be there.

Madonna denies rumors that she wants to buy one of the Palladian Villas of the Veneto in the surround area of Vicenza

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


When Madonna was about to arrive in Venice to premiere her movie W.E. at the Film Festival 2011, Italian press started to report that the diva wanted to buy one of the Palladian Villas of the Veneto on the surrounding of Vicenza, Italy. These villas, designed by Italian architect Andrea Palladio, are inscribed on UNESCO World Heritage List since 1994.

But, talking with WWD, Madonna said that these rumours are "not true."

"Every time I go somewhere, they say that, but enough houses,” she added.

 

This is not the first time Madonna is reportedly looking for a villa in Italy.

When Madonna was last months pregnant of Rocco, she spent a few weeks in Italy. First she went to Pantelleria Island, South of Italy, as a guest of Italian photographer Fabrizio Ferri, who took some intimate pictures of the pregnant star. Then she moved in Tuscany at Sting country-house. Finally, in all secrecy, she rented a car and drove to Umbria, Central Italy. She went to the Massa Martana country-side. Paparazzi got the scoop when she was already there. Reportedly she booked the country-side of Italian celebrity Marina Ripa di Meana, who was one of the guest stars on the W.E. red carpet in Venice. According to Marina Ripa di Meana, Madonna booked her house under an alias, and, when she was staying there, she expressed the wish to buy it, since she was looking for a villa in Umbria or Tuscany. But Marina requested Madonna to do a free concert in Umbria, to promote her battle against fur coats, as a clause. At the end, as known, there was any concert in Umbria neither Madonna bought Marina Ripa di Meana country-house in Massa Martana surrounding.
