Super Bowl to be streamed online and to mobile phones

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


The Super Bowl will be streamed online and to mobile phones in the U.S. for the first time, the NFL announced today. The Super Bowl will be available on the league's and network's websites and through Verizon's NFL Mobile app. The service will include additional camera angles.

"With such a big television audience, it will be interesting to see the expanded reach," Hans Schroeder, the NFL's senior vice president of media strategy and development, told The Associated Press.

The NFL and NBC will do extensive research to find out exactly how many people are watching the streams and how they're using them.

A reader of our dear friend Matthew Rettenmund's BoyCulture has some details of today's just-wrapped Madonna episode of Anderson Cooper, we recently announced:

 

Madonna looked stunning in an all-leather dress with huge bow that squeeked into the mic the entire hour. Huge leather heels, pretty gold jewelry...She seemed to be having a good time, laughing, kidding about Anderson's suit, singing little songs about how they passed time on the set. Her publicist came into the picture once to fix Madonna's pose so she wouldn't flash the camera. Andrea Riseborough was the only (other) star there, but hardly got any attention. I don't think this will air until the week before the Super Bowl.
