Lady Gaga: 'I love Madonna's Give Me All Your Love. There's no problem between us'

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Lady Gaga wanted to clear up that her supposed feud with the Queen of Pop Madonna is absolutely false. "Madonna is a wonderful ­influence on me," Lady Gaga told the Mirror. "I feel blessed to have grown up with a ­powerful, blonde woman to show us the ropes. It’s all down to her that I’m able to do what I do."

 

"There's no problem between us," Gaga added.

 

"I really like her new single too. The one that was leaked," Lady Gaga confessed talking about the demo of Madonna's "Give Me All Your Love".

Lady Gaga reinvents Madonna's Cherish video for her HAUS OF Ü / YOÜ AND I

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Here we go again. Lady Gaga keeps on taking from Madonna's 'portfolio', and lately, since Born This Way, everything is so connected with her Like a Prayer era.

This time she reinvented - someone would say copied - Madonna's Cherish video directed by Herb Ritts. The mermaid, the choreography, the photography, the black and white: everything reminds to Cherish and Madonna.

 

Have a look of the new video, the 3rd film, titled HAUS OF Ü ft. YÜYI, for her YOÜ AND I track.
