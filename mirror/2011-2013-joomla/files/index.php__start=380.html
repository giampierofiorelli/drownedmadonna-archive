Madonna has hired pop star Rihanna as the face of her coconut water drinks company.

The pop legend is a leading investor in the Vita Coco brand, along with Red Hot Chili Peppers frontman Anthony Kiedis, and she has now asked the Umbrella hitmaker to be the face of the drink.

A statement from Madonna reads, "Rihanna is a natural fit for the Vita Coco brand and I’m so happy to have her on the team, " while Rihanna adds, "I love Vita Coco. It’s real coconut water from hand-picked coconuts. It’s delicious and so good for you!”

Madonna's Fire-Damaged Childhood Home On Sale

Written by Vincenzo Ruocco
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


contactmusic.com

Madonna's childhood home in Michigan has been placed on the market for a paltry $48,600 (£30,375).

The pop superstar spent her youth in the four-bedroom Rochester Hills house with her parents and six brothers and sisters before relocating to New York in her search for fame and fortune.

Her family reportedly sold the property in 2001 for $270,000 (£168,750), but the home was ravaged in a fire in 2008 and has not been restored since, according to real estate blog Curbed.com.

In Vogue with Madonna

Tweet
Share on Tumblr


mcv.gaynewsnetwork.com.au

“I was infected with Madonna at an early age,” says Michael Griffiths with a laugh. “She came along at almost exactly the right time for me. I was in primary school when she started to make it big, and I don’t think Vale Park Primary School has ever recovered from my performances of 'Holiday' at recess time! As I got older I made it my business to know everything about her, and when it was announced that The Girlie Show was coming to Adelaide I knew I’d get there somehow. I was one of the 40,000 at Adelaide Oval and I’ll never forget it.

“My partner and I have decided that we’re going to go and see her next tour, somewhere in the world, and I wish they’d get on and announce the dates!”
