The first news we have got about Madonna's next world tour are included in the details of Madonna's collaboration with Smirnoff Nightlife Project.

 

The tour is planned for 2012 with rehearsals expected to start in the first quarter of 2012, probably in March. Dancers must have at least twelve months validity on their passport from March 2012.

 

Countries/continents which may be included in Madonna world tour 2012 are USA, South America, Canada, Europe, Australia and New Zealand.

 

  • UPDATE (7 October, 2011): According to always accurate and reliable Italian newspaper La Repubblica, Madonna will perform in Milan on June 14, 2012. Reportedly, the concert is taking place at Milan's San Siro Stadium. The stadium has been already booked.
  • UPDATE (9 November, 2011): Click here to read the first European dates that are confirmed at the moment. The concert in Milan on June 14 is one of them.
  • UPDATE (12 November, 2011): Dancer Lil' Buck is the winner of the Dance4Madonna contest in collaboration with Smirnoff., which took place at Roseland Ballroom in New York City on November 12. He will be touring with Madonna.
  • UPDATE (22 November, 2011): DrownedMadonna.com heard that Madonna will launch her new tour in Tel Aviv, Israel, on May 31th, 2012. Read more.
