The first single, described similar to a cheerleader song, from the upcoming Madonna's album has already been chosen, as MadonnaTribe.com reports. Madonna & Lady Gaga, Madonna & her fans: What really happened at the UK Premiere of W.E.

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


The ironic Madonna's exclamation "Lady Who?" on Lady Gaga made the headlines here at DrownedMadonna.com as well as at PerezHilton.com and a few other Madonna's fansites. But it looks like it was completely misunderstood; Madonna did not mean to dish Gaga. Now Perez Hilton's sources can tell what really happened:

 

"Someone in balcony screamed Lady Gaga just as she took her seat…Madonna said, Lady Who? in a totally fun way. It was so harmless and lighthearted. It isn’t madonna’s style to dis people in the middle of the premiere of her film. She has never been anything but supportive of Gaga in the media."

 

Madonna has also been attacked for snubbing her fans. While posting an Associated Press release, a certain UK tabloid also included a comment regarding Madonna's fans booeing her for this reasons. Here at DrownedMadonna.com showed that it was not true. The most of the international media followed what The Telegraph posted.

Now also Perez Hilton is defending her posting the truth. His sources reports that:

 

"There were l5,0­00 fans there. Madonna went over to a bunch of disabled fans the second she got out of the car. She signed all their autographs. She then missed one of them and her makeup artist ran over to Madonna during an interview so that could be signed. She went over to several areas and happily signed autographs. She certainly could not have signed everyone’s but she was very gracious. She went back and forth from fans to tv interviews and was again very generous in talking to all the crews and the media. Then it got very wild on the carpet and her security moved her forward and a few – and I swear very few people went ohhhhhh – not even a single boo. The film got a standing ovation at the end of the movie.. At film festivals people walk out if they don’t like the movie. The response was very very strong."

DrownedMadonna.com exclusive interview with Monte Pittman

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW - Madonna's fans knows him for being her guitar teacher as well as her guitarist since the promotion for the "Music" album back on 2000. But Monte Pittman first of all is a great talent, a musician and singer who has just released his second own album, "Pain, Love & Destiny" that you can buy here.

 

 

Talking about the writing sessions of "Easy Ride," one of DrownedMadonna.com Team favourite songs, Monte Pittman said that "it came about by being a chord progression you can play the Harmonic Minor scale over."

 

We asked Monte if he is planning to tour with Madonna next year. "If she’s playing somewhere and asks me to join her – I’m there," he said. "She gave me my break. She comes first."
