In Vogue with Madonna

Written by Vincenzo Ruocco

mcv.gaynewsnetwork.com.au

“I was infected with Madonna at an early age,” says Michael Griffiths with a laugh. “She came along at almost exactly the right time for me. I was in primary school when she started to make it big, and I don’t think Vale Park Primary School has ever recovered from my performances of 'Holiday' at recess time! As I got older I made it my business to know everything about her, and when it was announced that The Girlie Show was coming to Adelaide I knew I’d get there somehow. I was one of the 40,000 at Adelaide Oval and I’ll never forget it.

“My partner and I have decided that we’re going to go and see her next tour, somewhere in the world, and I wish they’d get on and announce the dates!”

Madonna responds to former employees’ lawsuit in Malawi

Written by Vincenzo Ruocco

Madonna’s legal people have filed papers with the Malawi court in response to a lawsuit by eight former employees of her abandoned Raising Malawi Academy For Girls venture, who are suing for unfair dismissal. The eight former staff members were let go after the RMAG project, funded by Madonna’s Raising Malawi charity, was halted in January amid allegations of mismanagement and money wasting.

Princess steps out in Madonna T-shirt

Written by Vincenzo Ruocco

from dailymail.co.uk

With tracks such as Mysterious Girl and Behind Closed Doors under his belt, Peter Andre is considered something of a musical legend by many in the pop industry.

And it seems the 38-year-old star is working hard to pass on his musical knowledge to his children, judging by pictures of the family out in Cyprus today.

While his daughter Princess, three, stepped out wearing a Madonna printed T-shirt, five-year-old son Junior, showed his allegiance to The Jam in a more masculine top.

Madonna feels fat

Written by Vincenzo Ruocco

Speaking at the Met Ball in New York this week Madonna, wearing this figure-hugging dress by British designer Stella McCartney, told astonished red carpet reporters: "I feel fat.”

The 52-year-old star has always taken pride in her looks and follows a strict diet to keep her in top shape for her rigorous touring schedule but her admission that she’s sometimes unhappy with her body image shows that she could be human after all.
