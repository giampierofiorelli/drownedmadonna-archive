Madonna turned to Philip Treacy again for the Super Bowl

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Madonna turned to the talents of Irish milliner Philip Treacy for her flamboyant Super Bowl look. Madonna wore an intricate gold headpiece that was custom made to complete her gladiator look. The piece was encrusted with diamonds and based on designs by Riccardo Tisci. It is rumoured to have taken several days to assemble, and has received praise worldwide for its meticulous design.

 

Madonna's association with Philip Treacy goes back further than a decade. In 2001, she caused outrage when she was pictured sporting a €1,200 hat he had made from the pelts of five foxes.

 

Source: Herald.ie.

Madonna describes Girls Gone Wild, and talks about MDNA and her 2012 World Tour

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Talking with Carson Daly, Madonna talked about the Super Bowl, saying that it was very demanding but a sort of "miracle" and supernatural experice. She said that she didn't know about what M.I.A. did with her finger on stage, and that she was kind of upset, because, even if she likes M.I.A. rock attitude, she thinks it was not the right place and the right moment to show a "negative" attitude. Madonna also said that M.I.A. apologized with her, and she accept her apologies.

 

Madonna also talked about her MDNA album, describing the whole thing. Especially she talked about Girls Gone Wild and how diffent it is compared to Give Me All Your Luvin'.

 

Finally Madonna talked about her upcoming 2012 World Tour. She confessed that she imported some people who were part of her Super Bowl Show into her inner circle to help her with the tour. Especially some group of people from Cirque du Soleil and a new group of dancers.

 

Madonna also talked about W.E. and the unexpected Golden Globe for Masterpiece.

It's Official: Girls Gone Wild is the 2nd single from MDNA, Madonna revealed today

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


BREAKING NEWS - Talking with Ryan Seacrest at 102.7 KIIS-FM, Madonna announced that the second single from MDNA will be Girls Gone Wild, co-produced with Benny Benassi.

 

Madonna added that she will film a music video for Girls Gone Wild in a couple of weeks. She is going to have auditions for her 2012 World Tour next week, and she will shoot the video for Girls Gone Wild after that. Asked about the rumour about Britney Spears being featured on the track, Madonna said it's not true. "No, it's just all me," she said.
