Madonna's MDNA launch in Malaysia

Written by Giampiero Fiorelli
Tweet
Share on Tumblr


Universal Music Malaysia in conjunction with the launch MDNA globally on the 26th of March 2012 will offer a pre-order of the album from the 17th of March 2012 to the 25th of March 2012 at participating retail outlets nationwide. Pre order comes with limited edition Give Me All Your luvin’ CD single.

Order a copy of MDNA from Speedy Video, Rock Corner, Victoria Music or Artist Gallery to get yourself a limited edition Give Me All Your Luvin' CD single now! (CD single is made available for pre order only). Available in 1 CD and 2 CDs version.

Madonna fans can rejoice that as the world celebrates the most anticipated album release of the year, Universal Music Malaysia will be organizing a major event to celebrate the album launch on the 31st of March 2012, Saturday. Simply head on down to the front entrance of Pavilion, Kuala Lumpur on the day, fans can enjoy a showcase of everything Madonna that will be held on the day. A Madonna museum featuring models dressed up according to each fashion style from her debut album right up to M.D.N.A (12th). Fans can grab this opportunity to have their photo taken at the museum with the Madonna models and take part in contest’s and win attractive prizes up for grabs.


Fans can even treat themselves to a live performance from each model as they dance to one song from each of Madonna’s albums. The highlight of the show will culminate when all the models will be joined by cheerleaders for a final performance of “Give Me All Your Luvin” taken from her MDNA, reminiscent of her halftime Superbowl performance earlier this year.

Fans who purchase the CD on that day at Speedy Video Pavilion will get a free poster. A portion of MDNA CD sales on that day from the retail outlet will be channeled to a charity body in conjunction with the celebration of Earth Hour on the same day as the Madonna Museum.

Special thanks to Madonna Malaysia Group.
