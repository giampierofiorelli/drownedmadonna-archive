News about Madonna’s I Rise remixes

Giampiero
on June 10, 2019 at 10:22 pm

We at DrownedMadonna.com heard some juicy news about Madonna‘s I Rise remixes.

Plans are to release an I Rise mix by Tracy Young around New York City Gay Pride.

Tracy Young mix is already getting played at her radio station in Miami.

At least another I Rise mix is on production, as well as for Crave.

In the meantime, Medellín went to #4 in the US Dance Chart. It is greatest gainer 2 weeks in a row.
