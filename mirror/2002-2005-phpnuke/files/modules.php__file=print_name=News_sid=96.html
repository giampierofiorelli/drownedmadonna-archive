Mariangela Melato on Madonna again.
Date: Thursday, June 12 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Movies

Mariangela Melato, guest on Taormina Bnl Film Festival, where has been given a prize, issued several statements, in which was strongly hard on Madonna. And this time, exaggerating a little bit. Read more…



Here is an excerpt of another interview she granted:

Mariangela, how could you define a good actor?: 

“A good actor is always a humanly good person. This is maybe the most important thing”.  

And how could you define your colleague Madonna in Swept Away remake? 

“How can I be a humanly good person and talk about that remake? I can’t say what I really think about that movie. But it was good just for one reason, I've revalued myself. I found myself beautiful and erotically wonderful.

How could you define yoursel, behind the set and stage?

“I’m an egocentric person. Yeah, absolutely self - centred. But I get bored even in front of the mirror. I get bored of myself. For this reason, I always wish to change”. 

We understand that.
