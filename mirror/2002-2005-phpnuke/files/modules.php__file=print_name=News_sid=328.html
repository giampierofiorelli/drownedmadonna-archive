The GAP campaign may involve 'The English Roses' promotion.
Date: Saturday, July 12 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: TV

The next GAP advertising campaign will feature Madonna and Missy Elliott. But this is well-known.
Maybe, this will be a "cooperative promotion" which will involve Madonna's "The English Roses". Read more...

The newspaper, magazine and TV campaign is scheduled to run eight weeks, beginning July 27.
"We are in preliminary discussions with Madonna on her new book," Gap spokeswoman Claudia Hawkins told "The San Francisco Chronicle."
What's being discussed is a "cooperative promotion" involving Gap, Madonna and her new book, "The English Roses," about which Gap will share details later this month, Hawkins said.
"The English Roses" it's due for release in 42 languages and 100 countries on Sept. 15, which fits nicely into the second half of Gap's fall ad campaign.
