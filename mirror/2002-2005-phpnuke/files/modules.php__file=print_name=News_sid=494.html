Madonna at VMA’s: her French kiss is the greatest in Music History.
Date: Saturday, August 30 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Awards

So, here she's again, after a long waiting, confirmed just a few hours before the beginning of VMA’s 20th anniversary, Madonna has opened the show on Thursday, August 28, in front of millions of television viewers and a gorges audience of celebrities, exchanging French kisses with pop starts Britney Spears and Christina Aguilera. Read More...



The Queen of Pop performed her classic hit "Like a Virgin" and "Hollywood", new single off of her record American Life, shocking and throwing audience into ecstasies. The performance started with six- year- old Lourdes, daughter of the Icon, walking on stage with a little friend, dressed as flower girls at a wedding ceremony, complete with boy toy buckles. Standing on top of a wedding cake, Britney joined by Aguilera, both dressed in a wedding dress, started to sing Like a Virgin in a homage to Madonna’s version at 1984 VMA’s, and suddenly Madonna came out on top of wedding cake dressed in black, singing Hollywood. Towards the end of performance, Madonna gave Britney a French Kiss, totally shocking the audience, then kissed Aguilera on the lips. The Trio was joined by rapper Missy Elliot before leaving the stage, performing her hit "Work it". The performance left hundreds of fans and celebrities gaping, making it define sensational and unique in all Mtv’s history. After the show Madonna and family missed the party held at famous Four Seasons restaurant, attended by Ingrid Casares, Britney Spears, Nicole Kidman, jetted to Scotland for Stella McCartney’s wedding of the year. But before leaving, Her Majesty finished the night, kissing Eminem goodbye in the audience. The show went on with Eminem himself, who won an award for best video from a movie with "8 Mile", popstars Avril Lavigne, P Diddy, 50 Cent, Kelly Osbourne, Mary J Blige, Coldplay, Ashanti and Beyonce Knowles, but everybody left down by Madonna, who still stole the limelight, on thrilled MTV night.
