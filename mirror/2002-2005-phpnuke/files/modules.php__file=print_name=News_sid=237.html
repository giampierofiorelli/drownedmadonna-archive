The Observer: 'Sexism: Who's Calling the shots?'.
Date: Sunday, June 29 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

Interpreting female images has never been more complicated, writes Joan Smith, who argues that we need to revise some familiar stereotypes. Read more...

Look at Venus and Serena Williams, stars of a new advertising campaign to promote women's tennis and sporting prowess. Look at Demi Moore, stepping out with her children, her ex-husband and her younger lover in a revealing outfit that would probably attract the style label 'hooker chic'. And look at Madonna, promoting her latest CD with a video that apparently shows her having botox injections.

What do these images - all appearing in one week - say about women today? The Williams sisters are celebrating female power and strength, and Ms Moore - who once posed naked and pregnant on the cover of Vanity Fair - is consciously breaking every rule about what middle-aged women should wear.

Is Ms Ciccone doing the same, boldly declaring that women shouldn't be ashamed of the devices they use to maintain their looks? Or is her video a sad comment on the narcissism that wealthy women can afford to indulge?
