Special DVD-Sampler with Madonna.
Date: Sunday, June 08 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

The German magazine "DVD Magazin" includes a Special DVD-Sampler with Music / Live-Performances from Madonna and the following artists: Eric Clapton, The Corrs, A-HA, Cher and many others. It is currently not known what title / live-performance of Madonna will be included. The magazine is available in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
