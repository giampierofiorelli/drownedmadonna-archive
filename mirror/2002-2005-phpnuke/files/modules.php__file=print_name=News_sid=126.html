Gaultier talks about his mission with Madonna.
Date: Monday, June 16 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Interviews

On the occasion of the event “Fashion Motion”, carried out a few days ago at Victoria Albert Museum of London, Jean Paul Gaultier stopped to talk about Madonna, revealing their “famous” mission. Read more…



Jean Paul Gaultier has announced that Madonna “had something to say and she always did it in a smart way”. Besides he’s also revealed, their mission in common, and not only 1990 “Blond Ambition Tour”, was to show to everybody a new powerful woman, haughty and active, not just a boy toy.
