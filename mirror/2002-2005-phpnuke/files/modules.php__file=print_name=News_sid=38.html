New information about “Hollywood” video.
Date: Sunday, June 08 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Video-clip

MTV.com asserts that Madonna has recorded “Hollywood” video-clip on June 2nd and 3th. There is no surprise that shooting took place in Hollywood. During the short, Madonna has changed several looks. According to some rumors of past few days, Madonna would wear different colored wigs.

American channel VH1, part of MTV family, will broadcast "Hollywood, Making The Video" on June 17th.
