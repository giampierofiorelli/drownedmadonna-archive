My Evening With Madonna

~Sighs~

Fourteen years ago a chubby, insecure and painfully shy young man, watched a show called Blonde Ambition on HBO. He sat in awe as he watched Madonna sing and dance her heart out infront of thousands and thousands of screaming fans without missing a beat. Watching that show changed that young man's life immediately and he was never the same again. His dream has been to see Madonna live in concert just once and that dream came true lastnight in New York City.

I have been dizzier than I usually am in anticipation of seeing Madonna on the Re-Invention World Tour; waiting was so hard and time seemed to stand still, even as I sat in Madison Square Garden watching the hords of people start packing into the Garden. I tried composing myself, trying to remain as calm as possible before the show because if I became too excited too soon I would fall over and faint. I wasn't about to faint or cause myself to become ill and miss the moment I have been waiting for, for over fourteen years. I sat calmly just taking in the view of annoying flamers and over the hill women trying to recapture their youth wearing god awful outfits, making themselves out to look like fools; let me digress before I start sounding like Joan rivers. One more thing, can gay men be anymore superficial and shallow? Boys get over yourselves, you aint nothing special.

Finally, the lights begin to flicker and Madison Square Garden is in total darkness; the Queen of Life is about to take the stage. (Thanks to Pud for coining the phrase). We all know that the show opens with the Beast Within so I won't go over that, only to say that it was incredibly mesmerizing and enlightening. I'm starting to feel light headed and shakey, but I took a breath and watched Madonna rise from below onto the stage filled with smoke. And there she was, my nonnie, the woman that shaped me and practicly raised me into becoming the strong, secure and determined man that I am today. My eyes focused soley on her and nothing else that was happening around me or on stage. I just tried to take all of her energy in and breath the same air as my nonnie. My heart was beating right out of my chest, my hands shaking uncontrollably and tears streaming down my face as flashes of my life and what Madonna has meant to me ran through my mind. I remembered Blond Ambition, I remembered dressing up as her for Halloween, I remembered when a man broke my heart and Madonna's music got me through it but most of all I remembered how she has inspired me and has taught me never to give up, the strength she has given me and the light she has shined on me for fourteen amazing years.

Lets fast forward a bit, shall we? We've already read reviews, seen pictures, heard clips and seen videos. There is no need for me to go over verbatum what has already been said. However, here are some personal hightlights. Like a Prayer brought Madison Square Garden to its feet and some to their knees. The crowd was on fire when she went into her classics Burning Up and Material Girl. Certainly the classic hits are what carried this show but that in no way deflates the weight and meaningfulness of her new songs from American Life. American life was performed beautifully and with so much power and force. Yes, the images displayed on the video screens were graphic and disturbing but it got the message accross and we get to see the destruction and the suffering people across the world face each and everday. When you break a nail or your fake tan turns orange,think of those images and you'll hopefully begin to understand that there are more important things in life; it is not just about you or me but all of us all around the world.

The show wasn't all serious and had many light hearted fun moments. Hank Panky, Into the Groove, Music, Hoilday were all light and fun and Madonna enjoying herself and the music.  Madonna looked incredible, her body toned and fit and those sexy arms with the big muscles, to die for and to envy, oh, and those legs, God bless those incredible legs!  Her voice has never sounded better, flawless, strong, emotional and just incredibly moving. She certainly has become a great live singer and puts to shame all those nothings that came way after Madonna.  Madonna is the greatest live performer to ever, past, present, future there will never be anyone like Madonna when it comes to putting on an unforgettable live concert.

All Hail The Queen of Life!

John aka...SexedUpCity.
