Swept Away sequel: Lina Wertmuller talks about Madonna.
Date: Tuesday, July 15 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Movies

The subject of "Swept Away" has come up again. "Playing the leading Role the golden couple in those days, Mariangela Melato and Giancarlo Giannini, and their sons of today", claims Lina Wertmuller. But she tells just a little bit of what is gonna happen in the sequel. “At least against bad luck”, announces Wertmuller, “ you should not talk in advance of what you’re concerned about. And anyway, I have to shoot another movie before”.

Read more to discover what Lina Wertmuller has told, in this occasion, regarding her Movie’s remake, Madonna and her husband Guy Ritchie.



When they asked to Wertmuller if Giancarlo Giannini and Mariangela Melato would have agreed to the sequel, she answered: “ Absolutely, they’re both enthusiastic for it. Mariangela was very disappointed about Madonna’s american remake…. What a pity, she’s so good and smart. Probably her husband Guy Ritchie wasn’t up to his task. But, you know, love is blind. And anyway, that movie was poorly attended, I did not watch it either. I did not want to suffer, making over my rights was a mistake”.

So, Lina Wertmuller has decided not to watch Guy Ritchie’s movie, nor in Italy. When it came out in Usa, Wertmuller claimed she would have wait until its coming out in Italy. In spite of everything, her remarks were intentionally misunderstood, media professed that Lina, after watching the movie, had rejected it as well as Madonna’s performance. Neither her denials were enough to stop their illation.

At present, the umpteenth confirmation of the lies written on magazines. It is the umpteenth proof that Wertmuller continues to value Madonna, even if that remake was a total flop.

However that may be, Lina Wertmuller has not seen the remake directed by Guy Ritchie.
