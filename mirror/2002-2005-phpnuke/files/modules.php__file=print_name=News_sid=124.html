Madonna and The Gay Pride.
Date: Monday, June 16 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Parties

Early in the month of June, in several towns all over the world took place different celebrations dedicated to Gay Pride. Madonna will be a leading role in Usa. Find out how: read more…



On Saturday June 28, two of the most important events: "Saturdays at the Roxy featuring Peter Rauhofer & Manny Lehmann" and "Deep Dark Jungle featuring Victor Calderone", will take place in New York City.

Victor Calderone is going to present for the first time his “Hollywood” remix. Everything enjoyed by a spectacular show with dancers and customs. The number is expected to be wonderful, as moreover all premieres about his Madonna remixes. Besides, Calderone will present a preview of his new record “Deep Dark Jungle".

Peter Rauhofer will introduce a choreographic show for the presentation of his still unpublished remix "Nobody Knows Me". Also, he’s going to present as advance preview two tracks from his new record "Live @ Roxy 2".

At last, Barris Harris of Thunderpuss, is going to attend on Sunday June 29, at Gay Pride party, taking place at Excalibur in Chicago. Without doubt, in this case, one way or another, Madonna will act the leading role.
