Madonna’s Roses raining on 100 countries!
Date: Thursday, September 11 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Books

Selling records as hot cakes and being on every newspaper after kissing on stage teen-pop stars such as Britney Spears and C. Aguilera, doesn’t seem to be enough for ever surprising Madonna! The diva prepares herself for another challenge in publishing history. Far from her last 1992 literary effort (SEX), this time Our Lady of Music addresses to the children all over the world with her forthcoming book “The English Roses"  which will be the first of five books, published by Callaway Editions.

The book comes out Monday (September 15th) in what is expected to be a headline-grabbing release.

What the illustrated storybook series is about...



...is a tightly guarded secret but the content is said to be extremely appealing to people from eight to eighty! Even the picture of the book cover, seen in these days on several sites and newspapers, is not the final version.

Madonna is however poised to make publishing history once again:

"The English Roses" will come out simultaneously in more than 100 countries.  The book will be translated in more than 30 languages.  More than one 1,000,000 copies will hit bookstores around the world, supported by a massive  global marketing which will take Madonna from London to Paris and then to the Oprah Winfrey Show in the United States.

Nevertheless the project is raising eyebrows among publishers and merchants.

Diane Roback, senior children's books editor with Publisher's Weekly, says "Madonna's book is a big question mark, just being a celebrity doesn't guarantee success."  "Children's books is a tough market because it has the most discernible buyer," Applebaum points out, and adds "It's the parent and not the kids buying the book, and adults are very selective about what they buy for their children. Fingers crossed, everybody!
