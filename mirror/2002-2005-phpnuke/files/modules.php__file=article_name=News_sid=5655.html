[WORLD EXCLUSIVE] New Song Title from the Musical revealed!

10,000 Fans Can Be Wrong!

Our exclusive interview with Mike McKnight, Madonna's touring technology wizard since her 1990 Blond Ambition Tour, was a success.  More than 10,000 fans read it and an incredible amount of emails have been submitted to us with a lot of questions to forward to Mike.

So Mike was very kind and happy to give us a second exclusive interview featuring even more interesting info than the previous one. Real surprises that didn't surface before.

The second interview will be online the next days... so stay tuned!

In the meantime, click on Read More to find out an exciting preview with the setlist as of February 19th, 2004 (featuring an incredible surprise) and the reasons why Madonna covered Imagine.



On the previous interview, Mike McKnight revealed DrownedMadonna that they started working on 40 songs (even 60 songs - as he explains to us in the second interview), which became 24 only during the latest 2 weeks of rehearsals. This time he told us how the show was as of February 19th, 2004:

    1. Vogue
    2. Swim
    3. Nobody Knows Me
    4. Express Yourself
    5. American Life (Headcleaner Mix)
    6. Burning Up
    7. Love Profusion (Headcleaner Mix)
    8. I’m So Stupid
    9. Hollywood (VMAs mix)
    10. The Devil Wouldn't Recognise You (new song from the Musical she was working on TBA)
    11. Hanky-Panky
    12. Dress You Up (in E flat)
    13. Take A Bow
    14. Bedtime Stories (musical section to video)
    15. Like A Prayer
    16. Don't Tell Me (promo tour version)
    17. Mother And Father (promo tour version)
    18. Live To Tell (in F)
    19. Into The Groove (GAP version) OR Holiday
    20. Music
    21. Crazy For You (down a step)
    22. Die Another Day

"You can see that things changed pretty radically from that time until we decided on the final set", told Mike.

As you can notice, Swim was replaced by Frozen in the final setlist.

The second section included 5 songs instead of 4. Here comes the first surprise: Love Profusion was rehearsed and included in the setlist from the very first time and to fill in the rock segment it was intended to be performed as the Headcleaner Mix version, included in the "Remixed & Revisited" Ep. Love Profusion was followed by I'm So Stupid, which was later replaced by Dress You Up and finally by Material Girl, as Mike already confirmed to us. But, here comes the second surprise: both Dress You Up and I'm So Stupid were in this setlist in two different sections.

The Carnivale section features the biggest surprise of our world exclusive: a new song from the musical Madonna was working on, titled The Devil Wouldn't Recognise You. More info about this track will be included in the second interview to be published. Moreover, the closing number of this section was Take A Bow and not Lament from Evita soundtrack. 

The Acoustic segment featured 4 songs instead of 5; at first Nothing Fails wasn't intended to be included. Note that at #18 there was Live To Tell. As Mike told us in the previous interview, they didn't spend so much time on this song, since Madonna didn't want to sing it. But this has nothing to do with the reason why Imagine has been included in the setlist. We asked Mike who had the idea of covering this touching John Lennon song. "That was Madonna’s idea," said Mike, "Considering the political climate at that time, she thought it was an important message to get out to the world."

The final section didn't include Papa Don't Preach. Finally, Die Another Day was intended to be the closing number of the show.
