I WENT TO UFG'S LAST SHOWS!

OMG IT WAS FANTASTIC!
I have just got home now, so I can't write too much as I am totally exhausted!
Got tickets for the matinee as well as evening, Row F centre seats and she was the best I had ever seen her!!
At the end of the show, we were waiting for a speech or something, but all that happened was she did a few extra encores, and gave all the cast a kiss.
The crowd were going wild, and then she came on by herself, and I have never seen her look so happy!
The rest of the cast came back on, and she totally lost it and burst into tears!!
It was so emotional and I was nearly crying with her...
She acknowledged all the fans screaming for her, said thankyou and she was crying so much....
I managed to get around to the back where there must have been at least 1000 people waiting to see her, police everywhere, complete madness and mayhem, very scary.
She walked out with Guy, and for the 1st time, smiled and waved at the crowd, still looking so happy!
There was a moment during the play when she does the little dance and sings, she did the first bit, and before she had finished, the crowd starting cheering, so she had to stop! She smiled and slipped out of character, so the audience went wild again, so she still couldn't continue, then, IMO she looked a bit pissed off and said to the crowd something like 'Can you let me do the fucking play now please', gave a little smile and carried on with the rest of the show...
She was so beautiful at the end, and I have never seen her look so genuinely moved by the reaction from the crowd.

I had Jean Paul Gaultier sat two seats behind me, and Rupert Everett to my left and three seats back!
Sorry for not writing more, but I am so tired and really sad that it's all over…

I will be back online later, after some sleep!!

Hi!

Some more details for you!

Forgot to say that in the matinee I was 2 seats behind Trudie Styler (who was in the front row centre) and someone else who looked familiar but don't know who!

I am so depressed now I'm home and it's all over..
Jean Paul Gaultier was great, signing autographs all the way through the interval (he's quite big in real life, very tall!)
Rupert was sat on an aisle seat on the lefthand side of the theatre looking at the stage, about Row L. To be honest, when I heard he was there and looked back (discreetly!)I looked right past him! I hate to say it, but he didn't look that great last night, quite scruffy and not as good looking!

When Madonna first came on stage and the curtain rose, the crowd went wild for what seemed like ages..She just stood with her eyes closed and waited for them to stop, when I think about it, I feel that she performed better in the matinee than during the final show, maybe because of nerves and the pressure of it being the last one?

I managed to get my camera inside for both the matinee and the evening show but I didn't use it. There were lots of other people taking pictures at the end, but I was only 3 rows away from her, in the centre, but I was too close and I really didn't want to piss her off! In a way, I wish I had, she acknowledged during the standing ovations,and I got smiles and eyecontact with her again! As it was my 3rd time of being in the 3rd row, I'm starting to feel like a stalker! I do wonder if she remembers faces, because when she looks at you right in the eyes, it is so intense..

 I don't know if it's just me being silly, but I really don't feel right taking pictures of Madonna when I'm so close to her. I know she must be used to it, but I don't feel comfortable doing it. I'd rather just say hi or just smile!
My friend who I was with managed to get about 10-15 of her outside when she was coming and going, but won't be able to develop them until tomorrow. It was so crazy at the front of the theatre, soo many people being really nasty to each other, that we waited around the back. I had a tip off that she would be coming through the pizza restaurant, so my friend and I waited there. I stupidly decided to check around the Albery theatre side, and yes, Madonna walks right by my friend, no more than two feet away, with hardly any other fans there! So my friend should have some amazing pictures!
