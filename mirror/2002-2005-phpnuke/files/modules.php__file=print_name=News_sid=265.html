Madonna: Maddyfest NYC 2003 8/16/03
Date: Tuesday, July 01 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Parties

Welcome Madonna Fans!

What better place to hold our 3rd Official Madonna Birthday Convention than the NEW YORK CITY on August 16th 2003, by the ALL ACCESS Madonna Fanclub coordinators Nick Mendoros and Julio Perez.

MaddyFest 2003 will include: merchandise vendors, memorabilia showcases, contests, gift giveaways, wall-to-wall decorations, and a video projection featuring rare music videos and performances all throughout the day. At night, the partying will continue at a local dance club with additional performances and gift giveaways.
