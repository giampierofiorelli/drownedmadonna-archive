Salvatore Licitra likes Madonna.
Date: Thursday, July 03 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Interviews

Italian Salvatore Licitra, who has been hailed as their generation's answer to Luciano Pavarotti, states "I still pay attention to Lenny Kravitz and Madonna, but when you think of Verdi, Puccini, Wagner, Monteverdi, Bernstein -- that's pure genius."
