Swept Away

Limited Edition

Italian ''Top Of The Pops'' interview.
Italian "Top of the Pops” episode, broadcasted on May 31 by Rai Due, it was entirely centred around Madonna. Host of the Italian version of famous British musical program, Alvin, had the honour to meet Madonna in London to interview her. The interview, in which Alvin looked so nervous and sorely tried by Madonna herself, it was broadcasted immediately before “Hollywood” performance, recorded in the BBC studios of London. Here are the main points about Madonna’s interview... Read more...

From her words came out clear and unequivocal the meaning of “Xstatic Process”, one of the masterpieces of “American Life”.

"Through the past twenty years, I’ve been famous and I’ve realized many of my dreams, but I’ve also realized that a lot of those things like fame, fortune and success sometimes can be a trap, they can distract you from what’s really important in life, and if you don’t have the rules to live by, if you don’t follow the course of the universe, actually these things will bring you in the opposite of what you think they’re gonna bring you. If the world came to an end, I know that what’s gonna last in terms of my energy, what I put out into the world has got nothing to do with how many records I sold, I think what I leave behind, what I’ll be remembered by is how I treat people, what I’ve taught to people, not how much money I have. I don’t think that religion and God are the same thing, I think they’re very different, I think God is the universal life force and I think religion separates people. So, What I do is to talk about God in the positive sense and I talk about religion in the negative sense. I think we are the answer, we hold the key, we are infinitely responsible for everything that happens on the world. All that God does provide us with choices to make, and we have the power of God, we can create or we can distract and it’s up to us, I think what religion tries to tell us is that it’s not up to us, that we have to give up all of our power away to another entity, but I don’t believe that, I do believe that the teachings of Jesus Christ essentially say the only thing that matters is to love your neighbour as yourself, it sounds like a saying in a chocolate box, but in fact it is the hardest thing in the world for everybody to remember to do."

Written by Andy on Tuesday, June 03 @ W. Europe Daylight Time.
