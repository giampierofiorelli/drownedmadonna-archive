'Hollywood' played at Versace Milan men fashion parade.
Date: Wednesday, June 25 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

The Milan menswear season was electrified Monday by the return of Jil Sander to the house that bears her name.
At Versace, Michael Douglas and Catherine Zeta-Jones (a diamond-decked hand waving a fan at curvy bosom) added to Milan's sultry heat as they sat front row. The Hollywood power couple then braved the mosquito onslaught in the palazzo garden at a Moroccan dinner that took up the theme of the show.
Donatella Versace went Moroccan! Thought Brazilian! Played Madonna's "Hollywood!" The result was a bravura mass of color, graffiti, caftan embroidery on shirts and scoop-front sweaters, but nothing seemed as stylish or as luxe as the setting of wine red silk tent with gilded tassels, brass lamps and cauldrons of incense. The safe-but-soft Versace line had familiar shiny suits with Moroccan djellabas as shirts; while the Versus clothes had some angry slogans and urban graffiti contrasting with the hot Brazilian colored sporty polo shirts.
"It was Morocco but Hollywood," said Versace, but this cultural mix of North Africa with California did not have the fashion dash of Bollywood.
