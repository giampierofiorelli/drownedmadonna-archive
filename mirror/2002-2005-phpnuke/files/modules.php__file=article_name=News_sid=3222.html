PRESS RELEASE: Madonna Tribute Party Concert Series Update

PRESS RELEASE: MADONNA TRIBUTE PARTY CONCERT SERIES

Contact:  Nikki Jean / Connoisseur Media E-mail: press@kyaentertainment.com   Regarding: Res and Jaguar Wright added to The Tribute   On Sunday and Monday of next week The Chic Group and KYA Entertainment will be hosting a 3-concert series of another one of our hot Tribute Parties at LQ in midtown Manhattan.  The Madonna Tribute Party is only a little over a week away and the lineups for the events are looking great.  We’ve broken the shows down into three sets, one being a pop/rock set at 5pm on Sunday the 27th, the second being a part of Pride Weekend in New York at 9pm on Sunday the 27th, and the last one, the soul set, at 8pm on Monday night.    Alternative Rock star, Res, will co-headline all three shows.  It is her style and sensual character that we thought exemplified the essence of Madonna’s vibe and convinced us to lure her from her LA home to the Big Apple to participate.  Neo-Soul Singer Jaguar Wright will co-headline the soul show on Monday night.  She’ll bring a powerful voice and soulful vibe that’s never been seen at a Madonna Tribute.  A slew of other hot up and coming acts have been added to the lineups as well including Meleni (Columbia), Jonell (Def Soul), Lizz Fields (ABB), Lucy Woodward (Atlantic), Ayanna (Rawkus), Alia Marie, Aisha Mike and more (updated lineups are posted at http://www.kyaentertainment.com/madonna).    We are still booking a few more acts for each show, along with celebrity deejays and hosts.  We hope to build upon the hot lineup that we have and have gotten great feedback (but no confirmations) from a number of A-List Celebrity artists.  Jessica Simpson, Avril Lavigne, Boy George, Hillary Duff, Tatyana Ali, Christina Millian, Esthero, Fe Fe Dobson, Mandy Moore, Vivian Green, Justin Bond, Leah Dilaria, N’Dea Davenport, Q-Tip (as a DJ), Tracie Spencer, Tweet, Santi White, Samantha Ronson, and others are all still being sought.  Check the website for updates.   Tickets are on sale now for all three shows and can be purchased at http://www.kyaentertainment.com/madonna.  Please forward this information out to all that may be interested.  Contact us at press@kyaentertainment.com for any press pass inquiries.  Also if you have suggestions and contacts with any artists that would be great healining additions to an event of this nature, please contact us at madonnatribute@kyaentertainment.com.   Please feel free to contact me with any inquiries. Nikki Jean     About out Tribute Parties: In the past these events have drawn swarms of people from all walks of life.  Tribute events to Stevie Wonder, Prince, Luther Vandross, Nina Simone, and more have drawn an array of the music world’s top celebrities to come out and participate including Moby, Bilal, Rosie Perez, Anthony Hamilton (SoSo Def), Ras Kass, John Legend (Columbia), Mecca (Ladybug from Digable Planets), Saul Williams, Andre Royo (HBO’s “The Wire”), Wordsworth (MTV), Pitch Black (Universal), Afu Ra, Imani Uzuri, ChinaBlac, Anthony Mills and many more.      DETAILS: The Madonna Tribute Party is coming- only 9 days away and the buzz around this show is ENORMOUS!!  Jaguar Wright BLOWING a MADONNA song???  What??  Alternative Rock Star RES putting her spin on "Material Girl" maybe?? The sweet voice of Lucy Woodward rockin "La Isla Bonita"??  Can you imagine??  Well you won't have to because the close out show to the Madonna Tribute Party Series will bring out some of the finest soul singers alive.    In what direction will these talented vocalists pull her tunes?  We think it's safe to say that you'll be mesmerized!!! Get your tickets this moment in music history NOW!!!  Half of them are gone already.  Don't wait until the day of or you may miss out!!!   ****************************** FYI: This is a 3 concert series with each show giving a different flavor and vibe.  Check the webpage to see which of the shows is best for you!! www.kyaentertainment.com/madonna   Location: Location: LQ (www.lqny.com) 511 Lexingotn Avenue between 47th & 48th streets 4,5,6 to Grand Central station or 51st street  Ticket Price $20   “Get Into The Groove” The Madonna Tribute Party Concert Series Rocked Out Set * June 27th @ 5pm Featuring Res (Geffen), Lucy Woodward (Atlantic), Meleni (Columbia), and more… Special Guest DJ TBA Special Guest host TBA   Pride Weekend Alternative Set * June 27th @ 9pm Featuring a slew of hot drags and divas  Special Guest DJ TBA Special Guest host TBA   Soul Set * June 28th @ 8pm Featuring Jaguar Wright, Res, Alia Marie, Lizz Fields (ABB), Jonell (Def Soul), Ayanna (Rawkus), and more.  DJ REBORN on the wheels Special Guest host TBA
