Mtv re-edits Madonna's VMA Performance
Date: Wednesday, September 10 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: TV

After a number of complaints from Christina Aguilera's fans, Mtv decided to re-edit the much talked about performance of Madonna during the opening number at this year Mtv Video Music Awards...

 



In the original broadcast Madonna's kiss to Christina was cut in favour of a close up of Justin Timberlake's reaction to Britney kissing Madonna. Aguilera's fans complained that they were not satisfied with their idol's lip-action with the Queen of Pop and as a result MTV have decided to re-edit the performance featuring more Aguilra and less Timberlake for future broadcasts.
