Hackers attacks make us STRONGER
Date: Tuesday, June 10 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Drowned Madonna

What are becoming "less and less unknown" hackers are constantly trying to bring MADONNAVILLAGE.IT down.

In the last months attempts like this have happened quite frequently.

Today was the day of another internet-battle against the famous MADONNAVILLAGE but lucky enough we are now getting to the people involved in all this. Of course it was not a surprise to find out they have something to do with other madonna sites.

You know what? Things like these happen only if you're big and famous enough. And it's not a secret that MADONNAVILLAGE.IT is the most famous .it-site (both in English and Italian) dedicated to the Queen! It's a lot of work fighting jealous children but it's so damn satisfying too!

Thanks to all of you for your kind support! We appreciate that.
