 Interviews with :
. Roberto Ciurleo, Antenna director of NRJ radio
. Bert Padell, Financial adviser
. Barbara Victor, Biographer
. Steven Gaydos, Journalist at "Variety"
. Florence Rajon, Biographer
. Philippe Manoeuvre, Editor of "Rock and Folk" magazine
. Maripol, Designer
. Patrick Hernandez, Singer (excerpt TV News)
. Fabien Lecoeuvre, Agent
. Mark Kamins, DJ
. Seymour Stein, Boss of Sire label
. Georges-Claude Guilbert, Professor of university and biographer
. Mario Ciccone, Madonna's brother (old and taped interview)
. Edwige Pasquet, Director of collection of foreign authors, Gallimard
. Rick Ross, President of an association which fights against the sects, New Jersey
 
 The show started with "the kiss" with B. Spears and C. Aguilera at the MTV Awards.
 Madonna's fortune is estimated at 500 millions dollars.
 She has sold more than 130 millions of albums in 20 years.
 The most interesting parts were with Fabien Lecoeuvre, Maripol, Seymour Stein and Roberto Ciurleo.
 
 Barbara Victor and Steven Gaydos :
"Madonna incarnates the American dream".
 
 Fabien Lecoeuvre :
When she was in France, Madonna worked during one year in the North of France. It was the disco period and everything in Europe were happening there. "She worked hard everyday there", he said. It was in Lille. She lived in a small hotel vis-a-vis the station and earned only 3 500 Francs each month. Fabien Lecoeuvre already noticed her charm and her seduction game : "She loved to be liked. She absolutely wanted to seduce, near over the limite". Madonna was 20 years old. It's worth to notice that he named Madonna "Louise" during the interview.
 
 Mark Kamins :
He told the story of the "Everybody" single we all know (she met him a club and so on...)
"What she has (that others don't) is that she writes her songs", he said.
 
 Seymour Stein :
"When I knew her, she was very poor. She was given nothing. She earned EVERYTHING.", he said.
"The first deal was under 20 000 dollars and it was not a big deal but it was fair at that time".
About her first single "Everybody" without her picture on it :
It was decided by Seymour Stein himself :
"Why not play the mystery so we could sell some recordings and  see if the popular market catches on it too?".
So that people didn't know who was the singer.
Mark Kamins adds :
"Maybe white, maybe black... we don't know, so I buy the record, you know".
 
 Madonna's supposed lies at the beginning of her career :
About her family, her father's job and the place they lived...
 
 Mario Ciccone, Madonna's brother (old and taped interview) :
He didn't like at all her lies on the family...
 
 Maripol :
She talked about :
 the creation of her look that made her successful ("Holiday", ...).
 "Like A Virgin" : Madonna's famous shocking appearance on TV
 the movie "Desperately Seeking Susan" : she told Madonna to wear her own clothes to promote her look instead of wearing the old ones that they wanted her to wear in this movie
She showed several unseen photos of Madonna and her.
 
 Bert Padell :
He managed her fortune during 15 years and made her buy several houses and a lot of art (Picasso, ...).
 
 Roberto Ciurleo :
He confirms that all the American radios made a boycott of the "American Life" album which caused the failure of the sales. Because the directors of the main US radios are owned by the same persons who were close to the political power in the USA and they decided to not play a song from the "American Life" album, which was against the war and the US government decisions.
 
 The children books :
"The English Roses", launch in Paris : Madonna reads an excerpt of the book in French.
 
 Edwige Pasquet :
The famous kiss with B. Spears and C. Aguilera was a problem for Gallimard editions.
 
 Rick Ross :
"She is rumoured to have given 25 millions to the Kaballah Center and the proceeds of her children books go to the "Spirituality for kids" of the Kaballah Center".
 
Capital concluded that Madonna has no intention to stop and that she is spending this summer in studio, recording her 10th album.

In the hotel's hall you can see these pictures:

 - A big photo of Madonna on the table with the anatomical table of a kidney

- Pair of M's pictures of her on the bed, with the pale outfit

- 2 shots from RIT's rehearsals, those on the electric chair. In the second one the chair has already gone down and you can see only M's face, the first one sees the chair when it's just started descending.

 In the art gallery you find - another picture taken by the electric chair sequence (half way between the previous two) - A big photo with M on the left and a burden dummy on the right - ... and another one, but we won't tell you anything else just to keep a sort of surprise..

In the hall, you can take your own pictures at the discretion of the staff working there. When you reach the hotel, go inside and you'll find a small room (circa 40 sqm..) where on the left there's the reception and a flyer, "Madonna X-STaTIC PROCeSS FIRENZE" and just in front of the main door there's the big photo of Madonna on the table with the anatomical table of a kidney. On the left, just after the reception there's the pair of M on the bed and on the right the big one with M dressed in red and on the bed. In the centre of the hall, there's a large sofa, to rest and admire the pictures. Opposite to the entrance's door, there's on the right a small corridor where you can see the sequence of the electric chair. Referring to the gallery, there's a single room (16 sqm circa). on the right is hanged a big picture with the burden dummy and M, while on the left there are the other 2, including the electric chair one. Unfortunatly, there're no videos or music or other photos here, differently from the exhibition in other locations. Hotel's guests pass by and they pay no attention to the pictures on the wall, it all seems aseptic, but they don't understand what they miss. Fans will like it since it's definetely not crowded... ;-)

As personal comments, we can add the following lines: "If you have the occasion to visit it, go and take a look. Don't expect to see it as it's been reported from other locations... as you know, Madonna in Italy has ever been taken not so seriously by non-fans.." (Cleo)

 "It may be small and aseptic, people may pass by without giving a glance at her, but what and emotion to be in front of the Lady..." (KeepTheFaith)
