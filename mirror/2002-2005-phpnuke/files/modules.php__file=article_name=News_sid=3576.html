Korean Politician admires Madonna

from Korea Times:
One-on-one with Jun Yu-ok, Spokesperson for Grand National Party:

Q: Who are your heroes?

Jun: Firstly, Madeline Albright _ I read her book ``Madam Secretary’’ and she struck me as a very strong woman, being a Polish immigrant in the U.S. overcoming different challenges. When I was doing my PhD and debating whether to continue or not because it was so tough, there was a part in the book that helped me go on. She mentioned that she was glad she was introduced as ``Dr. Albright,’’ not ``Albright, the woman next door.’’ Along with the fact that she made every right move during her time in the political world, I also admire the fact that she could be this honest with herself.

Secondly, Madonna _ she truly is a revolutionary person. Smart. One of the very few women able to succeed with just her talent. Albright, and Clinton, these people had either great families or money to help them. Madonna didn’t have anything, even a mother, and her strength is admirable.
