On the cover of 'Harper's Bazaar'.
Date: Tuesday, July 22 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

"USA Today" has revealed that Madonna will be on the cover of September issue of "Harper's Bazaar".



The cover was shot in Los Angeles on June 24, the same day Madonna and Missy Elliott posed for their joint print and TV ads for the Gap. Madonna is wearing a Gap sleeveless T and Gap low-rise cropped corduroy pants. In the ads, which start appearing next week, both Madonna and Missy sport the initial M engraved on the back pocket of their pants.
