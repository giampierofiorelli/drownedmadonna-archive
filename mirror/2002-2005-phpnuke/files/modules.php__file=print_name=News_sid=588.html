Britney new video will feature Madonna.
Date: Friday, September 19 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Video-clip

CNN states the video for "Me Against the Music" will feature Madonna. The video-clip will debut on MTV's "Making the Video" on October 13.

Another confirmation of Madonna-Britney duet has come from Peter Rauhofer's official website. They have announced that Peter is currently in the studio remixing Madonna & Britney Spears' upcoming single "Me Against the Music".
