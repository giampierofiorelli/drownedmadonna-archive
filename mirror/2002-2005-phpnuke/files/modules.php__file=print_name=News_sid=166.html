Wyndham's theatre: Madonna's shower.
Date: Sunday, June 22 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

Liza Tarbuck, in her dressing room at the Wyndham's Theatre, gestures behind her. 'Madonna's shower. She had it put in - but took our window away,' she laughs. You can't install anything here without removing something else. The theatre is petite, to put it politely.
Wyndham's is one of seven West End theatres that are to get the Cameron Mackintosh Treatment. He will spend £30 million of his own money restoring his theatres.
