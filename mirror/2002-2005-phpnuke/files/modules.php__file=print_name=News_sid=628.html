The English Roses grow from ITALY
Date: Saturday, September 27 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Books

New details have been revealed about The English Roses’ manufacture. Italy is the country which will provide the book for the whole European and Asian market. As you may already know, the book is printed in Trieste but Padua plays also an essential role in its production as “The Roses” will be bound at the prestigious Zanardi Editoriale. Zanardi is a well known company (established in 1964) which can deliver an impressive 150,000 copies per day. Read more...


...

The owner of the company, Mr Rodolfo Zanardi, proudly explains that “the book literally comes into life between a little town, called San Dorligo della Valle, not far from Trieste (where one of our companies “Lloyd” prints the copies in all the different languages) and Padua where we complete the process, binding the books and packing them”.

From this place, Madonna’s book reaches each and every corner of Europe and Asia!

The Zanardi company has been working with the most famous organizations all over the world, including some of the greatest temples of culture such as the Louvre Museum in Paris, the Museum of Modern Art in New York, the National Gallery in London, just to name a few). They also manufactured the series “Première Decouvert”, published by Gallimar in France, which sold more than 60,000,000 copies in ten years.
