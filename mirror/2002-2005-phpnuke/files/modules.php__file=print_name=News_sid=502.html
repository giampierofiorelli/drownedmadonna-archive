Madonna appearing in ''West Memphis Three'' ?
Date: Wednesday, September 10 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Movies

 

It looks like the media can’t help talking about Madonna-The Actress future projects, though criticizing her every move on the silver screen! After a lot of talk about the possibility of Madonna’s appearance in the movie “The Alchemist”, fresh rumours say Madonna is interested in a little part in another movie called... 



... called "West Memphis Three", still in pre-production. The story is about three teenagers sentenced to death for having murdered three 8-year-old children. The movie will be directed by Alex Steyermark while Michael Pitt and Jacob Reynolds will act in the picture.

It seems Madonna is in talks to have a role in the project and could also be involved in the soundtrack. Of course this could be just speculation, so take it with a grain of salt!
