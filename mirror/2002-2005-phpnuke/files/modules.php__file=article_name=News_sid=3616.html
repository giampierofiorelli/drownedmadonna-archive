''Up For Grabs'' to be perfomed in New Zeland.

The comedy "Up For Grabs" from Australian playwright David Williamson, which - as you well know - has been performed in London's West End starring Madonna, is going to be staged at the New Zeland Downstage Theatre (6 August – 4 September).

Here is an article from Creative New Zeland:

“Up For Grabs”, the latest comedy from Australian playwright David Williamson opening at Downstage Theatre on 6 August, invites us to take a ride through another of Williamson’s hilarious satirical landscapes.

“Up for Grabs” portrays a young entrepreneurial art dealer, Simone Allen, who commits to sell a Brett Whiteley painting for $2 million – well over its accepted market value. Not only does she risk her relationship with her clinical psychologist husband, Gerry, but also their apartment. In an effort to establish herself in the “big end of town”, Simone’s ambition turns to desperation. Under pressure, she identifies three potential buyers and in the process of securing their bids finds herself morally and ethically challenged.

Known for his sharp and incisive social commentary, David Williamson’s plays explore the lifestyles and values of the urban middle class along with their foibles and follies. First produced by the Sydney Theatre Company in 2001, “Up For Grabs” has since been performed in London’s West End starring Madonna.

Director Rachel More is excited about the high-calibre cast, their skill for comedy and talent for this play’s witty, fast-paced dialogue. “Williamson puts Simone into situations that ask ‘how far will you go for something you really want?’ Each character is driven by different needs and desires that the painting embodies for them. It articulates the tension between art as a commodity and its place as an object of beauty.”

“Up For Grabs” features Lauren Jackson as Simone with John Wraight playing her partner Gerry. Loren Horsley and recent drama school graduate Nick Dunbar play Mindy and Kel, a young couple who’ve made it rich through their Internet company that preys on unhappy couples whose relationships are breaking up. With more money than sense they play along with Simone in the hope that she will play along with them.

Jude Gibson plays Dawn Grey, an art buyer for a large corporation. Frustrated that she did not have what it takes to become a great artist, she has sold her soul and become disillusioned with herself and her employer. Tim Gordon and Geraldine Brophy play Manny and Felicity, both of whom come from established wealth and find their relationship tested as they seek a trophy to substitute happiness.

There’s a pre-show talk on Monday 9 August at 5.45pm. Tickets range from $15 - $35 and you can book by calling Downstage Theatre (Tel: 04-801 6946) or online by clicking here.
