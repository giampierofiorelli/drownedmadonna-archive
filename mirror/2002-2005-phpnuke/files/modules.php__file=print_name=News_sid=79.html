“Hollywood”, a Golden…. video.
Date: Wednesday, June 11 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Video-clip

Madonna wants to do things in a big way. Here arrives, just for controversial song "Hollywood", a video with a star budget. Only for ancient jewels, the production has allocated almost £15 million.

Madonna in the new video will play a series of legendary divas of cinema, between the 20’s and the 70’s. According to well- informed sources, the Queen of Pop is going to flaunt necklaces, rings and staggering brooches. A real treasure, some of it, according to British tabloid “The Sun”, belonging to Mae West, cinema icon of 30’s, famous for her funny quote: “Is that a gun in your pocket...or are you just happy to see me”?

The video, which will be directed by Jean Baptiste Mondino, looks intriguing. Something between "Express Yourself" video, a real triumph at Mtv Video Music Awards, thanks to stage designing inspired by Metropolis movie, and Vogue, judged the best video ever by MTV, second only to Michael Jackson’s "Thriller".
