Swept Away

Rocco was born three years ago.
Madonna gave birth to Rocco three years ago, August 11th 2000. This birth was surrounded by many controversies and rumours about a risky birth. British newspaper published many articles about Madonna's choice of giving birth in Los Angeles. "Evening Standard" wrote: "Madonna snubs Britain for birth." Read the full article...

Madonna revealed today she does not want to have her baby in Britain because our hospitals are not good enough.
Asked where she plans to give birth, the superstar, eight months pregant by British film director boyfriend Guy Ritchie, told a Los Angeles-based radio station: "Come on now, have you been to hospitals in England? They're old and Victorian."
The multi-millionairess has divided her time between the US and London in recent months, but plans to give birth in Los Angeles - prompting speculation that she will use the same team that delivered her daughter Lourdes three and half years ago.
She doesn't like the thought of complications caused by giving birth in a foreign country, she said, adding: "I like efficiency."
Madonna, who has been with the director of "Lock, Stock And Two Smoking Barrels" for more than a year, also revealed the baby is a boy. There was no immeidate response from ministers to her comments during the exclusive interview with Kiss FM, and the Health Department declined to get involved. But privately, some officials were scathing. One Whitehall source said: "She's a health tourist. She is taking our healt service for granted and then slagging it off."
Madonna's dismissal of Britain's hospitals is at odds with her evident taste for London life. She moved here to work on an album a year-and-a-half ago and is renting a house in Notting Hill with Mr Ritchie, although they haven't decided where to live after birth.
The actress and pop star complains that property in London is too expensive and that it leaves her too vulnerable to the fans who  hang around outside her door, but Mr Ritchie is keen to stay in England.
The most convenient London hospital for Madonna to have given birth in would have been the state-of-the-art Chelsea and Westminster Hospital in Fulham, opened in 1993, where Cherie Blair give birth to Leo in May.
Tracey Reeves, director of midwifery services there, said: "A lot of hospitals in London are in Victorian buildings, but offer 21st century services. Maternity services in London are some of the best in the world."
Popular with celebrity mothers, including Victoria Beckham and the Duchess of York, is the private Portland Hospital, built in 1984. Then there is the private Lindo wing at St Mary's in Paddington, where Princes William and Harry were born.
Defending the reputation of NHS maternity services in London, Karlene Davis, general secretary of the Royal College of Midwives, said: "While some facilities badly need investment, there are some outstanding examples of good maternity units."
Written by Giampiero on Monday, August 11 @ W. Europe Daylight Time.
