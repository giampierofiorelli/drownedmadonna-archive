Madonna: tea for two.

LONDON, May 11 /PRNewswire/ -- - National tea drinking preferences revealed as Brits gear up for the UK's biggest tea party - Tea at 3 Fundraising Campaign

A nationwide survey of tea drinking habits reveal that Jenny Bond, former BBC Royal Correspondent would be the favourite reality TV star amongst men to take tea with (35%), beating glamour model Jordan into 4th (20%), pop babe Kerry McFadden into 2nd place (30%) and stunning Nell McAndrew into 3rd (21%).

But it's not all good news for reality TV! With Big Brother about to start this month the survey of 1000 Brits has shown that a quarter of the British public are getting bored with reality TV, as 26% would rather have tea on their own than take tea with any reality TV star. The least popular place to drink tea would also be in the Big Brother house.

However, when people were asked which famous person they would like to take tea with The Queen came top (42%), with Kylie Minogue second (40%) and Sting and Madonna joint third (38%). Amongst men Kylie topped the charts (54%) and amongst women - The Queen and Elton John came joint first (38%).

The Scottish however would prefer tea with Kylie first and foremost (49%), in the North East they prefer local pop icon Sting (51%) and in London Queen of Pop Madonna is joint first with Her Majesty (39%).

Strangely though Brits would rather have tea with The Queen at The Ritz (19%) instead of Buckingham Palace preferring to tuck into biscuits (29%) rather than cucumber sandwiches and cake (2nd)! But in Scotland most people would like to be drinking tea sat on the sofa (25%) and munching toast rather than biscuits or cake.

The survey was carried out by NOP amongst 1000 people in the UK for Help the Hospices and Tetley, as part of the national Tea at 3 fundraising event, taking place at 3pm on the 18th June. Tea at 3 encourages the public, companies and schools to hold fundraising tea parties or to simply make a donation at their tea break in aid of hospice care.

For further details of Tea at 3 and fundraising activities visit - www.teaat3.org.uk.
