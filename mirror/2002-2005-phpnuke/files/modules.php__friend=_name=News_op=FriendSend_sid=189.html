Advertising campaigns and 'Rolling Stone' cover.
Robster, one of the moderators of Madonna-Mad Forum, stated that "Love Profusion" is going to be in the background of the next Estée Lauder's advertising campaign, which will run for a whole year. The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. is one of the world’s leading manufacturers and marketers of quality skin care, makeup, fragrance and hair care products. It was founded in 1946 in New York City.

Then, he said that "Rolling Stone" cover with Madonna is pushed back to October. Initially, it should be published in August.

Finally, Robster confirmed that GAP advertising campaign, featuring Madonna and Missy Elliott, will hit TV stations on August the first. This commercial will feature "Hollywood" and Missy Elliott's remix of "Into The Groove". This is not the first time that GAP uses a Madonna song. Some years ago, they made use of a cover of "Dress You Up".

EXCLUSIVE: Madonna leaving Donatella Versace party.
European Music Awards 1998 took place in Milan (FilaForum di Assago) on November 12th, 1998. Madonna came to Milan, she performed live "The Power Of Good-Bye" and she won 2 awards. Madonna arrived at Milan on November 10th night.

Immediatly after receiving her second award, Madonna left FilaForum di Assago, without waiting for the end of MTV show. She came to Versace home in Milan (via della Spiga). In fact, Donatella Versace gave an exclusive party for Madonna. Almost every MTV show guess was there... and not only... Luca Tommassini attended the party too. William Orbit was there. Every other Milan parties - MTV After Party too - was deserted.

It was about 4:00am when Madonna left Versace home. Many fans and paparazzi was there. She was very beautiful, black dressed (with the same jacket she wore during Spain show hosted by Miguel Bosè) with dark hair. She immediatly came into her car. Liz Rosemberg was there with her. Orbit said Lourdes slept there.

Collegiate Dictionary.
Next week the 11th edition of Noah Webster's Collegiate Dictionary is launched. They said that nonstandard words are being covered as never before. Boytoy is one of the words that is being included. They think that boytoy, though used in the early 1980's from a self-description on Madonna's belt, is now in use with its sex changed to ''an attractive man regarded as being a readily compliant companion or sex partner.''

Junior Vasquez Madonna radio mixshow in New York.
D.J. Junior Vasquez weekly radio mixshow “Junior’s World” on KTU 103.5FM in New York City on Friday night was devoted to Madonna and Mariah Carey. It was “The Junior’s World Pride Show” as a celebration of Gay Pride weekend in New York City. The first hour concentrated on Madonna mixes and the final second hour on Mariah Carey. It was a non-stop megamix format.
Junior played a lot of Madonna mixes; many of them commercially unreleased mixes being only previously heard in his live club sets. He also played commercially released mixes already heard by fans from 1990s maxi-singles.
