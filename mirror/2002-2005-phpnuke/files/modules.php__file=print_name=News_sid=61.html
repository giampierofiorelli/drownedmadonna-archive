Details on Madonna and Missy Gap advertising campaign
Date: Tuesday, June 10 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: TV

The English newspaper "The Sun" reports that Madonna and Missy Elliot will be the stars of the next Gap advert! The fashion chain in the past already used a cover of the Madonna song  "Dress You Up"  in an advert... but now we have new details! Read more.....


The adverts will debut on tv this summer in the States and later in this year in the UK too! The divas will wear corduroy velvet jeans.... Madonna will sing her new single, from "American Life" album, "Hollywood", and her co-star will perform a version of Madonna's 1985 hit, "Into the Groove"... Of course, new criticisms at the horizon: MTV Italy talks about a fall of style for Madonna and says that many fans will think she is underselling herself but it seems that someone of the Madonna entourage would have said "No matter what you think about Gap clothes, their adverts are very cutting edge and have featured some big names, including Dennis Hopper. Madonna is proud to put her name to Gap."
