''Me Against The Music'': The details about Madonna & Britney collaboration.
Date: Sunday, September 28 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Interviews

Britney Spears tells that the idea of working with Madonna was born during the recording sessions of her previous album, "Britney". Their performance at the Video MTV Music Awards was very important: "We performed together on stage", said Britney in a press conference in a hotel in Rome, "Then I make her listen to my song (" Me Against The Music", n.d.r.) and she liked it so much".

Read more... to find out much more details.

Britney and Madonna haven't met in the recording studio, but Spears is "Very impressed by Madonna ability of using her voice and elaborating the melody. It enriched the track so much." Today, Britney is going to leave for the United States, where she'll shoot the video of the song. That video will feature Madonna. However, Britney denied all the rumours about Guy Ritchie directing the clip.

"Me Against The Music" will be on the radio since October 7th.
