Madonna Won't Give Up Acting Career.
Date: Wednesday, June 25 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Movies

Jeannette Walls reports Madonna is not willing to give up on her acting career. The much criticized actress reportedly is in talks with Merchant Ivory, the filmmaking team behind high-brow period dramas such as 'Room With a View.'
