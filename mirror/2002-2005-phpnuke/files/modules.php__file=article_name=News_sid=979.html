Video-clip
''Love Profusion'' video.
MTV Germany has exclusively broadcasted the "Love Profusion" video. It seems they used the album version.

Madonna gets into a virtual reality added up in post production. It seems she's walking on a fable world. The scene changes continuosly: there are many dragon-fly fairies that are flying around her, Madonna walks on petals fluttering in the air, she walks on the water.
There's a very beautiful scene: Madonna lies down on the water. There are tropical fishes around her. It seems the camera is into the water.
At the end, Madonna is covered by the dragon-fly fairies. When they go away, Madonna is disappeared.

Enter Madonna Village's Forum for the first pics and more details.

Note: Info: RosCologne, our German correspondent.
