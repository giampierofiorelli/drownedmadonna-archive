Date: Monday, June 09 @ W. Europe Daylight Time

Here is an article published on June 7th by newspaper “Il Giornale di Brescia”, about new Madonna’s cinema challenge. Read more...

A strange case, the one recorded through the past years in Ringaskiddy, town with crowed nursery schools, streets full of senior lovers and pets that fall in love with themselves. Since when a factory of Viagra was settled down on this small Irish town, the effect of the blue pill on the citizens was prompt. Just a bunch of little houses, three pubs, a shop, a social club and the fishermen’s village where the Pfizer produces the main ingredient of Viagra. After its realization, the product sets out for France and America, where it turns into the blue pill. But as  Christine Davis, manager of a pub explains: “The people do not need to buy those pills, they just breath the air”. The funny reputation of Ringaskiddy has crossed the ocean, arriving in Hollywood. It was a spark, its fame was enough to capture the interest of Madonna’s Maverick Entertainment and Konisberg-Smith. Both the film industries started to work on a screenplay about the story or the myth of the local citizens, became Latin - lovers thanks to heady smokes of the factory. Madonna’s cinema industry has already invested $30 million in a comedy with romantic connotations about the way Factory has affected three men. Free inspired to Ringaskiddy, the movie will be filmed in California.
