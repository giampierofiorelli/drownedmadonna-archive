Q magazine: Madonna 20th anniversary collector's edition.
Date: Thursday, September 18 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

Finally, Madonna's official website and Warner Bros. website have revealed the cover of "Q" magazine Madonna 20th anniversary collector's edition. The UK music magazine is going to celebrate 20 years of Madonna career with 148 pages featuring old and unseen pictures, classic interviews, best Madonna songs chart voted by her fans, etc...
