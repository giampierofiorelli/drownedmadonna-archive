Madonna on Rolling Stone !
Date: Wednesday, June 11 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

According to Robster,  the webmaster of Madonna-Mad, Madonna and Gwen Stefani have both agreed to shoot a cover for the August issue of Rolling Stone's "Women in Rock" issue. The photographer will be Jean-Baptiste Mondino. Read more...

In the past Madonna was on the cover of this special edition of the famous magazine with Tina Turner and Courtney Love.

The last year  the cover was dedicated to  PJ Harvey, Shirley Manson and Tori Amos.
