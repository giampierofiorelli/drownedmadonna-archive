New York Times' children book list.
Date: Saturday, September 27 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Charts

"The English Roses" will enter the New York Times best-seller list in the October 5 edition of the New York Times Book Review. Here's the list:

Children picture:

1) The English Roses, by Madonna

2) Diary Of A Worm, by Doreen Cronin

3) A Is For Abigail, by Lynne Cheney

4) I'm A Manatee, by John Lithgow

5) Walter The Farting Dog, by William Kotzwinkle and Glenn Murray

6) Panda Bear, Panda Bear, What Do You See?, by Bill Martin Jr.

7) How I Became A Pirate, by Melinda Long

8) The Wolves In The Walls, by Neil Gaiman

9) Good Night, Sweet Butterflies, by Dawn Bentley

10) Who's Got Game? The Lion Or The Mouse?, by Toni Morrison and Slade Morrison
