Junior Vasquez Madonna radio mixshow in New York.
Date: Saturday, June 28 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Remixes

D.J. Junior Vasquez weekly radio mixshow “Junior’s World” on KTU 103.5FM in New York City on Friday night was devoted to Madonna and Mariah Carey. It was “The Junior’s World Pride Show” as a celebration of Gay Pride weekend in New York City. The first hour concentrated on Madonna mixes and the final second hour on Mariah Carey. It was a non-stop megamix format.
Junior played a lot of Madonna mixes; many of them commercially unreleased mixes being only previously heard in his live club sets. He also played commercially released mixes already heard by fans from 1990s maxi-singles.

He began the show with a 'Crazy For You' mix. This track along with ‘Die Another Day’, 'Deeper and Deeper’ and ‘American Life’ were the standout unreleased mixes. The 'Die Another Day' mix was interesting. The 'Deeper and Deeper' mix is an anthemic delight. He didn't play his commissioned ‘Hollywood’ mix that was recently played at Donatella Versace’s fashion show. That 'I Want You' mix that people use to say was his and then by Julian Hirsh is apparently his because he played this mix tonight. The ‘You’ll See vs. If Madonna Calls’ mix was good too, but unfortunately didn’t include ‘Verás’. In his previous live sets he has prominently featured ‘Verás’, in this mix. He ended the show with a very groovy & fab 'American Life' chillout mix.

The tracks previously commercially released had some minor variations (extra looping, extra/different beat, bits from the next song lightly playing) to fit in with the rest of the songs. Some of the unreleased songs have been leaked online over the years or been on promos, but in this show these mixes had some unique variations as well. Throughout the whole set he teased the listener by throwing in short excerpts, music and sound effects from various Madonna songs (Ray of Light, Vogue) without actually fully bringing out the song. He also combined songs like ‘Human Nature vs. Up Down Suite.’
