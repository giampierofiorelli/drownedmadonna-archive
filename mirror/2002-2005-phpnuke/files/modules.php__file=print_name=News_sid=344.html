MAD'S CHEAP TRICK
Date: Wednesday, July 16 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

"The Mirror" has written a brand new article about Madonna. They state that she is losing her crown to younger, sexier rivals. Read more...

She's been undisputed queen of pop for over 20 years. But it looks like Madonna is losing her crown to younger, sexier rivals.

The singer released her muchhyped single Hollywood on Monday. Backed by a heavily publicised "controversial" video of her pretending to have Botox injections, she had expected to go straight to the top of the charts.

But all is not going to plan.

And in a desperate bid to get to the top spot her CD is selling at just £1.99

Other new releases such as Eminem's Business and Beyonce Knowles's Crazy In Love sell for £3.99 and in some cases £4.99, but Madge, boss of her record label Maverick, is offering hers at the bargain-bin price.

Shame really, as Madonna, 44, has gained acres of column inches for the video, in which she dresses up as a series of screen sirens, including Rita Hayworth and Jean Harlow, wearing £15million worth of vintage jewellery - some of which once belonged to screen legend Mae West.

Yet despite her best efforts, the Material Girl is being soundly beaten to the No 1 spot by Beyonce, who stole the show at last Sunday's Party In The Park.

Secret industry figures reveal the Destiny's Child singer is on course to remain at the top of the UK singles chart after shifting 27,500 copies of her single this week alone

Meanwhile, Hollywood has managed to sell just 16,000 copies.

A music industry insider tells us: "This is an embarrassment for Madonna. She is supposed to be the queen of pop and she can't even get to No 1 with what is supposed to be the strongest song on her album. It's highly unusual for someone as high-profile as her to be selling her single for such a low price. It smacks of desperation.

"The discount trick hasn't worked. It does make you wonder if people are losing interest in Madonna's music."

All this is in contrast to Madonna's heyday in the 80s and 90s when her singles and albums went straight to the top of the charts.

But with her latest album, American Life, she recorded her worst UK sales for five years and after just five weeks it dropped out of the Top 10 altogether. And it is not just her music career that's on the wane. Madonna has made one disastrous movie after another.

Her last film, Swept Away, which was directed by husband Guy Ritchie, bombed so badly in America that it was never released in cinemas here. And her acting was much derided.

In Hollywood, Madonna sings about movie star wannabes.

She says of the single: "We're living in a time when everyone wants their 15 minutes of fame."

Far be it from us to suggest that yours is up, Your Madgesty.
