Guardian: Ramblers lose out to Madonna.
Date: Wednesday, July 02 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

"Guardian" published a brand new article about Madonna and UK countryside.

Ramblers who had hoped to combine a stroll with a bit of star-spotting were disappointed yesterday by a decision to declare part of Madonna's country estate out of bounds.

Read more...

The singer had feared that the new "right to roam" law would allow walkers to trample through her 1,200 acre (480 hectare) hideaway in Wiltshire.

But the countryside agency has ruled that ramblers should be denied access to 15 acres (6 hectares) around the Georgian mansion which Madonna and her husband, Guy Ritchie, use as their weekend retreat.

The couple bought the house two years ago but their tranquillity was disturbed by a burglary and a planning row over security measures.

They were aghast when they realised the legislation could allow visitors to get to within 100 metres of their house.

After three months of consultation the agency agreed walkers should not be allowed so near the mansion.

The agency denies it has shown favouritism to the pop star, insisting the decision was part of the ongoing process of defining which areas constitute open countryside.
