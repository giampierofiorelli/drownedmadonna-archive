Remixes
Daniel Abraham.
Read more to find out a profile of Daniel Abraham, who has remixed "Your Honesty". Do you remember Donna DeLory's "Just A Dream"?!?!

Read more...

Daniel Abraham has remixed many songs. But we cannot forget he has remixed Donna DeLory's "Just A Dream", which was written by Madonna.
But we can't forget he has remix some Madonna's tracks, too: "Rain", "Fever" and "Bye Bye Baby." Daniel has worked with Madonna for "Bedtime Stories" album too. He has addionally produced and remixed "Don't Stop". So it's not a surprise he has remixed "Your Honesty" too, which is an out-take from "Bedtime Stories".
Then, he has worked with another star closed to Madonna: Me'shell NdegeOcello.

Duran Duran stand out amoung all the artists that have wanted him to remix their songs. Duran Duran wanted him to produced their records. The list of all the artists that have commissioned a remix is very long: Celine Dion, Seal, Baltimora, Roxette, Gloria Estefan, Tina Arena, New Order, Aaron Neville, Adrienne McDonald, Amy Grant, Ce Ce Peniston, Deacon Blue, Eric Eden, Fernando Saunders, For Real, Jeanette Katt, Joey Lawrence, Malaika, Michael Damien, Or n' More, Sofia Shinas, Wendy Maharry, Daryl Hall, Information Society.
