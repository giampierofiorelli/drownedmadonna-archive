Ane Bettz on Madonna: We're very great friends.
Date: Saturday, June 14 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Music

“Femme”, new Ane Bettz's single, has come out anticipating her “Freedom” record. From her words we've found out she is a very great friend of Madonna’s. Read More...

Italian singer Ane Bettz, stage name, some years ago leaved Rome for United States, climbing the top of dance charts, just in a few years. Her debut in 1999 with “Ecstasy", produced by Junior Vasquez.

Regarding Madonna, she's declared in these days: "We’re very great friends, we see each other every time I used to be in Los Angeles". Junior Vasquez introduced Ane to Madonna.
