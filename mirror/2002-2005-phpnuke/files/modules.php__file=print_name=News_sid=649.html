Further details about Samuel Bourdin suing Madonna.
Date: Wednesday, October 01 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Video-clip

Just an update about Guy Bourdin's son suing Madonna.

"It's one thing to draw inspiration; it's quite another to simply plagiarize the heart and soul of my father's work," Samuel Bourdin said in a statement.
A rep for the superstar said, "Madonna hasn't been served yet, so there's no comment for now."

Jennifer Lopez recently found herself in a similar predicament with her video for "I'm Glad," an over-the-top homage to Adrian Lyne's 1983 film "Flashdance." Paramount cried foul and fired off a legal notice to Sony (parent company of Lopez's label, Epic Records) but ultimately it was a win-win for both sides. The matter was resolved out of court for an unspecified amount, label and studio both got press coverage out of the incident, and J.Lo's name was instantly linked to a much-rumored remake of "Flashdance."
