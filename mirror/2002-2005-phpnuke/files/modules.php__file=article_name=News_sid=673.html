Rumours: NOTHING FAILS release date

It seems that MADONNA's next single will have a November release date. We've been informed that NOTHING FAILS will be in British stores on NOVEMBER 10th.

Read more to see the list of the UK singles which will be out on the same day.



Madonna will release her NOTHING FAILS single together with the following:

Abs - 7 Ways...
Belle & Sebastian - Step Into My Office
Black Rebel Motorcycle Club - We're All In Love
Busted - Crashed The Wedding
Melanie C - Yeh Yeh Yeh/Melt
Electric Soft Parade - Lose Your Frown
Delta Goodrem - Not Me, Not I
Enrique Iglesias - Addicted
Iron Maiden - Rain Maker
Ronan Keating - TBA
Lemar - 50:50/Lullaby
Lorna - Papi Chulo...Te Traigo El Mmmm
Love Inc - Into The Night
Madonna - Nothing Fails
Vincent de Moor - Flowtation
Muse - Hysteria
Outkast - Hey Ya
Room 5 featuring Oliver Cheatham - Music And You
Stereophonics - Since I Told You It's Over
Usher - Confessions
