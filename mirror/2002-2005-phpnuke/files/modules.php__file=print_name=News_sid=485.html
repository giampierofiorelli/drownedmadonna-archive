More about Warner Music and BMG merger.
Date: Tuesday, August 12 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Records

Warner Music and BMG have entered the home stretch in talks over a joint venture and could wrap up a deal next month, in a deal that would create the world's second biggest music company. They are still negotiating, but they are getting closer to an agreement. "If there is a deal, it will be done by the end of September, but it could be sooner than that," said one source.
