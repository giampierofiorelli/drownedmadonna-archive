Emas Nominations.
Date: Monday, September 29 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Awards

Today MTV has announced the nominations of the MTV European Music Awards 2003. Madonna was nominated for "Best Female" with Christina Aguilera, Beyoncé, Pink and Kylie Minogue. But, it's unlikely she'll win this award.
Unfortunately, "American Life" - the album - wasn't nominated for the "Best Album" award, neither "Hollywood" video was nominated for "Best Video". We think it's a shame!
Finally, Madonna wasn't announced as a performer.
