'Hollywood' video premiere at the Gay Pride in Los Angeles.
Date: Sunday, June 22 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Video-clip

Yesterday, at the Gay Pride Festival in West Hollywood, CA, after Cindy Lauper performed, the host announced that today, Sunday 6/22, there will be the World Premiere of Madonna's new video "Hollywood" there at the Festival.
