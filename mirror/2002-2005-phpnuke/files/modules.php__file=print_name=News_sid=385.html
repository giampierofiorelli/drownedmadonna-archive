MTV Video Music Awards nominees.
Date: Thursday, July 24 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Awards

2003 MTV Video Music Awards nominees have been annunced today. Madonna is nominated only for "Die Another Day". Watch out the nominees for Best Video from a Film:

JC Chasez - "She's Blowin' Me Up"

Eminem - "Lose Yourself"

Madonna - "Die Another Day"

Britney Spears (feat. Pharrell of N.E.R.D.) - "Boys (Co-ed Remix)"
