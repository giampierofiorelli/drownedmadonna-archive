A brand new 'American Life' review.
Date: Monday, June 30 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Review

We propose you to read "Ha'aretz" review of "American Life". Read more...

Just when we think Madonna has done it all, she reinvents herself with a razor-sharp sound and image, each time spawning a host of copyists. Until now. Her decision to hang onto French producer Mirwais Ahmadzai - who worked on her last album, "Music," three years ago - has resulted in a bunch of lackluster melodies and sickly sweet vocals, which reflect on old ground (money can't buy you love, it's hard to make friends when you're Madonna, etc). Those tired of Mirwais' tricks - the stilted Eurobeats, the sliced acoustic guitars and the dreaded vocoder (which even Cher gave up years ago) - have little to dance to here. And as for her efforts at a Bond Theme with "Die Another Day": We're not even shaken, let alone stirred.
