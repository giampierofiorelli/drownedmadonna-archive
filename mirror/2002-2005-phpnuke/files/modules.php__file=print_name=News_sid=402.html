Is Stella McCartney heading doon the watter for wedding?
Date: Monday, July 28 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

All the hotels are booked and the local pipe band is on standby - all that is needed now is a bride and groom.

Rumours are sweeping Bute that it will soon play host to one of the celebrity weddings of the year and islanders are high on anticipation that Stella McCartney, daughter of former Beatle Sir Paul, is to take her wedding vows there rather than in the Mull of Kintyre.

Mount Stuart House, a baronial Victorian mansion and the ancestral home of Johnny Dumfries (Lord Bute), the one-time Formula One racing driver, is rumoured to be the likely wedding venue.

It is known that Ms McCartney has had a recent disagreement with Sir Paul over the location of the forthcoming wedding, with the former Beatle wanting the marriage to take place in the Mull of Kintyre where he has a home.

The prospect of Bute for the marriage ceremony has islanders hoping for similar world-wide publicity to that which surrounded the marriage of Madonna and Guy Ritchie, her film director husband, at Skibo Castle in Sutherland.

Members of the Rothesay Pipe Band have also been asked to stand by for a big date in the coming weeks.

Charles Soane, chairman of the Bute Marketing Group said: "Recently some up-market people from down south visited us and took photographs of the rooms. We have not been told what the booking is for yet but if a wedding like this came to Bute it would be brilliant."

James McMillan, Bute's tourist information officer, said such an event would be a major boost to tourism.

He said: "In recent years Bute has been a favourite location for folk from the mainland wanting to get married.

"If this wedding goes ahead it will be a boom to the economy."

The Ardmory Hotel in the Ardbeg area of Bute is another hotel with "house full" signs for certain days ahead.

Murial McGhee, who with her husband Ron operates the five-bedroom hotel, said: "We, along with many other hotels on the island, are fully booked for one of the big weddings taking place on the island in September. If Miss McCartney comes to Bute for her ceremony I'm sure every hotel and B&B will do fantastic business."

Mount Stuart House, now a popular tourist attraction, is closing on August 24 to accommodate a number of large events including weddings and a classic motor sport rally.
