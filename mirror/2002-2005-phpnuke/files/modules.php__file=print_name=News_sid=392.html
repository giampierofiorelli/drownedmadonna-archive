Patriotic Americans against Madonna: Update.
Date: Friday, July 25 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Web

As we have written before, labeling Madonna an "anti-American entertainer," the website for Patriotic Americans Boycotting Anti-American Hollywood ( PABAAH.com) posted an "action alert" in June, calling on its supporters to ask the Gap to reconsider its endorsement deal with Madonna. But, the Gap said it was not aware of a letter-writing campaign.

Now, PABAAH.com is protesting against "Harper's Bazaar". Its webmaster has written an email to the editor:

Dear Harper's Bazaar:

It has come to our attention that your magazine will be featuring the anti-American entertainer Madonna in an upcoming issue. As an fyi, neither my wife nor my daughter will be purchasing this issue as we find this woman and her politics to be utterly distasteful.

Are you aware that she THANKED FRANCE for their opposition to the United States?

She will never see another dime of my money! 

It's time for us to take a stand. These movements are bullshit and extremist.
