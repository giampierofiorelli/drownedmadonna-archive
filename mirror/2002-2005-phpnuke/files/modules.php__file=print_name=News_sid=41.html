Twenty Century Style Icons took pride of place at Getty Gallery of London until June 5th. The honor to choose them was granted to Lucy Yeomans, editor of British magazine “Harper’s Queen”. Among them, obviously, there was Madonna. The exhibition was entitled "Style Queens of the Twentieth Century”.  

In addition to their look and personal fascination, they have been chosen for the capacity to christen new fashion trends and for their charisma which turns them into role models in front of thousands of  women. The administrator of the show predicts that Jackie Kennedy is going to be on top of the list regarding the preferences of public.

Speaking of Madonna, the organizers of exhibition claim: “She is a real trends and fashion creator. She gave rise to many trends, from bras trimmed with lace and crucifixes to tracksuits by Juicy Couture to wear in every occasion. The intention however, was not to create a museum exposition but to show the novelty of many innovations into the costume and taste. By sheer coincidence there are not sober releases. Also, many selected images have inspired contemporary artists. “The thing that fascinates us was to verify how much the way women live and dress at present days has affected people”, remarked on by general manager of Getty Gallery, Louise Garczewska. 

Among Italian icons appeared Sophia Loren and Gina Lollobrigida.

In a poll activated by Italian newspaper “Il Corriere della Sera”, entitled “Vote the most glamour woman of 1900”, Madonna is on Top, standing out from the many other icons.
