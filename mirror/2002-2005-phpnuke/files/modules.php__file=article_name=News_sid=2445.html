Review
Madge looked like a vampire.

Published in the Asbury Park Press 4/30/04
By KELLY-JANE COTTER
Music Writer

Summer's almost here and the time is right to get stripped and toxic.

Or, to put it more precisely, fans of Christina Aguilera and Britney Spears will be able to shimmy through the "Stripped" tour and the "Toxic" single, respectively, at concerts at the PNC Bank Arts Center in Holmdel.

When last we saw Aguilera at the arts center, she was coasting on her hit "Genie In A Bottle." The opening act on that tour -- Destiny's Child -- overshadowed Aguilera, due to the charisma and pipes of one Beyonce Knowles.

This time around, Xtina is much more established and has thrown off her slightly randy teen-pop style for a full-throttle, adult-oriented R&B-pop style. Aguilera's "Stripped" tour will play the arts center June 16.

Last we saw Britney at the arts center, she was a teen star, known for the schoolgirl uniform she wore in the video for ". . . Baby One More Time." She's always been a flirt, but she's no longer coy about it. When Madonna gave a TV kiss to Britney and Christina (people always forget she kissed Christina, too), it struck me that Madge looked like a vampire, trying to suck the youth out of her pop descendants.

As it turns out, though, Britney is the one who got the infusion from that kiss. Britney's "Onyx Hotel" tour is like a revue of Madonna's silliest stage tricks. Once upon a time, Madonna rattled everyone's teacups with a stage show that included simulated masturbation. A decade or so later, Britney's going at it. And everyone's shocked all over again.
It's a shame that the "Onyx Hotel" relies on that kind of thing, because Spears' album, "In The Zone" is actually a lot of fun. The current single, "Toxic," sounds like it was lifted from a Bollywood musical -- it's jumpy and bouncy.

Spears returns to the arts center on July 13.
