Madonna to be cut of 'The Sims'.
Date: Monday, July 07 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: Magazines

"Stars" magazine states that there won't be Madonna character in "The Sims" game anymore. This famous video-game reproduces the biggest American stars. Madonna is likely to be cut of this game because of her statement against war. It's rumoured that Avril Lavigne will take her place.
