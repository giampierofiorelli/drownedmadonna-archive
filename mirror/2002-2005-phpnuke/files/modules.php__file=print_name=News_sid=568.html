Oprah Winfrey Show.
Date: Wednesday, September 17 @ W. Europe Daylight Time
Topic: TV

It was aired in the US the "Oprah Winfrey Show" with Madonna.
She neither sang nor talked about her songs. Oprah asked her what young artists she prefers. Madonna named Justin Timberlake, Missy Elliot, Eminem and Britney Spears. With regard to Britney, obviously they talked about the famous kiss that Madonna gave to her and to Christina Aguilera during the performance of "Hollywood" at the MTV Video Music Awards 2003. Then they talked about the spirituality, her children, Guy Ritchie and obviously "The English Roses", the main reason why Madonna was there.
As we knew, Madonna and Oprah were dancing, together with the dancers of the GAP advertaising.
During the show - at the end of the Madonna's attendance - there was a very moving moment with a final surprise. It was read a letter written by a girl whose friend had died from a rare liver cancer at age 17. While the voice was reading the letter, the camera was on Madonna, who seemed extremely moved; her eyes were full of tears. Finally, it is been revealed to Madonna that the family of the girl was in the audience. They came up on stage and hugged.
Madonna looked very attractive and calm. Oprah commented she has changed since the last time she interviewed her five years ago. She said she was less nervous.
