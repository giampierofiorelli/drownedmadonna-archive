Christopher Ciccone exclusively talks to The Mirror about his sister.

From Daily Mirror:

Talking about the pressure that must come with being married to his famous sister, Christopher says: "You can’t really prepare yourself for it. I mean, I don’t know how a person prepares himself for that.

"I was there from the beginning so it was no big surprise for me. But if you’re not around for the evolution of it, for what it becomes and how intense it can get, it can be surprising. It just takes getting used to - it’s as simple as that."

[...]

"I would say Guy has managed to cope," adds Christopher. "They’re still together. He’s famous himself on a different level and in a different field." 

[...]

Christopher Ciccone admits: "I don’t listen to my sister’s music really. I’ve just heard it too much.

"There are certain songs that I really like, such as Like A Prayer, and the Erotica album was a great record - probably her best, in my opinion. Confessions is a more joyful, a little happier - like she’s lightened up a bit."

THROUGH Madonna, Christopher was drawn into the Kabbalah religion, but he has since become disenchanted with the faith - a mystical branch of Judaism.

He says: "She took me to her house and all of the teachers were there and I just listened.

"There are some really good things in it. You have to take it and make it your own, the same as being a Catholic, make it whatever works. But anything that’s organised can become very cultish. It got that way for me, so for a long time I sort of stopped.

"With all the celebrities involved, it takes something that’s actually kind of cool and really useful and makes it appear foolish, stupid and faddish -- and it’s not.

"But when you read about Britney Spears investigating it, it becomes so..."

Surprisingly, their father Tony - a devout Catholic - didn’t turn a hair when told about Madonna’s obsession with Kabbalah. But then it seems he gave up battling his strong-willed daughter a long time ago.

Christopher says: "I think my father did what only the father of Madonna could do and that’s just let it slide off and carry on. It could make a person crazy, but he just makes his wine and he’s a happy guy."

London guitar teacher talks about his lessons for Madonna.

You probably remember that Drowned Madonna exclusively reported that Madonna sang some songs from the musical "The Sound Of Music" at last year's Purim party in London (LINK).

Now London guitar teacher Bobby Tider tells that he tought Madonna the "Sound Of Music". Here's an article from ContactMusic:

Pop superstar Madonna took guitar lessons so she could play The Sound Of Music to her children Lourdes and Rocco.

The Material Girl visited London guitar teacher Bobby Tider three times a week for four months in 2001, and was soon out-strumming her film-maker husband Guy Ritchie.

Tider says, "I got a call from Guy. I didn’t know who he was, just some rich dude. I used to go to his flat and I was sitting there one day when Madonna came in. She asked me to give her lessons too.

"Because I had been charging Guy £30-per-hour I had to charge Madonna the same - she was used to paying £60.

"I taught Madonna the scales. She wanted to learn The Sound Of Music for her kids. She also wanted to play early David Bowie songs.

"She was very good and worked very hard. You have to put in the hours. Guy was slow but Madonna was a good pupil."

Excerpts from Harper's Bazaar.

Madonna says she’s "strong again" after breaking nine bones in a horse riding accident at her country home outside London last year.

"Well, I did survive 2005," the pop diva says in an interview in the March issue of Harper’s Bazaar, now on newsstands. "It was a very tumultuous year. I felt like I came out of my disco ball like most people get shot out of a cannon. Forget about riding a horse again - I didn’t know if I was going to be able to dance again."

Madonna suffered three cracked ribs, a broken collarbone and a broken hand after falling off a horse on her 47th birthday on Aug. 16.

"When I shot the video (for ’Hung Up’) none of the bones had gone together. Pharmaceuticals and my will got me through the shoot. So to come out of that, I felt so much inspiration and so much joy just to have my body back and to feel strong again," she says.

Madonna says she stopped doing yoga while her shoulder was healing.

"I used to be a freak about doing yoga, but I had to do a lot of Pilates to rehab my shoulder joint and get the use of my arm back. So I started really getting into Pilates and dancing more," she says.

"My Pilates instructor was a dancer, so my workouts became much more ballet-oriented, rather than the visceral, gymnastic, Ashtanga yoga thing."

Madonna’s love affair with Britain, where she lives with husband/filmmaker Guy Ritchie and her two children, continued as "Sorry," the second single from her "Confessions on a Dance Floor" album, shot straight to the top of the singles chart.

"Sorry" is her 12th British No. 1 and matches the success of her last release, "Hung Up," the Official UK Charts Co. said Sunday.

Madonna in C-charge blast.

Madonna has launched an extraordinary attack on the congestion charge, saying she refuses to pay - even though it costs her thousands of pounds in fines.

The 47-year-old star, who lives in Marylebone with husband Guy Ritchie and her two children, will not register her personal details with Transport for London so she can pay. It means she is fined £100 a day every time she drives inside the zone.

As Marylebone is in the zone, she could get the 90 per cent residents’ discount on the charge. She said: "I’m pissed off about the congestion charge. It’s ridiculous. I live there and I’ve got to pay. I can’t pay because they want my phone number. Why would I give my number? I may get a pay-as-you-go phone so they can’t know where I live."

The singer spoke last night backstage at the Brit Awards, where she was named best international female solo artist. Some 6,600 people a day are fined for entering the congestion zone without paying the charge.

A TfL spokesman said: "You have to prove you are living in the congestion charge area to get the 90 per cent discount. That means giving your address, which is only fair.

"The rules are the same for everyone. No one gets special treatment."

[EXCLUSIVE] More about Julien D'Ys's books.

Marine Le Joncour tell us more about Julien D’Ys and his books.


Claudine: As you can see it here, we announced the release due in January 2005 of the book "Cahier n° 7 - Madonna" by Julien D’Ys at Colette shop on 20th December 2004 thanks to an article published in French magazine ELLE.
Marine Le Joncour: Thank for your interest in the Cahier N° 7 Madonna by Julien D’Ys. Indeed, you took part of those who spoke about Julien D’Ys book, along with the press, ELLE France and Madame Figaro. Thank you for your help and your support. 

Claudine: On 20th January 2005, following the advertisement about the release of the "Cahier n° 7" by Julien D’Ys on our website DrownedMadonna.com, as well as on other Madonna's fansites, the sales of the book was a great success according to Colette shop, with the orders immediately exceeding 500 copies... Had you considered a second edition if the requests would have exceeded 3,000 copies?
Marine Le Joncour: I was regularly in contact with Colette team who gave me news about the sales. We are really glad to see how much this book dedicated to Madonna was impatiently awaited. At the beginning, we did not know yet what we were going to do. Anyway, we do NOT consider a re-edition. We like the idea that this book is a sort of "collector" and becomes an invaluable and rare item. 

Claudine: Can you tell us some more about the other books?
Marine Le Joncour: Here are the informations about Julien D’Ys previous books :
N°1: Julien D’Ys’s very first book
N°2/3: dedicated to Chiara Mastroianni
N°4: dedicated to Amber Valletta
N°5: dedicated to Linda Evangelista
N°6: dedicated to Milla Jovovich
N°7: dedicated to Madonna 
Julien is the one and only able to speak better about his work, his book and his collaboration with Madonna.


 

And here is what Julien d’Ys himself says in his book :

- 5th March 2004 is the day when he met Madonna again thanks to Steven Klein.
- He started with Madonna almost 10 years ago in New York and Hawaii in 1986 with Herb Ritts.

Julien D’Ys works a lot in the United States. After the "Re-Invention Tour", he also worked with Madonna for the photographs of the August 2005 issue of Vogue US.

[EXCLUSIVE] Cahier n° 7 - Madonna by Julien D'Ys: We chatted with the distributor.

It’s one year this weekfrom the release of the book ’’Cahier n° 7 - Madonna’’ by Julien D’Ys . A collection of hair-stylist Julien D’Ys notes and photos on working with Madonna during  the rehearsals for her "The Re-Invention Tour" in 2004.

Our French correspondent, Claudine, had the privilege to chat with Guillaume Salmon from the press department of the book distributor, Colette shop in Paris, giving us an exclusive flashback on this release... which is now a rare item for the collectors.

   



Claudine: Hi Guillaume, welcome to Drowned Madonna. First of all, tell us when Colette - this amazing store in Paris - has been launched...
Guillaume Salmon: Colette exists since March 1997.

Claudine: As you can read here, we at Drowned Madonna announced on 20th December 2004 the release of the book ’’Cahier n° 7 - Madonna’’ by Julien D’Ys, due in January 2005, thanks to an article published in French magazine ELLE. Then, on 20th January 2005, we announced its release and its sale on-line the following day on your website (links: 01, 02). So, this news was also spread on other websites. What were the effetcs of these advertisements over the following day at your shop?
Guillaume: Indeed, more than 500 copies were ordered, and thus, we thank you. It is thus to you that we owe this great success !!!

Claudine: How many copies of the book were available?
Guillaume: I know that the total production was 3,000 copies and considering that we were the one and only distributor of this book, we ordered some up to this point.

Claudine: Did you have it exclusively?
Guillaume: This book was indeed exclusively released at our shop, just like all Julien d’Ys previous books.

Claudine: Are some books still available?
Guillaume: Unfortunately, they are no more available.

Claudine: Did you get such an immediate success with the previous books?
Guillaume: I must admit.. No, we didn't. The previous books worked very well, but not as strong as the one about Madonna, which really impressed us.

Claudine: How did you become its exclusive distributor?
Guillaume: This is what characterizes Colette: exclusiveness.

Claudine: Have you ever met Julien d’Ys in person?
Guillaume: No... not yet, sometimes by telephone but nothing more.

Claudine: Can you tell us some more about him?
Guillaume: I do not know him personally, I only know that Julien d’Ys is a big name of the hairstyle in the fashion world, with an excellent reputation.

Claudine: To which countries went the majority of the sales of the book at the beginning ?
Guillaume: Italy, Spain, England, USA, Netherlands...

Claudine: When did you learn about the release of this book for the first time?
Guillaume: Oh, around 6 months before its release.

   

A very special thanks to Guillaume Salmon, at Colette press department, for his willingness and his kindness.

JOHN SLAMS MADONNA AGAIN!!
SIR ELTON JOHN slammed MADONNA as a "miserable cow" after she failed to attend his pre-wedding cabaret on Monday night (19DEC05), according to British newspaper The Daily Mirror.

The CANDLE IN THE WIND hitmaker, 58, reportedly hit out at the pop superstar during a speech thanking guests and performers for attending the lavish London bash.

John will exchange vows with longterm love DAVID FURNISH later today (21DEC05), taking advantage of new British laws affording gay unions the same legal protection enjoyed within traditional marriages.

John reportedly said, "Madonna, the miserable cow, wouldn't do it. David asked her three times."

Madonna about falling of the horse.

The sexy singer, who is a keen horse rider, claims her sleep has been haunted by equine night terrors since she was thrown from a horse on her 47th birthday in August.

The superstar, who was left with a broken hand and collar bone as well as three cracked three ribs after the accident, said: "I’ve been having a lot of bad dreams lately about horses and horse riding. It’s been a recurring theme."

Despite the accident, Madonna revealed she has been horse riding since and wasn’t daunted at all by getting back in the saddle.

She told Britain’s Radio One: "It was good. I love ripping across the fields really fast."

Last month, Madonna admitted: "Falling of the horse was the most painful thing that ever happened to me in my life, but it was a great learning experience."

Madonna has banned her children from using cell phones.

The sexy singer - who is married to film director Guy Ritchie - insists she would never buy daughter Lourdes, nine, and five-year-old son Rocco phones because it’s unnecessary.

She told Britain’s Radio One: "There’s no way they will be getting phones. That’s not going to be happening."

Earlier this year, Madonna revealed she is a strict parent - and even banned her children from watching TV.

The pop diva admits she’s a tough mother and says Rocco and Lourdes live by strict rules.

Madonna, 47, said: "My kids don’t watch TV and I have strict rules. My daughter has a problem picking things up in her room.

"If you leave your clothes on the floor, we put them in a bin bag. She has to earn them back by being tidy."
