DrownedMadonna Suggests...
Mike Rickard.

On account of our tradition, we suggest you to take some minutes to get into the world of a brand new singer, Mike Rickard, who visits Drowned Madonna daily. Here's what he wrote us:

I just wanted to let you know that I visit your website daily to keep track of the goddess.

A few months back, I released a full length CD, Stirred Not Shaken. The CD is all original music. I wrote all the lyrics, music, sang lead vocals and played guitar on two tracks. I’ve gotten some great reviews on my CD, and listener/audience response has been great.

Madonna has been a huge influence on my life. The song that most reflects her influence is "Who I Am." While there are no samples, I did take elements of "Like A Prayer" (the grunge guitar and gospel background vocals) and "Vogue" (a spoken bit in the song).

I have a website (www.mikerickard.com), and the CD is available at CD Baby (www.cdbaby.com/cd/mikerickard) or Tower Records (www.towerrecords.com or http://uk.towerrecords.com). There are 2 minute samples of each song on CD Baby or Tower. There are also samples on my website, they’re just shorter.

Thanks, and take care,
Mike Rickard
www.mikerickard.com

DrownedMadonna Suggests...
How NOT to make it in the Pop World.

Our reader tenor saxophonist JOHN BARROW, one of the journeymen of pop music who worked and recorded with world name artists including BOY GEORGE, CULTURE CLUB, IGGY POP, THE CLASH, BANANARAMA and so on, chose Drowned Madonna to promote his new book titled "How Not To Make It In The Pop World".

HOW NOT TO MAKE IT IN THE POP WORLD 
A great new book by Leicester tenor sax man JOHN BARROW. One of the journeymen of pop music. In a playing career spanning twenty five years he has played and recorded with world name artists which include:

BOY GEORGE / CULTURE CLUB / THE FUN BOY THREE / IGGY POP / MUSICAL YOUTH / THE SWINGING LAURELS / JERRY DAMMERS / LAUREL AITKEN / THE CLASH / RHODA DAKAR (THE SPECIALS) / CRAZYHEAD / THE BEAUTIFUL SOUTH / BANANARAMA.

Always on the periphery, never quite hitting the pay dirt, this is the tale of one man’s quest for unlimited world wide fame and fortune.
