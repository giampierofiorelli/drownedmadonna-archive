Claudine: As you can see it here, we announced the release due in January 2005 of the book "Cahier n° 7 - Madonna" by Julien D’Ys at Colette shop on 20th December 2004 thanks to an article published in French magazine ELLE.
Marine Le Joncour: Thank for your interest in the Cahier N° 7 Madonna by Julien D’Ys. Indeed, you took part of those who spoke about Julien D’Ys book, along with the press, ELLE France and Madame Figaro. Thank you for your help and your support. 

Claudine: On 20th January 2005, following the advertisement about the release of the "Cahier n° 7" by Julien D’Ys on our website DrownedMadonna.com, as well as on other Madonna's fansites, the sales of the book was a great success according to Colette shop, with the orders immediately exceeding 500 copies... Had you considered a second edition if the requests would have exceeded 3,000 copies?
Marine Le Joncour: I was regularly in contact with Colette team who gave me news about the sales. We are really glad to see how much this book dedicated to Madonna was impatiently awaited. At the beginning, we did not know yet what we were going to do. Anyway, we do NOT consider a re-edition. We like the idea that this book is a sort of "collector" and becomes an invaluable and rare item. 

Claudine: Can you tell us some more about the other books?
Marine Le Joncour: Here are the informations about Julien D’Ys previous books :
N°1: Julien D’Ys’s very first book
N°2/3: dedicated to Chiara Mastroianni
N°4: dedicated to Amber Valletta
N°5: dedicated to Linda Evangelista
N°6: dedicated to Milla Jovovich
N°7: dedicated to Madonna 
Julien is the one and only able to speak better about his work, his book and his collaboration with Madonna.


 

And here is what Julien d’Ys himself says in his book :

- 5th March 2004 is the day when he met Madonna again thanks to Steven Klein.
- He started with Madonna almost 10 years ago in New York and Hawaii in 1986 with Herb Ritts.
- He was born in Bretagne, France.
- For the Re-Invention Tour, he worked day and night to not disappoint her.
- He wrote : "Madonna was an enriching work". He describes her as being "hard, demanding, strong, impressive, inspiring and perfectionist".
- At the end of the book he wrote that, on 4th May 2004, Madonna’s word when she left was : "I love you".
- In his thanks, he dedicated the book to his missing father, gone 2 years before.

Julien D’Ys works a lot in the United States. After the "Re-Invention Tour", he also worked with Madonna for the photographs of the August 2005 issue of Vogue US.

 

A very special thanks to Marine Le Joncour.

Our French correspondent, Claudine, had the privilege to chat with Guillaume Salmon from the press department of the book distributor, Colette shop in Paris, giving us an exclusive flashback on this release... which is now a rare item for the collectors.

   



Claudine: Hi Guillaume, welcome to Drowned Madonna. First of all, tell us when Colette - this amazing store in Paris - has been launched...
Guillaume Salmon: Colette exists since March 1997.

Claudine: As you can read here, we at Drowned Madonna announced on 20th December 2004 the release of the book ’’Cahier n° 7 - Madonna’’ by Julien D’Ys, due in January 2005, thanks to an article published in French magazine ELLE. Then, on 20th January 2005, we announced its release and its sale on-line the following day on your website (links: 01, 02). So, this news was also spread on other websites. What were the effetcs of these advertisements over the following day at your shop?
Guillaume: Indeed, more than 500 copies were ordered, and thus, we thank you. It is thus to you that we owe this great success !!!

Claudine: How many copies of the book were available?
Guillaume: I know that the total production was 3,000 copies and considering that we were the one and only distributor of this book, we ordered some up to this point.

Claudine: Did you have it exclusively?
Guillaume: This book was indeed exclusively released at our shop, just like all Julien d’Ys previous books.

Claudine: Are some books still available?
Guillaume: Unfortunately, they are no more available.

Claudine: Did you get such an immediate success with the previous books?
Guillaume: I must admit.. No, we didn't. The previous books worked very well, but not as strong as the one about Madonna, which really impressed us.

Claudine: How did you become its exclusive distributor?
Guillaume: This is what characterizes Colette: exclusiveness.

Claudine: Have you ever met Julien d’Ys in person?
Guillaume: No... not yet, sometimes by telephone but nothing more.

Claudine: Can you tell us some more about him?
Guillaume: I do not know him personally, I only know that Julien d’Ys is a big name of the hairstyle in the fashion world, with an excellent reputation.

Claudine: To which countries went the majority of the sales of the book at the beginning ?
Guillaume: Italy, Spain, England, USA, Netherlands...

Claudine: When did you learn about the release of this book for the first time?
Guillaume: Oh, around 6 months before its release.

   

A very special thanks to Guillaume Salmon, at Colette press department, for his willingness and his kindness.
