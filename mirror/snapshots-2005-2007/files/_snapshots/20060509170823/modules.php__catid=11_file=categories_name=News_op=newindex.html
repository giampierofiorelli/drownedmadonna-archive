DrownedMadonna can show you the press release that Warner Music Canada is just going to release.

MADONNA REACHES NUMBER ONE IN CANADA

IN JUST TWO WEEKS

“Hung Up” is the fastest rising single in BDS history

For immediate release

The fans have clamoured for it and radio stations across Canada have delivered it. According to Nielsen - Broadcast Data Systems (BDS) Madonna’s new single, “Hung Up,” has reached #1 on Canada’s Contemporary Hit Radio Chart in only its second week on the air, making it the fastest rising single since BDS began monitoring Canadian radio airplay in 1995. “Hung Up” is the first single from Madonna’s forthcoming album Confessions On A Dancefloor.

The track is also Top 5 Hot AC and Top 5 Overall at radio

"What makes this even more impressive is that this achievement has happened after just 10 1/2 days of airplay. No other song has even come close to this in the last 10 years," said Paul Tuch, Nielsen Entertainment – BDS.

Anticipation for the new Madonna album is building to a feverish pitch and airplay is exploding all around the world. The single has already reached #2 on the European airplay charts as well as #8 in Japan and #8 in Germany. The video for “Hung Up” is scheduled to debut around the world on Thursday October 27.

“Hung Up” also dominated the digital world this week debuting at #1 on Canada’s Digital Download chart which monitors legal downloads of music.

Set to arrive in stores November 15th, Confessions On A Dance Floor once again establishes Madonna’s preeminent place on the international club scene. It’s a mesmerizing sound that evokes and embellishes all the excitement, energy and innovation that first set her apart as a dance music pioneer over two decades ago.

MTV just sent us the following new press release:

MADONNA AND MTV HOST SPECIAL ADVANCE SCREENING OF
“I’M GOING TO TELL YOU A SECRET” FOR FANS AND FRIENDS

MADONNA’S DOCUMENTARY SET TO AIR EXCLUSIVELY ON
MTV, VH1 AND LOGO IN OCTOBER


Madonna and MTV: Music Television today announced that they will co-host a special screening of “I’m Going to Tell You A Secret” for fans and friends on Tuesday, October 18th at Sony Lincoln Plaza in New York City. MTV and Warner Bros. Records are partnering to fly in winners to the special screening who have won access through radio contests across the country. Fans can also sign up to win tickets to the screening through MTV.com (madonna.mtv.com) through tonight, Thursday, October 13th.

“I’m Going to Tell You A Secret” is the fascinating story of Madonna’s journey on stage and off during her recent Re-Invention Tour. In Madonna’s second documentary - which follows the groundbreaking “Truth or Dare” released over a decade ago - it is clear from the touching, hilarious, revealing, ironic, and surprising moments during her travels this time out, that the artist has reached even higher levels of creative power. But she has also chosen to ask more questions, seek answers and grow spiritually.

The documentary, brilliantly filmed by director Jonas Akerlund, shows an intimate first row view of Madonna performing hits from the last two decades of her stunning career. The film includes close up views of Madonna assembling the music to dancers auditions, to rehearsals to all the highs and lows of life on the road. Madonna also lets us into the world of her family and friends, husband and children, artists and the people who inspire her work and feed her soul. The film follows Madonna from opening night thru to the final show in Lisbon when the touring family says their heartfelt goodbyes. “I’m Going to Tell You A Secret” continues with Madonna’s personal and spiritual journey to Israel to pray for world peace. It’s a place where we see the purest form of this artist and her true beauty inside and out.

“I’m Going to Tell You A Secret” will debut on MTV Friday, October 21st and air on VH1 and Logo the next week. A ten-minute sneak peek is currently available on-demand on Vspot, VH1’s broadband entertainment service (vspot.vh1.com).

The special screening is part of a larger slate of MTV Networks Music/Logo Group multiplatform programming surrounding the Warner Bros. Records November 15th release of Madonna’s latest studio album Confessions On A Dance Floor. MTV, VH1, Logo, MTV Overdrive (overdrive.mtv.com), VH1 Vspot (vpot.vh1.com), LOGOonline.com, MTV.com, VH1.com, *MTV on Virgin Mobile, MTV Hits, VH1 Classic, VH1 Mobile and other content-driven platforms will each offer fans the ability to celebrate the music from one of the world’s biggest artists via multiple mediums. Madonna will kick off the programming with a special appearance on MTV’s “Total Request Live” Monday, October 17th, offering fans a first-listen to the hotly anticipated single “Hung Up” simultaneous to the single’s release at radio.

The video for Madonna’s new single “Hung Up,” directed by Johan Renck, will World Premiere in late October on VH1 with airings the same day on MTV’s TRL, Logo, MTV Hits and VH1 Classic. Additionally, MTV News and VH1 News will be the first outlets to conduct in-depth interviews with the Material Girl around the album’s release.
