Madonna is reportedly considering the purchase of an apartment in Tel Aviv.
Date: Tuesday, September 25 @ 01:52:11 CEST
Topic: (Private) Life

Madonna, who spent Rosh Hashanah in Israel along with fellow Kabbalah enthusiasts, has voiced interest in a luxury residential complex going up in northern Tel Aviv, Yediot Achronot reported Sunday. Prices for apartments there begin at $1 million. Other prospective clients include Steven Spielberg, the daily added.

When Madonna was in Israel two years ago, local media reported that she might buy a house in Rosh Pina, a village near the mystical town of Safed.
