Ex-Madonna Mercedes appeared on Deeper And Deeper.
Date: Saturday, August 07 @ 03:19:01 CEST
Topic: Auctions

Ex-Madonna 1969 Mercedes-Benz 280SE Cabriolet - white with black leather interior and black top - will be on sale on August 12 at a Los Angeles Christie's International auction.

The 280SE offered at this auction was formerly the personal property of Madonna, and also appeared in her 1992 music video Deeper And Deeper. Since purchased at auction from Madonna in 1995, this vehicle has been sparingly used and garage kept and shows approximately 70,000 miles. Most recently it has undergone a thorough servicing by one of the country's largest Mercedes-Benz dealerships and is reported to be an excellent driver, but currently the windshield wipers and air conditioner are out of commission. This is an extraordinary opportunity to purchase a sleek, classic automobile with a celebrity history.

Estimate: 40,000 - 60,000 U.S. dollars.
