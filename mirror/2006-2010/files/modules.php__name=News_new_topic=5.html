Madonna new movie is called WE.
Category: Movies.

Interviewed by Ryan Seacrest, Madonna revealed that her new movie is called "We."

She added that she has just finished writing the script. "It's a love story," said Madonna. "It's two parallel love stories... one is an historical story that took place with the Duke and Duchess of York, and the other is a story I made up of a couple in New York."

Madonna is trying to get financing and casting done now.

"I modelled my main charecter to you [Ryan Seacrest (editor's note)]," Madonna joked about her script, but she added a few details that are likely real. "He's very intellectual, very actractive, and he is very successful, funny... he's like the perfect guy," said Madonna about her made up main male character.
"He's a guy that everybody thinks he's gay, but he's the perfect guy," Ryan joked; so, Madonna laughed.

"Madonna, I do think that you are more fun now," said Ryan. And Madonna replied, "Well, I had to be back in America, I had to be back in New York... and I feel good... I adapted with my new life."

Then, Ryan described Madonna's apartment as a castle. Madonna, replied, "Castle? You mean... which one? I never had a castle." And Ryan went on, "A saw pictures of some big place in England."
"Yeah, I had to give that away," replied Madonna.

Then, Madonna asked Ryan, "How old are you?" Ryan replied he is 34 years old. Therefore, Madonna joked, "Honey, you're kinda old for me!" And Ryan said, "What?"

"How is that younger guys make you happy?" asked Ryan. "You know, younger people are generally more adventurous... they are more open, more fun," said Madonna.

Then, they talked about her training program everyday to keep in shape.

Ryan asked Madonna how many houses she owns. She replied, "Three: one in London, one in New York, and one in Hollywood."

"Another boy in each house?" asked Ryan. Madonna replied, "Boys doing works for me?" Ryan said, "No, boys you hang out with."
"Oh, what do you think I am?" asked Madonna. "You're Madonna; I've seen the videos," joked Ryan. Madonna laughed and replied, "Don't confuse my professional images with my personal."
"No, but I want you to be that Madonna," said Ryan. "Yes, desperately!"
"So, you are saying that the Madonna we got to see is not as sexy and provocative as the Madonna that really is you?" asked Ryan. "No, I'm not saying I am not sexy and provocative," replied Madonna. "But... I'm a one-man woman."

Then, they talked about marriage, and that she said on the Letterman show that she is not marrying again. "Marriage is... mmmm," said Madonna. But she has no problem with Jesus (she actually never mentioned his name during the whole interview) at all. "Most of the people get married for the wrong reasons," added Madonna.

Then, they talked about Jennifer Lopez, Lady Gaga and Kanye West.

Ryan asked her about her speech at this year's MTV Video Music Awards. Madonna said it was her tribute to Michael Jackson.

''42 ROTATIONS'' short film featuring Madonna wins award.
Category: Movies.



Adrian + Shane's short film featuring Madonna, "42 ROTATIONS" has just been awarded 'The Special Jury Award' at Pride Film Shorts Awards which took place in Dublin on Sunday.

Filmed at the Astoria (G-A-Y) in London on November 19th 2005 using a Canon IXUS 50 digital camera. It was originally meant to take a photograph but the camera was on the wrong setting and recorded a (one second long) accidental film instead! We then used that second of video footage, repeating the same clip over and over 42 times. The color changes with each repetition. We used the original sound, you don't hear any music, just a loud violent noise.

Adrian + Shane are two artists working as one since 1998.

This years judging panel will consisted of leading Irish film practitioners:

Mark O'Halloran is an Irish scriptwriter and actor. A native of Ennis, County Clare, Mark has written award-winning screenplays for the films Adam and Paul (in which he co-starred) and Garage and the RTE mini-series Prosperity.

Barry Dignam is an Irish filmmaker. Barry studied drama at Trinity College Dublin and Film at the National Film School of Ireland (IADT). He has made a number of internationally successful shorts including Chicken", "Dream Kitchen" and "A Ferret Called Mickey".

Martina Niland is an honors graduate of Communications (Film & Broadcasting) at the Dublin Institute of Technology. An internationally established producer, Martina co-produced Perry Ogden's debut film Pavee Lackeen. In 2006, Martina produced Bachelors Walk for RTE and also produced 'Once'

The film has just been posted on YouTube. It could potentially win another award, 'The Youtube Award' and we are encouraging viewers to vote by rating it 5 stars. The winner will be announce next sunday.

Find out more at www.adrianandshane.com.

I AM BECAUSE WE ARE Online Debut.
Category: Movies.

I AM BECAUSE WE ARE will be featured on Hulu.com this Thursday, March 26th.

I AM BECAUSE WE ARE, the remarkable documentary film by director Nathan Rissman and Raising Malawi co-founder Madonna, documents the extraordinary challenges faced by Malawian children in the wake of the AIDS pandemic.

While the film features appearances by Madonna, Archbishop Desmond Tutu and President Bill Clinton, its real stars are the spirited children whose stories so heart wrenchingly relate the complexities of life in the developing world.

I AM BECAUSE WE ARE has inspired audiences at film festivals worldwide with its message of interdependence and intrinsic connection between all peoples. Its title is derived from a concept in African spirituality which states that the very existence of any one of us is defined by the well-being of others.

After a successful featured run on The Sundance Channel, the film’s message of global interconnectedness now arrives at a very fitting venue on the worldwide web. Spread the word: I AM BECAUSE WE ARE enjoys a limited six-week online engagement at Hulu.com beginning this Thursday, March 26th.

Filthe and Wisdom At Turin Festival
Category: Movies.
Hollywood Reporter ROME -- Bill Maher and Larry Charles' anti-religion documentary "Religulous" John Maybury's romantic drama "The Edge of Love" and Jia Zhang Ke's "24 City" (Er shi si cheng ji) will be among the highlights of the 26th Turin Film Festival, organizers said Friday. Although the festival's full lineup will not be released until Nov. 7, the festival released the names of about two dozen titles in various sections a week ahead of time. Other highlights include Madonna's directorial debut, "Filth and Wisdom," and Kelly Reichardt's drama "Wendy and Lucy." Most of the film named so far -- and all of the films mentioned above -- are in the official selection. But there has been no word yet on the makeup of the festival's competition lineup, which focuses on directors' first and second works. As previously announced, Oliver Stone's George W. Bush satire, "W.," will be the opening film for the festival, which also will include a retrospective of director Roman Polanski. Both Stone and Polanski are expected to make an appearance at the festival, which is set to run Nov. 21-29.

Madonna´s documentary to receive Dominican premiere
Category: Movies.
Santo Domingo.– Madonna's film "I Am Because We Are," an arresting presentation of the impact that HIV/AIDS has had on a small nation in an unfamiliar part of an already devastated continent, will have its Dominican premiere at the II Dominican Republic Global Film Festival. A total of 36 feature-length fiction and documentary films have been confirmed to be screened during the second edition of the festival, which will be celebrated November 19-23. The Dominican Republic’s most prestigious international film festival will simultaneously be celebrated in five cities: Santo Domingo, Santiago, Puerto Plata, Higüey and Nagua, at 10 different venues, making it an event of unprecedented proportions. Under its motto, Global Issues, Personal Stories, the Festival –an initiative of Global Foundation for Democracy and Development (GFDD) and Fundación Global Democracia y Desarrollo (FUNGLODE)– will bring the Dominican Republic, the best of international film, while at the same time offering opportunities for learning, professional development and the creation of professional networks.
