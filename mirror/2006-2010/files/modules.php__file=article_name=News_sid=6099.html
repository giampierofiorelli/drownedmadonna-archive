[Read It First Here] '' Madonna opened my eyes ''.

Here's an excerpt from a interview with a spanish hairdresser now working in Hollywood who dressed Madonna's hair for Swept Away and Die Another Day music video.Surprisingly she is named Eva Duarte, like the famous wife of Argentinian dictator Peron, portrayed by Madonna in the movie " Evita"

"Eva Duarte worked with the hair of  Penélope Cruz, Jennifer López, Cristina Aguilera and the one and only Madonna. This woman, daughter of hairdressers, who flew to London at the age of 18, thanks to her talent and to Kabbalah explained to her by Madonna, realized her American Dream. Now, she  is the owner of a popular  beauty salon in Los Angeles, in the centre of Hollywood.  Now it's Oscars time and her beauty salon it's the hottest place for the Hollywood stars. Eva took the time to talk about her daily contact with the stars of the big screen.

-How did you meet Madonna?

-it was two years ago. She called me. When I heard her voice on the phone , I did not believe it..  It was Madonna calling me from her office!  She wanted to cut her hair for her movie  'Swept away' .  When I hung the headphone, I cried. I have always admired Madonna as The artist, and this was my chance to know her in person. 

- So Eva Duarte knows the actress, that did  the other 'Eva Duarte' in 'Evita'

-Yes (laughing).  Madonna  was very surprised  because my name and my last name coincide with those  of Perón, and exactly she represented that character in the movie 'Evita'.  That  was the main theme of our first conversation and immediately she asked me if I  was born in Argentina.  She asked me to cut her hair, smoothed and curly: utilizing plates to smooth and tenacillas to cut the hair.  And she was very elegant, very " actress in Hollywood " .  Later I did for her another cut, very sexy, for the videoclip 'Die another day'. 

- Is she so brilliant in private? 

-Yes, she is spectacular.  She has a perfect body,  based on a strict diet, a lot of yoga and gymnastics. 

-And what about her temper? 

-She Is like a sky: amusing, nice and compromised with the present social problems.  She is very outspoken.  She speaks like a truck driver!  (Laughing).  I remember that when she sat down in front of the mirror she asked me, ironically,  to do a «spanish magic voodoo with me».  Wich means that I should use  my Spanish black magic to make her  good-looking.  Politically she is totally 'antibush', as it's understood in her CD 'American life'.  She is a warrior, what happens now is that she has a family and some children to protect. 

-Thanks to your  friendship with her, you discovered what is the 'kabbalah' : the Jewish mysticism

-Yes.  That was her best  gift to me, during the days in wich she visited the salon where i was working . She opened my eyes on how to change my life. She gave me as a gift the book: 'The power of the kabbalah'.  Thanks to it I opened my own beauty salon in Hollywood.  With 'kabbalah' I am happier spiritually. 

-Now, do you  continue to see each other or do you  have lost the contacts? 

-We meet sporadically in a Kabbalah center in Los Angeles.  She always greets me with a lot of affection. 

special thanks to our spanish correspondent hpmk
