Date: Sunday, September 02 @ 15:57:22 CEST
Topic: Interviews & Statements

Interview de Sandra Bernhard dans "The Guardian".

"Je ne crois pas qu'il soit nécessaire de juger Madonna"

  

La tournée de Sandra Bernhard "Everything Bad & Beautiful" commence le Dimanche 21 Octobre au Royaume-Uni à Huddersfield Town Hall.


Interview de Sandra Bernhard dans "The Observer".

"Madonna est une Lady futée"

I doubt Madonna will come to my show, though I run into her all the time, so it's not like there's any irresolvable strangeness. I like to think of the time when we were hanging out as the end of an era. We played out our friendship in the public forum, but it wasn't drunken or irresponsible airheads hanging out - she's a smart lady and I'm obviously my own brand of intellectual.

Kabbalah gives a spiritual meaning to being Jewish. It's not just religion - religion is kind of the downfall of civilisation.

I lived on a kibbutz straight after high school. They grew oranges and cotton, but my main job was cleaning chickens in the slaughterhouse. It was life changing - it gave me a lot of self-confidence and discipline to do all the years of performing, because it never really gets easier.

Cosmetic surgery isn't for me. Our culture is so obsessed with staying young, people don't mature emotionally. It's great to look young, but something within has to reflect your years of experience.

I've never set out to be shocking. Shocking is a cheap shot, and I don't think my work is cheap.

Celebrity fashion lines drive me insane. The whole face of fashion has changed; it doesn't have the same specialness. There's too much cross-pollenisation, too many regular people in on it.

I might do a line of essential oils. I've liked patchouli since I was 12. It would be earthy and tactile and fun - it wouldn't be like J-Lo and all those cheap perfumes.
