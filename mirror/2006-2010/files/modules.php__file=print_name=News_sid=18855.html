DM#02 coming to your mail box!
Date: Friday, October 26 @ 13:48:09 CEST
Topic: Magazines

After a long professional printing process, the SECOND issue of DM FANZINE is finally ready. The first copies are being shipped these day to those who pre-order their copies!! And a few copies have already reached some mail boxes!

Send your comments and pictures with your copy as soon as you receive the DM Fanzine (second issue)!

As for the previous issue, the A4 format magazine (21cm x 29,7cm) has been printed in a professional typographic office with an OFFSET printing technique in combination with a lithographic process to provides you a high quality product (consistent high image quality, longer printing plate life, etc.). 

Below you can see the typographic office, where the magazine has been printed and bound.

The cover - designed by UK graphic artist Radim Malinic from Brand Nu - has been printed in a heavy-weight paper (350gr) and then a gloss plasticization has been add as a final process to make the cover even better. Finally we wanted to add a special effect on it... don't want to discole it now!
