''Madonna Icon'' Biography to be released tomorrow in the Netherlands.
Date: Monday, October 15 @ 18:47:47 CEST
Topic: Books

Our dear friend GertJan at Madonna.nl - the Official Dutch and Belgium Madonna Funclub - wants to inform our readers that the Dutch version of the biography "Madonna Icon" by Lucy O'Brien will be released tomorrow in the Netherlands.

The local title is "Madonna Het Icoon". The book has been translated by Selma Bakker with the help of MadonnaNed / Madonna.nl.
