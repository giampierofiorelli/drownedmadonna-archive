The 'Trifecta' for Joe Henry.
Date: Sunday, October 21 @ 03:21:52 CEST
Topic: Interviews & Statements

An excerpt from a San Francisco Chronicle interview with Joe Henry:

San Francisco Chronicle: Are you the only person alive who’s worked with both Ornette Coleman and Madonna?

Joe Henry: Yes, as far as I know. I’m certainly the only person who’s worked with Ornette Coleman, Madonna and Ramblin’ Jack Elliott.

SFC: That’s the combo to have?

JH: That’s the trifecta. Anybody could hit two of those bases. The trick is to hit all three.

SFC: I’m sure Elvis Costello could give you a run for the money.

JH: We’ve actually had this conversation. He’s worked with a lot of good people, but not that particular combination.
