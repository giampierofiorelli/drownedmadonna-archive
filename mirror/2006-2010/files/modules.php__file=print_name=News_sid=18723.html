Sofia Boutella Talks About Madonna in Spain.
Date: Wednesday, October 03 @ 16:30:23 CEST
Topic: Interviews & Statements

Sofía Boutella has visited Spain during this week, promoting her new Nike Woman Campaing.

She found the time to give advise and a dance class to the contestants of the Spanish reality "Super Modelo 2007".  

During her interview with Telva Magazine, they asked her about her working experience with Madonna, this is what she said about it: "Amazing. It was really hard work, but it has helped me to know myself a little bit more, I really like Madonna because she is a tireless worker".
