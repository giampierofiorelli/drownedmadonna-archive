''I Am Because We Are'' description.
Date: Saturday, October 13 @ 03:50:27 CEST
Topic: Movies

Madonna's documentary titled "I Am Because We Are" has been PREMIERED unexpectedly during a Spirituality for Kids event at the Ilana Goor Museum in Old Jaffa, Israel. 

The documentary is very beautiful and touching, a source told Drowned Madonna. It focuses on the poor, full of suffering lives of Malawi children. It shows how AIDS effected Malawi children lives, since most of them lost their parents due to the AIDS disease and have to live on the streets.

Finally, "I Am Because We Are" shows how the Spirituality For Kids program, Raising Malawi, brought them light and hope... gives them the chance to change their future.
