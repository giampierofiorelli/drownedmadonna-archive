Madonna's Backup Singers for the Live Earth concert.
Date: Saturday, June 23 @ 19:25:05 CEST
Topic: Live Performances & Tours

After the news about the Confessions Tour dancers, Drowned Madonna has been informed that Confessions Tour backup singer Nicki Richards and Madonna's longtime backup singer Donna DeLory will perform with Madonna as backup singers at London's Live Earth concert on July 7th.
