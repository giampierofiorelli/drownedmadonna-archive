Ari Gold about having the Shabbat dinner with Madonna.
Date: Thursday, October 18 @ 13:38:28 CEST
Topic: Kabbalah

In a previous report, we talked about Madonna having the Shabbat dinner at New York Kabbalah Center with other celebrities, including singer Ari Gold. Ari Gold talks about that dinner in a new personal letter to his friends and fans he posted in his official myspace page. Here's an excerpt:

"This past Friday night my journey led me to the Kabbalah Center. I was invited to services and dinner by one of the teachers there, Michael Moskowitz, who I’ve known since my Bronx days, and he and his wife were such gracious hosts. According to Michael, I was the special male guest that night and Madonna was the female guest, celebrating Karen Berg’s birthday (with her husband, the founders of the Kabbalah Center) as well as the new moon coming out on Sabbath. As I stood there saying the silent prayer of the "Shmoneh Esreh" I couldn’t help but remark on the irony that throughout my youth I would say that prayer and dream about Madonna taking me out of the constraints of my orthodox upbringing and here it is that I had escaped it, only to be brought back all these years later to saying the same prayer with Madonna herself. I guess there really is one God after all."
