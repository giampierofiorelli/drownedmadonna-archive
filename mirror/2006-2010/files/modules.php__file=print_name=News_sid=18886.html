Madonna Mentioned in a New Song: ''La Chanteuse'' by Sandrine Kiberlain
Date: Wednesday, October 31 @ 20:59:55 CET
Topic: Music

Madonna is mentioned in the song "La Chanteuse" (''The Singer'') written by French actress and singer Sandrine Kiberlain. The song "La Chanteuse" is on her new and second album "Coupés bien net et bien carré" out this month in France. "La Chanteuse" will be the first single and a video is already done.

Lyrics of "La Chanteuse" by Sandrine Kiberlain mentioning Madonna:

"Vous trouvez pas que j'ai des faux airs
De Mylène et de Madonna ?"

("Don't you think that I look like
Mylène and Madonna ?")

Note: Mylène is Mylène Farmer.
