Has this girl learned NOTHING from Madonna?

from The Anderson Herald Bulletin:

RODNEY RICHEY: So Over It: Has Britney speared her career?

An entertainment opinion piece on the Yahoo Entertainment Web site Monday asked the question: “Is It Fair to Savage Britney’s Figure?”

This was in reference to Britney Spears’ less than stellar opening of the MTV Video Music Awards Sunday night. In clips of her performance widely seen in the media, Britney lazily and clumsily sashayed through what was supposed to be an “Oops ... I Did It Again” dance number to her new single “Gimme More.”

Britney also appeared a bit too “not thin” to be wearing the skimpy outfit she had on.

And so, the article postulated, we should not put the young woman down for looking a bit plump. After all, it sends the wrong signal to impressionable young girls.

Indeed, it does. So we should tread carefully here.

There is nothing wrong with looking the way Britney did, especially since she has given birth to two children and got VERY heavy doing it. She has managed to get herself back into a healthy shape. Good for her.

And there is certainly nothing wrong with women of size wearing tight little outfits, although they should not be surprised at the comments they might receive for flaunting their figures in unflattering wardrobe.

Now, a good friend of mine hit it on the head when he shook his head and muttered, “Has this girl learned NOTHING from Madonna?”

No, she hasn’t.

Madonna, nearing her 50s, still commands attention on the stage, still commands an impressive amount of respect, even among young fans.

She has done that by reinventing herself, changing her look, understanding where the mood of her audience might be and targeting it.

Of course, Madonna has never had the seeming emotional problems that Spears has. And if she had them, they were masked brilliantly.

Britney Spears, on the other hand, is still trying to play the coy teen vixen role, the one she began to develop in her post-“Mickey Mouse Club” years.

Trouble is, she’s not that innocent. At least not anymore.

Spears is a mom, a divorced mom, 25 years old. She’s had some undeniable problems in the past few years, and those left her with an image disconnect, one that was not helped by her retreading her tires as she did on the MTV program.
