'Celebration' video: Madonna stands the test of time by owning up to it
Category: Madonna music videos.
By Olivia Smith DAILY NEWS STAFF WRITER Madonna begins her new video with an obvious come on: "Haven't I seen you somewhere before?" The 51-year-old is still her pelvis-swirling overtly sexual self in her new video "Celebration," available for free download on iTunes until the end of the day Wednesday. But the same line, which she says to no one in particular, could also be seen as a hint that she's an artist with a past. We have seen her before, many times, in a lot of places, through major life changes and in many previous music videos. And she seems ready in her new effort, part of a greatest hits collection that by its very nature tells us she's far into her career - to acknowledge that past. Is Madonna ready to admit that even for her, there's a difference between 51 and 25? First there's the outfit. She tones it down in a ladylike (and way fashionable) pointy shouldered dress that covers her up much more than the conical bra of yore. (In fact, her current boy toy Jesus Luz, age 22, shows a lot more skin than she does - more on that later). While she's moving every minute she's on camera, she doesn't do a single choreographed dance step, leaving that to the professionals. Her maneuvers are mostly confined to the kind she likely practices at home - not in the dance studio, but in the bedroom. And they only guy she lays a hand on is real life beau Luz, in a relatively chaste scenario that puts her firmly in the driver's seat (as if it could be any other way.) While a parade of men boogey their way through the highly danceable single, it's all about the dancing here, and everyone, including Madge, is a solo act. In fact, the most blatantly sexual parts of the video are not between her Madgesty and her young courtier Luz. They're when she's all alone on screen, either grabbing her crotch in a demonstration after Michael Jackson's heart or on all fours. Seems like the ecstasy of the music is enough to get her and everyone else going, and in fact that is what the song is about. "Come join the party" the refrain says. It's "an invitation to the dance of life." The closest the video gets to any sort of non-dance consummation is when she peels off Luz's leather jacket (he's in the guise of a D.J.). The two brush lips before she pushes him away - sorry honey, back to the dance floor! Even the real dancers moves are digitally tweaked, but there are flashes of hip-hop on the way to the video's ending, a dance party in which again, everyone gets their groove on alone in the center of the crowd. One of the movers is Madonna's first-born, Lourdes - who is referred to as Lola Leon in the press release and who does a back bend in an '80s polka-dotted leotard and pants in style way before she was born. So Madge has her family - her daughter and her lover - at her latest party, in which she's still at the hotblooded center of her life.

Madonna Shows Off Sharp Dance Moves in “Celebration” Video
Category: Madonna music videos.
ROLLING STONE REVIEW Madonna’s new video for “Celebration,” one of two new tracks on her upcoming greatest hits collection of the same name, was released this morning on iTunes (it will be available as a free download for 48 hours before going up for purchase on Thursday). Director Jonas Akerlund, who previously collaborated with the Material Girl on the videos for her Grammy-winning “Ray of Light,” “Music” and “Jump,” filmed the “Celebration” clip in Milan, Italy in between dates of her Sticky & Sweet Tour. The video is pretty much what’s advertised in the title: it’s a “celebration” of Madonna’s career, with the Rock and Roll Hall of Famer proving her dance moves are still razor-sharp as she turns 51. If the DJ in the video looks familiar, it’s because Madonna’s rumored boyfriend Jesus Luz plays the role, and though she hasn’t publicly spoken about her relationship with the Brazilian model, they get mighty close in the clip. Madonna’s oldest daughter Lourdes “Lola” Leon also makes a cameo. Based on the teaser-trailers that preceded the “Celebration” video, it appeared that producer Paul Oakenfold’s wacky dance moves would be in the vid, but Oakenfold’s appearance did not survive the final cut. So why is iTunes giving away the video for free? Probably because it’s a good way to make you aware of their Celebration pre-order deals, which also opened today on Apple’s digital music service. The compilation is being offered as three packages: there’s the standard version, which features 17 Madonna hits and “Celebration.” The Deluxe Celebration includes two discs of greatest hits, “Celebration,” a “Celebration” remix and collaboration with Lil Wayne, “Revolver.” And then there’s the Premium deal, which is the Deluxe set plus 30 music videos from Madonna’s entire career. The Premium version also includes a previously unreleased bonus track called “It’s So Cool” and the uncensored “Justify My Love” video. All three Celebrations will be released September 29th.

Details on the extras shooting today in Milan for Celebrate video.
Category: Madonna music videos.

One of our community members tells what happened at today extras shooting in Milan for Madonna's Celebrate video.

"It was great experience... We were about 40 people, boys and girls.
Before we entered and we dressed up, they took some polaroid of us with first and second name under our chins, just like they do for the prisoners (laugh).
The whole song is very beautiful... catchy and fresh... it also include a spoken part. Anyway it has nothing to do with Get Together.
We danced and danced a lot... and everybody by herself/himself."

And here are the details he gave of the shoot.

"The shoot took place at Metropol. After they 'labelled' us, we signed the acquittance, etc.
They helped us to dress up and also gave us something to wear. For example, they gave some dresses to those who only have a tshirt.
A girl has been dressed up a-la-Frozen even with the wig, and a boy a-la-Nothing Really Matters.
The camera on the set were very big... one down for the close-up shoots and one up for aerial perspective.
The set was like a small part of a room, one completely white and one completely dark... First we entered one by one on the White Set dancing and then they made us enter all together dancing and jumping to the rhythm of Celebrate.
Then the same thing on the Black Set, first one by one and then all together. But here they suggested us to be more sensual.
Finally the atmosphere became hotter, not only because it was really hot due to the spotlights, but also because everyone started to encourage the one who was filmed, with applauses, screams and singing.
Madonna's staff has been very nice. They made you be at your ease, even outside the set.
It will remain an unforgettable experience, even for those who won't be in the final video.
Too beautiful to do, too hard to tell."
