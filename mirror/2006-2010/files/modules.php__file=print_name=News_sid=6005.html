Bizzare column on The Sun newspaper:

Madge twangs her G-string

Madonna is re-inventing herself again - this time as the Queen of Rock.

The undisputed pop goddess has nearly finished her next album and it will have a heave guitar sound.

I've got my hands on a secret blueprint for the new material which is out at the end of the year.
I can reveal the poppy, dance feel of the last release American Life has gone in favour of an ore edgy theme.
Madonna will even play most of the big guitar riffs herself. Her inspiration has come from the rise of British guitar rock bands. A source close to Madge told me "Living in Britain she has become a real fan of Franz Ferdinand and love the kitshy sound of The Darkness.

"She is moving more in that direction - real sounds, like a real band.

"She is close to finishing the new album and there is a real hard rock feel to it.

"Madonna always keeps in touch with what is popular in the charts and has been really into the guitar-based rock bands of 2004. The new album is taking shape as one of her best ever."

She has enlisted help from several big name producers for the project. Swedish duo Bloodshy & Avant, who produced Toxic for Britney Spears have asked to write a song.
Christian "Bloodshy" Karlsson and Pontus "Avant " Winnberg won a Grammy for Toxic just this week. Winnberg revealed Madonna called them in person to make sure they took the job - but their contribution will be less rocky than the other tracks. He said "We're going to give Madonnas sound a real disco feel".

Madge is also working with French electro whizz Mirwais and Stuart Price - the main producer. The album will include a cover of John Lennon's Imagine, which she showcased during last year's tour.

Monte Pittman, her official guitarist and teacher, has co-written a rock song called "I Love New York" ** and she will record the riffs.

Victoria Newton
