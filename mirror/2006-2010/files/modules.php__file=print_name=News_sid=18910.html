The Confessions Tour Screen in the TV Show ''Star Academy''
Date: Friday, November 02 @ 22:22:26 CET
Topic: TV

The same screen as Madonna's Confessions Tour is used in the TV show "Star Academy" on French channel TF1. This show was usually from September to December. But, because of the Rugby World Cup this year in France, it started for the first time in October (last week) and will end in... February 2008 !
Celine Dion and Kylie Minogue will be the special guests respectively on November 2 (tonight) and November 9.
