Live Performances & Tours
All began here One Year Ago.

On 1/16/2004 gossip website Gawker.com reported that Madonna was going to tour in the summer 2004.
Gawker wrote:

"Madonna World Tour 2004

Singer. Songwriter. Producer. Actress. Author. Superstar.

No matter what you call her, when Madonna hits the road this Spring for her first tour since 2001’s Drowned World Tour, you can expect an evening of high energy entertainment!

Madonna will kick of her World Tour 2004 on June 1 in San Francisco and will continue to play arenas in 32 US markets, followed by performances in 12-15 European markets as well as four dates in Israel.

The U.S. leg of the tour goes on sale nationally on March 1."

Soon the Net and media were filled with rumors and "maybe" about this possible Tour until on February 18 2004 Drowned Madonna discovered the calling for the dancers auditions for the upcoming world tour, giving for the first time the official confirmation to the news (LINK). Shortly later our World Exclusive became a public issue and the day after was stated on the headlines all over the world!

From that moment on, Drowned Madonna Team decided to inform the online community about every step of the creative process helped by some dancers that actually succeeded to be chosen by Madonna herself for the Reinvention adventure!
Doing so, we put our credibility on a risk (In fact our info have been challenged for months) because when creating a great show like The Reinvention Tour things change pretty radically day by day until the final set, as Mike McKnight confirmed (see our interviews with Mike McKnight). 

Here are some of our exclusive newsbits:

 Dancers auditions announcement

 New York dancers auditions location (S.I.R. studios)

 NY dancers auditions reportage

 Tour director (Jamie King)

 Dancers have been asked to dance combination of tango to the introduction of Die Another Day (album version)

 Dancers were taught to tango for Die Another Day from an Argentinean couple

 Madonna attended the second day of the NY dancers auditions with Jamie King and Caresse Henry

 We revealed the first date of the rehearsals in Los Angeles

 Dancers were going to do a photo shoot in LA 

 LA dancers auditions reportage

 During the LA audition, Madonna explained the dancers that Die Another Day was going to be kind of a LOVE/HATE thing, very sexy, but very fighty between Madonna and one male dancer.

 During the LA audition, Madonna was asking the dancers very unusual questions like their star sign, favourite color and stuff like that. Madonna was very funny and very kind - One of the female dancers later revealed this info to a newspaper

 At the LA auditions, the dancers used 6 feet long sticks and top hats for Hollywood.

 More old hits to be performed this time

 This time Madonna was going to be way more involved in the dancing routines... she was going full of dancing. Dancing was going to be hard very athletic. Very "Blond Ambition"... hence all the rehearsals.

 Tour book info

 Madonna was rehearsing with a guitar the Love Profusion Headcleanr Mix - Mike McKnight talked about it later during our interviews with him.

 The tour was going to have more than one costume designer

 Part of the creative team of Cirque Du Soleil was joining the creative team for the choreography of one of the sections - See the Carnivale set

 Bedtime Story was rehearsed by the dancers

 Madonna rehearsed Peter Rauhofer’s Perfect Private Life Mix of Nobody Knows Me for the show. 

 There was going to be a 20’s section of the show with Hollywood - See the Carnivale set 

 The show was going to feature girl on girl actions - See Hanky Panky choreography

 Many wigs were going to be used 

 Part of the show is very old school feel with Deeper and Deeper and Bad Girl - our dancers mistook Hanky Panky for Bad Girl

 Take A Bow was rehearsed - Take A Bow was not in the final setlist, but McKnight revealed it was on the first set

 Nothing Fails was rehearsed

 Vogue was rehearsed 

 Like A Prayer was rehearsed. They was rehearsing a more heavy version, which was more dancing too. 

 Crazy For You was rehearsed

 American Life was reheased and Madonna was going to rap in it

 Madonna and Jamie King still haven’t decided on the finale. Among the options there were Die Another Day and Holiday - Also Mike McKnight talked about it later during our interview with him

 Lament (from Evita OST) was rehearsed

 Imagine was going to be the big surprise

 We revealed The Final Setlist 

 Some details on the stage and the dancers 

 Madonna wasn’t going to tour in Germany and Italy, but more European dates were going to be added - See Holland and Portugal

 No changes in the European setlist

 The new "purple" corset revealed in real time live from Manchester

 No broadcast at VMAs from the Slane Castle

 Real time reportage from Slane Castle sound check and concert, revealing how the stage and the costumes have been changed

The magic of the re-Invention Tour wasn't over on Drowned Madonna after the tour ended in Lisbon. In fact, we interviewed Stefan Smith (Director of the visual effect of the Vogue backdrop video), Mike McKnight (Programmer/Keyboards) and Lorne Cousin (Bag-Piper), plus in November 2004 we brought you the first plans for the commercial release of the re-Invention Tour DVD, that according to our insiders was already scheduled for November 2005.

In the process we also made some mistakes and we apologize for them.
