The Irish Independent sur Les Grandes Sorties de l'Automne incluant Madonna

"The Irish Independent" annonce le nouvel album de Madonna pour Novembre et "The Beat Goes On" comme premier single.

Wednesday September 12 2007
John Meagher on the big autumn album releases, including a trio from pop's female royalty

It's the clash of the pop titans. In two months time, Madonna, Kylie Minogue and Britney Spears will release new studio albums.

Far more than mere song collections, these veritable comeback albums are likely to give a beleaguered industry a shot in the arm.

In a year in which album sales have been even lower than expected, there's a sense that all three could shift in significant quantities.

Although their music is quite different, all three will feel they have something to prove. Madonna will be anxious to return to the zeitgeist, a place she hasn't been since the Ray Of Light album almost a decade ago. Despite the chart success of Hung Up – the Abba-sampling lead single from last album Confessions On A Dancefloor – the 48-year-old American is seen as increasingly irrelevant in a youth-obsessed world. For a former trailblazer, Madonna is now seen as someone following the pack rather than leading it.

Kylie's place in the pop firmament has been damaged having been away from the scene for two years, following a diagnosis of breast cancer. Before this abruptly imposed hiatus she was in flying form, having reinvented herself as a pure pop champion. But has a traumatic illness and the resulting recuperation hurt her musical instincts?

Britney has most to prove, following a high-profile meltdown earlier this year, a disastrous performance at this week’s MTV Video Music Awards and the sense that she had gone off the boil creatively long before she parted ways with husband Kevin Federline or shaved he hair off in front of the paparazzi's prying lenses.

MADONNA

(out November)

Madonna is taking few chances with her eponymous forthcoming album. Timbaland – one of the world's most in-demand producers – has been recruited to sprinkle gold dust on proceedings. One of the tracks they worked on, The Beat Goes On, is set to be the first single and inds Madonna duetting with another acclaimed figure from the hip-hop world, Pharrell Williams.

Also leaked in advance is one of several Justin Timberlake collaborations, Candy Shop, which has been described by one UK critic as “a pneumatic sex-pop monster”.

KYLIE MINOGUE

(out late November)

Kylie has also been busy on the collaborative front, working with a slew of producers including Groove Armada, Calvin Harris, Hot Chip, Boy George and Mylo as well as veteran songwriters Bloodshy & Avant, Cathy Dennis, Jewels & Stone, Hannah Robinson, Johnny Douglas and Richard Stannard.

While some collaborations have made the cut – the Calvin Harris-produced track In My Arms is apparently in the running for the first single – others have fallen by the wayside. Another potential lead single is Two Hearts, an extremely un- Kylieish track submitted by the London electropop outfit Kish Mauve.

As is now traditional with Kylie, many other pop moments have already leaked online, along with a list of 49 song titles – including Sexual Gold, To The Nines, When The Song Comes ON and Down Down.

BRITNEY SPEARS

(out November, no date as yet set)

Britney has already caused something of a stir by inviting fans to vote online on titles for her new album. Among Britney's suggestions are OMG Is Like Lindsay Lohan Like Okay Like, What If The Joke Is On You? and Dignity. Whatever you think about the waspish former title, the latter suggests defiance in the face of adversity.

The fact that pop's former golden girl has been recording with Timbaland protege Nate “Danja” Hills and producer du jour, Sean Garrett, is of minimal interest compared to the album's backdrop of personal chaos.

Britney's career as a pop star depends on the success of this album. Fake tracks on the internet have shown that the appetite for her music remains, but with only 19 US radio stations reported to be playing her new single Gimme More in the lead up to its official release, chart success could be elusive.

ANNIE LENNOX

(out September 28)

Songs Of Mass Destruction is the first solo album from the Eurythmics singer in four years and boasts a “feminist anthem” starring 23 female artists, including Madonna, Shakira and Fergie.
