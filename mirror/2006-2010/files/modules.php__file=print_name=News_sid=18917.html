Drowned Madonna talks with Sundance Film Festival organizers.
Date: Monday, November 05 @ 20:04:22 CET
Topic: Movies

We have talked with Sundance Film Festival organizers regarding the rumours on Madonna to premiere her movie projects at next year festival.

"We are unable to confirm or deny any rumors regarding our programming for the 2008 Sundance Film Festival," they told Drowned Madonna.

"We will make the program announcement for feature-length films on the 29th and 30th of November and the short films will be announced the following week, probably on Wednesday."
