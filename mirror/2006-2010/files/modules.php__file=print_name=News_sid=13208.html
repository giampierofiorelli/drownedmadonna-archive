Danish tickets fever.
Date: Friday, April 28 @ 15:42:52 CEST
Topic: Live

Tommorrow the ticket sale will go off in Denmark, and already 130 fans are camping outside Horsens ticket center to get tickets. Also in Copenhagen people are camping outside the variuos Fona stores. UPDATE: 500 people are at the Horsens theater now.

***
Sale begins at 9:30 am.
***
The limit will be max. 6 tickets per customer.
***
Price will be dkr. 695,- + ticket fee
***
10.000 pre-printed tickets will be sold from Horsens Ny Teater
***
Another 25.000 pre-printed tickets will be sold from FONA-shops across the country.
These shops will open 30 min. before normal hours, to service tickets buyers.
When all pre-printed tickets are sold, we advise you to phone the callcenter at Billetlugen +45 70 263 267 or visit www.billetlugen.dk to order your tickets.
