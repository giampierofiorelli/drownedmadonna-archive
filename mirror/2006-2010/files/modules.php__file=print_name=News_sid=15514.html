Guy accuse Madonna d'être mauvaise perdante au scrabble
Date: Sunday, September 24 @ 11:19:20 CEST
Topic: (Private) Life

Madonna, 48 ans, qui a passé la majeure partie de son temps dans des suites d'hôtel pendant sa tournée mondiale après réception de menaces de mort de la Mafia russe, a commencé des sessions de marathon au Scrabble avec son mari mais, après une série de rangées au-dessus d'un mot triple, l'atmosphère s'est acidifiée.
 
Ils ont même essayé le jeu de Trivial Pursuit mais la rivalité intense a abouti à d'autres discussions.
 
"Une source a indiqué au Sun: Madonna n'aime pas perdre à n'importe quoi. Elle et Guy  jouaient chaque nuit au Scrabble et Madonna le prenait de loin trop sérieusement.
 
"L'atmosphère était si intense et elle était une si mauvaise perdante qu'il lui a dit qu'il ne jouerait plus avec elle.
 
"Ils ont commencé à jouer au Trivial Pursuit mais cela a été pire. Même Tiddlywinks pourrait commencer la 3ème guerre mondiale."
 
Traduction: Claudine.
 
 
As the queen of pop Madonna's entourage have come to expect a few diva-like tantrums, but husband Guy Ritchie is not standing for it as he refuses to play Scrabble with the singer because she is a 'bad loser'.
 
The couple have reportedly been arguing over the word game, while on Madonna's Confessions world tour as film director Ritchie, 38, believes she is too competitive.
 
Madonna, 48, who has spent most of her time in hotel suites during her world tour after receiving death threats from the Russian Mafia, began the marathon Scrabble sessions with her husband but after a series of rows over a triple word score the atmosphere soured.
 
They even tried a nice game of Trivial Pursuit but the intense rivalry led to further arguments.
 
"A source told The Sun: Madonna doesn't like losing at anything. She and Guy were taking on each other every night at Scrabble and Madonna was taking it far too seriously.
 
"The atmosphere was so intense and she was such a sore loser that he told her he would not play her again.
 
"They started playing Trivial Pursuit but it only made matters worse. Even Tiddlywinks could start World War III."
