Stuart Price Big Night In Barcelona.
Date: Sunday, April 24 @ 08:07:38 CEST
Topic: Music

LIVE FROM BARCELONA - A review by our reader Alan

" Barcelona turned crazy with Stuart Price at the Nitsa Club.

I've just arrived at home, and all I can say is that Stuart's night at Nitsa Club (Barcelona) was TERRIFIC!

This club is one of the most prestigious and fashionable from Barcelona, in a techno-indie style. Jean Paul Gaultier uses to go when he travel to the city.

Stuart arrived at 3 a.m. at the DJ table. He wore a red "lacoste" polo shirt. The show began with Gwen's famous hit, What you waiting for, remixed by Stuart in a catchy-retro sound, and quickly drove the audience crazy.

He also played famous hits, like Rasputin (Boney M), Mr Brightside (The Killers), Sweet Dreams (Eurythmics), many songs of the 80's and three songs from his album with Les Rythmes Digitales. All remixed with his taste of style.

Of course, Madonna's Hollywood (the mix played at the 2003 VMA's) was one of the most celebrated songs of the night.
However, he didn't play Four to the Floor, the top selling remix at the moment everywhere.

It last only 2 hours, but all the people got plenty satisfaction about Stuart's play.

The last thing to say is that... Stuart has the sexiest smile of the world!!!! "

A very special thanks to Alan.
