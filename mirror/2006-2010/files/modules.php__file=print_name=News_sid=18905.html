On the Set of ''Shadows and Fog'' with John Malkovich
Date: Friday, November 02 @ 20:59:53 CET
Topic: Interviews & Statements

Interview with John Malkovich in French magazine "TRANSFUGE".

John Malkovich is on the cover of French magazine "TRANSFUGE" (October 2007) for a big interview of 14 pages he gave to the magazine. During this interview, he talked briefly about the set of "Shadows and Fog" ("Ombres et Brouillard" in France) directed by Woody Allen in 1992 with Madonna and Mia Farrow.
