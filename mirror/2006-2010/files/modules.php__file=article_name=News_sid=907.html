[Rumours] Brand new music u-turn. Madonna wants to record a ballads album.
Mtv Italy website states that Madonna is rumoured to want to record a ballads album. Here's what they have written.

Rumours about Madonna keep on coming.
The "Remixed & Revisited" Ep is confirmed and will be released (in Italy) on November 25. It will feature 6 remixed and revisited songs plus a previously unreleased track. Now, it's rumoured that the Queen of Pop wants to record a brand new album very soon.
This release could be the nth Maddy's sound u-turn. Some unofficial sources state that she would like to record an acustic ballads album. This would reverse the "Ray Of Light" electronic u-turn.
This is not official, but it would be very interesting if the world pop goddess become a folk singer.

Note: Source: MTV Italy. Info: Marcuzio.
