Madonna & David to come back in Malawi later this month?
Date: Monday, November 05 @ 02:33:35 CET
Topic: (Private) Life

From Contact Music:

MADONNA’s adopted son DAVID BANDA will be reunited with his biological father later this month (Nov07) - more than a year after he left his native Malawi.

The pop superstar chose the two-year-old tot following a visit to an orphanage in the African country in October last year (06), and the pair will fly back to Malawi in the coming weeks to finalise the adoption process.

And although Banda’s father, Yohane, is happy his baby is no longer living in poverty, he fears his son will not be able to recognise him. He says, "I can’t wait to see my son again after a year. It will be a fantastic moment. But I have my anxieties though. Will he recognise me or his granny after all these months? It’s a surreal feeling. It’s terribly sad that David will be officially de-linked from us. But we want the adoption to go ahead in David’s best interests. When he grows up he will be a wealthy man. I may be gone by then. But I hope he will lift his other relatives out of poverty in the way that Madonna is doing for him."
