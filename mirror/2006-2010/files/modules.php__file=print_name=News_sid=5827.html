Madonna quoted in article about illegal online downloads of music.
Date: Tuesday, January 25 @ 10:30:42 CET
Topic: Music

Only a tiny mention on Madonna, but a very interesting article spread by Associated Press today.

Jamey Keaten
The Associated Press
01-25-2005
SNEP director-general Herve Rony said the industry needs to do a better job of convincing consumers that stealing music through peer-to-peer, or P2P, networks doesn't just hurt big-name artists.

"Practically all the artists are seeing a drop in sales. Every segment is getting eaten up,'' he said. "Everybody is suffering a little bit. That is what the public doesn't understand."

"If there is less money for Madonna, where do record producers get the funds to develop other artists?" he said.
