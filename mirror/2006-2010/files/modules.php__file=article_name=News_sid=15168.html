Top 100 favourite UK number one albums.

As part of the 50th Anniversary celebrations in 2006, The Official UK Charts Company asked people to vote for their favourite UK #1 albums of all time. In total they received 220,741 votes.

The Top 100 was broadcast on BBC Radio 2 on Monday August 28th, 2006 and the show was presented by Simon Mayo.
