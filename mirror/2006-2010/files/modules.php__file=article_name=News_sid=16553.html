FRANCE 2: ''Complément d’enquête'' on Madonna's Adoption Affair

As we told you, French channel FRANCE 2 did a special investigation on Madonna's adoption. They went to Malawi, interviewed many people included Yohane Banda, David's father, showing them the French magazine Paris Match with Madonna and David on the cover. They filmed the village, David's orphanage, David's bedroom and the hotel where Madonna and Guy stayed when they were in Malawi, and more...

Whitney, director's daughter, showed David's orphanage to FRANCE 2. Madonna never came there but sent her team.

Interview with Lucy Chipeta, the woman who directs David's orphanage "La Maison de l'Espoir" :
She was surprised that David has been adopted.
"I was told that people will come and visit orphanages in Malawi. So they will also visit mine. They would like to take photos of the babies because someone was interested by the adoption of a baby. And I have to tell you that the baby who should have been adopted should have been this boy, 6 months old (she hold him in her arms), instead of David. But they went around the orphanage and finally saw David in the corridor. And then I told them that I wasn't sure that David's father would agree (for the adoption) because he is his only son now. He really loves his child. He comes all the time to visit his child with his bicycle."
FRANCE 2: Madonna has chosen David on photos.

Interview with Yohane Banda :
He is embarrassed by the controversy and the publicity from it. He prefers to laugh about it. He looks at Paris Match magazine.
"Yes, that's him. I didn't make a mistake on this adoption. Now I see him (he is pleased and laughs) and one day, maybe, I will have the chance to see him again or I will go there, or he will come to the house and we will see him. I am not worried. I would like that Madonna send me photos, so that I see him growing up. I am happy she is taking care of him so well. The baby seems to be in good health. And I would like to see Madonna regularly because she is the woman who takes care so well of my child."

Interview with Asinethi Mwale, David's grandmother :
"I heard that the person who has adopted David is very rich. It is God's will to give richness to Madonna. We are so happy about all this. I want that David lives there, not here."

Interview with Maxwell Matewere, President of the association "Eye of The Child", the man who brought the adoption to court to contest it :
He attacks because the adoption has been hidden and that laws haven't been respected. He is at the head of 67 ONG of the country (Malawi) which contest the adoption.
"When we have a visitor who has done good things for us, we usually give him goats or cows to thank him for the actions that he has done. We have a law and if we don't follow this law, it means that, to thank Madonna for what she has done or the promises she has done for Malawi, we have decided to give her a child as a gift to thank her, while we are waiting for her promises or to get relationship stronger between Madonna and our country, or to be sure that she will do her projects for Malawi."
This day, he is going to the court because the procedure isn't finished and also to see a TV team from BBC.

Interview with Raphaël Tenthani, a local correspondent for English channel BBC :
He is the one who revealed Madonna's adoption affair. He covered Madonna's visit in Malawi.
"We learned Madonna's adoption by accident. People from Malawi's government have immediately confirmed the information. They said that Madonna will give money for an orphanage and will adopt a child from Malawi. But when we tried to get confirmation from Madonna's camp, they all strongly denied it. Liz Rosenberg, her spokeswoman in Los Angeles, has threatened to attack us in justice if we were going to publish an article. She denied it. This brought the story more interesting because authorities here didn't deny it and even confirmed that a procedure has been filled."

Interview with Guy Pickering, owner of the hotel where Madonna and her team stayed in Malawi :
He saw them everyday. This hotel is far from the town.
"All the group stayed here. I think it was for 9 or 10 days. She is the most famous person we received, a charming woman, with her husband. All the team was polite, kind, it was very good."
No swimming pool in the hotel. Madonna only requested white sheets and curtains. 95 dollars the night.

Interview with Yacinta Chapomba, co-founder of the association "Consol Homes" :
She is taking care of 12 000 orphans. This interview took place where an orphanage is in contruction thanks to Madonna.
"That day (when she came), yes, we enjoyed it because it was the first time we saw her, physically. Then we danced with her with many grandmothers, and even me. I was so happy."
She became a real Madonna fan. She showed the constructions (230 000 euros on 5 years). But she had the surprise to see special teachers coming for her orphans. They were from Malawi but she never saw them. They were coming from United States where they stayed 3 months.

FRANCE 2 attended a lesson in a school :
A teacher, Christopher, talks to children about the light, holding candles in the end.
"The main thing to remember today is that we have to do the things which the one who commands our hearts has decided. Can you tell us what he is telling us ? (he asks children)
- We have to listen, Emily answered.
What else ?
- We have to obey."

Interview with Christopher, a teacher :
"We encourage them to share things between their friends. They are orphans so they don't have things they might need. So we encourage them to have this spirit of sharing. That's what we want."
FRANCE 2: "Where did you learn this exercice ?"
- "I can't answer that. I teach. Maybe later. thank you."
He will refuse to tell more. In fact, Christopher and the teachers have followed Kaballah classes in USA. This is why Yacinta Chapomba is attending all school lessons.

Interview with Yacinta Chapomba :
"I'm going through and I'm looking for, and I'm watching, I want to see if there is something wrong becoming from this group. In the case I discover that, we will have to sit around a table and review our agreement because we have accepted a partnership, not to work with that group".

Interview with Thomas Ligowe, assistant at High Court in Malawi :
This is the High Court which has accepted the adoption.
He read the Malawi law and confirmed that to adopt here in Malawi, you have to be resident in the country. If not, the adoption can't be approved. There is no exception.

The paper from Bavid Banda's adoption is showed.
The law has been adapted and changed for Madonna to : "to mere physical presence in the country at the time of the petition" (it means that you only have to be in the country at the moment you are filling the papers for the adoption).

Malawi only accepts 10 adoptions a year.

Interview with Andrina Mchiela from Malawi government (woman and child developpement) :
She supervised Madonna's procedure.
"Here, adoptions always follow the same processus. Madonna or not, the processus is the same. If the question is why did you trust her, it's because she brought a big project to us. So yes, we know we can trust her. She is investing a lot in Malawi. You can trust her. But if someone will come saying I want to buy a child, no ! It's not working like that. You see the difference ?"

Conclusion :
So the adoption has been done in less than 4 months.
Yohane Banda, David's father, has got married again. All he has from his child is only 2 photos of David.
Thanks to Madonna's affair, the world discovered a country called Malawi. Today, Malawi's authorities receive a lot of adoption requests because people thinks it's easy to adopt in Malawi.

Interview with Axelle Red :
After this TV report, an interview took place live on the show with Belgium singer Axelle Red to comment this affair. She answered :
"My first impression was that she used her notoriety to have a baby faster, but, concerning the adoption, instead of criticizing Madonna, I think the child has an incredible chance and the father agrees to the adoption. She has engaged for the country, the village. My critical will be about the law which is too severe, and asks too many questions to people who wants to adopt. There are no questions for people who wants a baby naturally."
Axelle Red was a lawyer, before being a singer.
