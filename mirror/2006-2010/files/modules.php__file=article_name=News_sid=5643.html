[Exclusive Preview] ''I sent a fax to Madonna''- INTERVIEW with Jean-Paul GAULTIER !

A First look from our Team member Claudine

French stylist Jean-Paul GAULTIER is the guest of the month in  the French Cine Magazine STUDIO, issue of January 2005 (out yesterday).

 
In this interview of 5 pages, he talks about the movies  he likes, wich give  inspiration to him. The movie "FALBALAS" it's the origin of his own vocation.
At the end of the interview, the journalist Michel Rebichon asks  him if he has a Star in the movie industry (actor or actress)  who would  work only with him. He says :
"No. I don't like to have business relationships with the persons that I love."
That's why he is flattered when he sees several stars wearing his creations (Catherine DENEUVE, Nicole KIDMAN...). Then he adds :
"I am also very proud that Madonna has chosen me for her shows, and I remember walking the red carpet with her at Cannes, for "In Bed with Madonna", where we were almost crushed by the crowd. "
(Gaultier's assistant comes into the room to say that this long interview is over.)
"I would have also liked to speak to you about "Triplettes de Belleville", "La Bûche", "Un air de famille", "Ma femme est une actrice", "Lost in Translation" - which is too much fashion to me-, "In the Mood for Love"- says Jean Paul - But I will end with "Hero", from which I adored the vertiginous plastic beauty. When I came back home, I sent a fax to Madonna to ask her to go and watch  it. She did, and she was fascinated too. Yesterday evening, I saw "Le secret des poignards volants". It is so great ! I think I am going to send again another fax to Madonna..."
a very special thanks to  Claudine
