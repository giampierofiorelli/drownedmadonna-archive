Rosie O'Donnell heard the whole album and said it's great!
Date: Monday, November 05 @ 02:14:20 CET
Topic: Interviews & Statements

From MADONNAir at Iconboard:

"Hey Iconers! I just got back from meeting Rosie O’Donnell at Borders here in Miami and it was AMAZING. She is soooo sweet and looks GREAT in person. The first thing that came out of my mouth was that I was an Iconer and gave her a big hello from all of us. 

I then asked her about 'I Am Because We Are' and she said it was fanstastic and confirmed that Madonna is gonna release it at the Sundance Film Festival.

I OF COURSE then had to ask about the album and when it is to be released and she said she didn't know but that she 'Heard the WHOLE thing and it is great' and then she said that 'The stuff with Timberlake is awesome'."
