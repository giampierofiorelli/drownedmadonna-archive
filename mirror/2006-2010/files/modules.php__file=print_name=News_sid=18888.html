Christophe Willem to cover Madonna on stage ?
Date: Wednesday, October 31 @ 21:30:38 CET
Topic: Interviews & Statements

Christophe Willem was a guest in the second part of the TV show from M6 "Hits de Diamant" ("Diamond Hits") on October 19 revealing the TOP 100 of the best selling albums of the last 20 years in France. Christophe Willem sang 3 songs and talked about his upcoming tour in November. He said he will sing the songs from his first album of course but also some covers mentioning that he loves Madonna a lot... Then he revealed that his show will be in 3 parts : funky, acoustic and... dance floor ! So maybe "Hung Up" ? The answer... in November!

We remind you that the show "Hits de Diamant" is broadcast again in one part tonight.

M6 - ''Hits de Diamant'' - Again on October 31 - 08:50pm
