EXCLUSIVE: MADONNA & GUY TO ADOPT TWINS
African trip to see siblings
Heartache over orphans
By Alice Walker
 
POP queen Madonna and husband Guy Ritchie may adopt TWINS from an African orphanage next month, The People can reveal.
 
They will fly out to poverty-stricken Malawi to find a child and may even adopt either twins or ordinary siblings.
 
They will even consider kids with special needs.
 
A source close to the celebrity couple confirmed: "Originally Madonna and Guy planned to adopt only one child.
 
"But when they were told that might mean breaking up a family unit they immediately said they would take siblings or twins if that was the right thing to do.
 
"They want the children to be as young as possible but are prepared to take kids up to the age of four.
 
"And they are also willing to consider a child with special needs arising from being abandoned in an orphanage."
 
Madonna, 48, and 38-year-old British-born film director Guy will spend a week in Malawi meeting adoption lawyers.
 
If the process runs smoothly they could be the legal parents by the time they leave the country.
 
Our source added: "It will be a very hectic trip with each day packed with meetings, but they are both really cited. They understand the implications of adopting a child from another country and are confident they can provide a loving and stable home.
 
"They are determined to make sure their child or children learn about their roots."
 
The couple already have a six-year-old son Rocco together, and Madonna has a daughter Lourdes, who will be 10 next month, from a previous relationship.
 
The source said: "Both youngsters are also really excited ex about having a brother or sister - or even both."
 
Earlier this month The People revealed how Madonna and Guy hoped the adoption would help save their stormy marriage.
 
The couple decided to approach an adoption agency in Los Angeles earlier this year after struggling to conceive again naturally.
 
They have been vetted and had their parenting skills assessed. Next month's trip will be the first chance Madonna has had to visit Malawi since her sell-out Confessions tour.
 
The singer and Guy may meet Malawi's President, Bingu Wa Mutharika.
 
Malawi is one of the world's poorest countries and one million of its 13million population have HIV/AIDS.
 
Last night a spokesman for Madonna confirmed: "Madonna will go to Africa in the next six to eight weeks for her charity."
 
Source: The People.
