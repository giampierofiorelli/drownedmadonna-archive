MADONNA WANNABES TO BENEFIT GAY PRIDE 2005.
Date: Thursday, February 10 @ 18:07:12 CET
Topic: Parties

by: John Pennell,

CINCINNATI - The Pride Alive 2005 committee has announced a Pride event fundraiser called All Madonna Only Madonna Dance Party.

The event will be Feb. 26 at Jacob's bar, 4029 Hamilton Ave, at 10 p.m. The evening will be filled with all your favorite Madonna songs and dance mixes. Madonna vs. Madonna contest, a special All Madonna "Chicks with Dicks" show 10-11 p.m. and DJs Zazoo and Satori will help make the event crazy. "This is my special birthday event I throw every year," said Dawn Weiner, bartender at Jacob's bar. "Last year there were so many Madonnas as well as Divas here, you could barely get in the door." Jacob's hopes to have another event fundraiser before June. "This event last year was outrageous, but this year I'll be sure to bring a camera," said JP, Pride Alive 2005 co-chair. "Pride will benefit from a 50/50 raffle and special shots available that evening." The Pride Alive committee is still looking for someone who knows the in and outs of the entertainment world to head up an entertainment committee. The Pride Alive committee announced its new website www.prideisalive.com and picked a theme for this year's festival, "Free To Be Me" inspired by November's defeat of Article 12. Want to help? Want to show your Pride? If so, attend the next Pride Alive meeting Feb. 19 at noon at St John's Unitarian Church. To stay updated on Pride, join the new yahoo group at cincycoalitionpride-subscribe@yahoogroups.com or contact the Gay and Lesbian Community Center at (513) 591-0200. [2/10/05]
