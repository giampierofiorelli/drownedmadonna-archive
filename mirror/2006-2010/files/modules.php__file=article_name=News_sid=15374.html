All of the shows except Prague's shows were sell-outs. Prague was only 338 tickets away from selling out each night there. Also to be noted is that the per night gross in Prague was higher there than any other arena stop on this tour besides Las Vegas and Atlantic City. Madonna played to an astonishing 85,232 people in Horsens, Denmark. Madonna hasn't performed a concert to a crowd that colossal since the Girlie Show Tour!
