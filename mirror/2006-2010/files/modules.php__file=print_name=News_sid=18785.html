More exclusive details on ''I Am Because We Are''.
Date: Sunday, October 14 @ 08:31:59 CEST
Topic: Movies

"I Am Because We Are" is not the only documentary being filmed in Malawi, a source told Drowned Madonna.

In fact, there is another documentary produced by the Kabbalah Centre and directed by a US female director.

Instead, "I Am Because We Are" is a Madonna own project. Director Nathan Rissman is working on this documentary for Madonna for several months. Nathan Rissman and other persons went to Malawi many times on Madonna's behalf. Nathan stayed there for a long time and people even know him by name, Drowned Madonna has been told - "Besides the documentary for Madonna, he is also taking care of Malawi children."

As for Madonna, her trips to Malawi were also meant to be trips to collect facts and footage for her documentary, "I Am Because We Are".

As we already reported, "One of the solutions that Madonna identified to help Malawi children - as showed on the documentary - is the Spirituality For Kids/Raising Malawi program."
