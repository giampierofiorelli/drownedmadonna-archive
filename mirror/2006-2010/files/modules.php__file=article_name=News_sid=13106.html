[WORLD EXCLUSIVE] SPOILER: Drowned Madonna live from Los Angeles Rehearsals.

WORLD EXCLUSIVE - Here is a little update for our readers about Confessions show...

As we already told you, the Opening Number for Madonna's Confessions Tour is planned to be "Future Lovers". Now we can add that Madonna will sing "Future Lovers" live on stage.

As Drowned Madonna revealed weeks ago, both "Music" and "Everybody" feature a "roller-skating" choreography. Now we can add that, while "Music" is an INTERLUDE with all the disco roller skaters, "Everybody" will be actually sung by Madonna among the roller skaters.

The costumes are insane... very elaborate and of course Madonna will wear huge fashion boots.

Madonna looks absolutely amazing. We have been told that she has lost another 5 pounds and her arms are ripped.

The rehearsals are extremely intense and stressful. In the past weeks the musicians and the dancers rehearsed at different venues. Tour staff will go to the Los Angeles Forum next week to finalize the staging and to bring in the band.

The provisional setlist exclusively revealed by Drowned Madonna some weeks ago hasn't changed yet. Click here to read it.
