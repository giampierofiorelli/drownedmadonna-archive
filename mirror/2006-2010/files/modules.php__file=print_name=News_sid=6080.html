Lorenzo Ciompi talked about his experience with Madonna.
Date: Thursday, February 24 @ 00:22:57 CET
Topic: Movies

Italian actor Lorenzo Ciompi was one of the guest of Italian tv show "Cronache Marziane" hosted by Fabio Canino. Lorenzo had a small role in Guy Ritchie's "Swept Away".

Canino said that Lorenzo "did a movie with Madonna, but nobody saw it... a very bad movie". The hoster also said that Madonna is very talented, but she should do what she does best. Since her role was originally played by Italian actress Mariangela Melato and it was one of her best acting, Canino stated that Madonna shouldn't have played this character.

Then the hoster asked Lorenzo Ciompi to admit that this remake was very bad. However, Lorenzo said that there are many scenes where Madonna is very good and he likes so much the director, Guy Ritchie. So he is very proud of being part of the cast.

Finally, Canino asked Lorenzo about Madonna. Lorenzo said that she is very professional... she used to wake up at 6am for jogging... but at the same time she is very kind and easy-going. He said that when he met her for the first time he clasped her hand too strong and she made fun of this. Then Madonna asked him many personal questions.
