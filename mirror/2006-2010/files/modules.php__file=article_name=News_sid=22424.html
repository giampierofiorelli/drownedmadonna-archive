Madonna face of Dolce And Gabbana
Category: Fashion.
Press Associated Madonna will be swapping music for modelling once again, as she becomes the latest cover girl for Dolce And Gabbana. The Italian fashion duo - who designed iconic costumes for her Girlie Show and Sticky And Sweet tours in 1993 and 2008 - announced on their online magazine, Swide.com, that the Queen of Pop is the face of their spring/summer 2010 campaign. "To have Madonna in our campaign is a dream come true," said designers Domenico Dolce and Stefano Gabbana.
