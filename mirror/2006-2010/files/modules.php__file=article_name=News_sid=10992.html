EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW - ELLE
 
Madonna Forever - The Woman Behind The Star

This interview took place at the"Mandarin Oriental" hotel in London, suite 220-222.
 
- Madonna is an artist who is telling us : yes, there is a life after being 40 years old. It's possible to last. Well, that's what I'm trying to explain to her... And here is her answer.
So I am a third sex ? "Interesting". I take it as a compliment.
 
- Where is this energy coming from ?
Frankly, life gives me back what I give to it.
 
- From gym courses ?
Not really, I broke bones in a horse accident 3 months ago.
 
- How did you survive ?
To my accident ?
 
- No, to your celebrity.
When you grow up in this world, when you are a star, when you do entertainment, nobody is telling you that all this is only an illusion and that, in the end, means nothing. Nobody explains to you that it's a temporary happiness. On the contrary, they push you to ask more. And they give you more. At this moment, you are trapped because you are thinking that it's true, that it's reality. The only way to survive to all this is to get out of it. That's the story.
 
- I guess that it took time to get to this conclusion.
Yes, a lot of time. To have children, get married, discover a life more spiritual helped me to see what is important and true. The mistake, it's to think that you are the start of everything around you. If you think that you are the Creator, you are dead. I know that I'm not the owner of my talent. I am "the manager". And if I manage it well, it will continue to circulate. Like money.
 
- Is it Kabbalah which learns that ?
Kabbale is coming from the word "Kebel" which means receive. Kabbalah says that everything you do, everything you say, has an impact on the world. Once you realised this, you become more responsable. You think twice before doing something, and all your vision of life is changed.
 
- You are initiating your children ?
Yes, I say to my daughter that she has two voices above her head. The "bad guy" who is, to resume, our ego. And the "good guy", who is connected to the others. I explain to her that when she is not nice with someone, it's like she is not nice with herself. I try to teach her simple things.
 
- That should not be easy to be Madonna's daughter... nor her husband, moreover !
No. But I think that Guy [Ritchie, the director] knows that all is happening around him is the result of what he is doing and not the reason. I am experimenting celebrity since more time than my husband and I did more provocative things. Now that he is doing it too, I tell him : "So, what is it to push people ? What is it to make them furious ? You know what I'm through now." But I have a lot of respect and compassion for his way to get along with this.
 
- And your daughter ?
It makes her mad when people try to be friend with her only to ask her questions about me. For her, I'm her mother and that's all. That should not be easy but I trust her ; she is a good judge. And she goes to French school, here, in London. She speaks French better than me !
 
- You are living in London ?
Because of love. But I often go to New York and Los Angeles for work. I am a gypsie.
 
- Are you still loving France ?
Always as much as before. I have real friends there. But I have a special connexion with New York, like with a first lover. You see what I mean ? [She seems moved.] It's where I tasted independance, made my dreams come true, became an artist. You know, I fought a lot in New York, I suffered a lot in New York. [Suddenly, she has tears in her eyes, maybe because of being tired.] I had to manage myself all alone there. And when you go through this, you never forget it. [She is controlling herself.] Today, it has been twenty years that I am under the public eye. And I want to say : stay armed, believe in what you are doing, no matter what people says. Go !
 
- Do you have regrets ?
Not one. I only have hopes.
 
She gets up. It's 09:00 PM. The pop star day is over. She went away.
A star is reborn.
