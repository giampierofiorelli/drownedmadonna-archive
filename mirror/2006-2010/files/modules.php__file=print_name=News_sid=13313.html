Confessions t-shirt celebrating Bay Area stop.
Date: Wednesday, May 03 @ 09:55:51 CEST
Topic: Live

You probably remember "MadonnaSummer" t-shirt designed by our friend Kramm for the re-Invention Tour. Madonna's Confessions Tour is coming, so Kramm has something new for you. Read what he has to tell you...

In 2004 I designed a t-shirt to celebrate Madonna's Re-Invention Tour stop in the San Francisco bay area, my home. It was a navy blue shirt with "MadonnaSummer" written in red across the front. While the tour was here, someone from Madonna's entourage picked it up and gave it to her. She then wore it in New York and you can imagine my surprise when I saw the pictures. I contacted her assistant and was told that Madonna "loved it".

So, for the Confessions Tour, I decided to design a brand new shirt to once again celebrate her stop in the bay area. This time around, it's black with the graphic in metallic gold. We're also planning to put up "Madonna theme" store windows shortly, so either come by if you're in the area or check back here real soon for pictures.
