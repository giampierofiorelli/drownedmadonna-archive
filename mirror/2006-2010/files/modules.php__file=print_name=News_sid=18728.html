Annie Lennox talks again about ''Sing''.
Date: Thursday, October 04 @ 15:40:59 CEST
Topic: Interviews & Statements

An excerpt from an interview with Annie Lennox published on SignOnSanDiego:

SignOnSanDiego: You assembled the masses of female fame for "Sing" — Madonna, Dido, Shakira, Fergie, Celine Dion, among others. Tell us about that.

Annie Lennox: I wanted this song to be a vehicle to raise awareness of the pandemic of HIV/AIDS, in (South) Africa specifically. Millions of people are being absolutely decimated. Nelson Mandela has described it as a genocide. I thought, people know my name and my music, but if I could get other artists to endorse this cause, that would be a far more powerful and effective way of spreading the word.
