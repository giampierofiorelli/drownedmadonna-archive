''Greatest Remix'' for Madonna?
Date: Wednesday, December 08 @ 16:55:02 CET
Topic: Interviews & Statements

FIRST PUBLISHED ON A MADONNA WEBSITE

In an interview with Italian press, DJ Giangi Cappai declared yesterday that his remix of Easy Ride, made along with Tracy Young,  from the American Life album will be released soon on a new Madonna collection,   "Greatest Remix", wich "will feature the less successful songs in a new formula, new rhythms and arrangements".

The italian DJ also claimed that his collaboration with Madonna isn't over after the Easy Ride remix

"I have signed a contract with Warner now, it includes dance remixes for one of the songs that will be featured on  new Madonna's CD. I'll do one of the remixes all by myself and one along with Tracy".
