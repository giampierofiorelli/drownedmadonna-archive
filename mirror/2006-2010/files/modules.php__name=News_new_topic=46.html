Madonna urges fans to please donate to Red Cross.

The Tsunami in Southeast Asia has cost over 230,000 deaths. On January 16, 2005 Madonna performed John Lennon's Imagine at Tsunami, A Concert of Hope Event which has raised over 18 million dollars. Please continue to give to this cause. You can donate by going to the American Red Cross by calling 1-800-HELP-NOW or going to http://www.redcross.org/. Or you can donate by downloading Madonna's performance from the Tsunami, A Concert of Hope by going to www.connect.com/tsunami  . To see pictures from Madonna's performance, go to the image gallery and choose the "A Concert of Hope Event Pictures".

Photos courtesy of NBC.
source: Madonna.com
