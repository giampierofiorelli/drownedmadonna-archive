New friends at Drowned Madonna.

We are glad to announce that our friend Joey Alvarado, who has just launched his own entertainment company, TRUST! ET, is having his first DJ spinning this Wednesday Night in New York featuring Andre Shannon.

ANDRE SHANNON
Andre Shannon has been a professional DJ for over 5 years playing in cities as diverse as New York, Denver, Montreal, San Francisco and Toronto.
For now being a DJ is fun for Andre but it’s not the only thing on his mind. “Being a DJ is only the start for me. Jack Nicholson said it best in Batman, “Wait till they get a load of me.”

Andre’s Top 10 List
1) Sizequeen - "Tribal Madness" Airmale remix
2) Utada - "Devil Inside Me" Scumfrog mix
3) Madonna - "Mother and Father" Peter Rauhofer’s Re-Invention mix (not released, he doesn’t have it but we’ve heard Peter play it and we love it)
4) Midnight Society and Jade Elektra - "How Do I Look"
5) Britney Spears - "Outrageous" Friscia and Lamboy mix
6) Offer Nissim - "Searching" Ohzee and the Butcha mix
7) Narcotic Thrust - "I Like It" Coleman dub
8) Joi Cardwell - "Soul 2 Bare" Peter Bailey Mix
9) Harry "Choo Choo" Romero - "What Happened" Who Da Funk mix
10) Shapeshifters - "Lola’s Theme" original mix

On October 13th Andre will be spinning at the newly renovated G Lounge on 19th St between 7th and 8th aves in New York.
Time 6pm - 1am
