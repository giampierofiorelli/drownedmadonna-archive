Madonna, Donna Karam, Ari Gold and Guy Oseary have the Shabbat Dinner in NYC.
Date: Monday, October 15 @ 05:13:11 CEST
Topic: (Private) Life

Madonna attended the Shabbat dinner with fashion designer Donna Karan, singer Ari Gold and her manager Guy Oseary - among the others - at the Kabbalah Center in New York City last Friday night.
