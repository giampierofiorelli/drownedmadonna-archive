6th Annual Madonnathon Party In NYC Tonight

Elizabeth Cline, NYMag

Madonnathon: 6th Annual Madonna Birthday Bash

August 15, 2007
8:00pm

B.B. King Blues Club & Grill
237 W. 42nd St., New York, NY 10036
nr. Seventh Ave.
212-997-4144

Madge is turning 49! Pay homage with this annual freakfest of gay, topless, country, folk, and reggae send-ups (plus a few spot-on renditions) of old-school M. We’re most eager to see barely clad queens the Dazzle Dancers and evangelical chanteuse Tammy Faye Starlight strike their poses backed by the live band, the Material Boys. Between acts, a D.J. will spin … Madonna! Show up in the perfect crucifix, fishnets, and beauty mark, and you might even earn a chance to vogue onstage.

To purchase tickets online: ticketmaster
