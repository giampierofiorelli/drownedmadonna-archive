Madonna's Guy Ritchie To Be Best Man At Brad Pitt's Wedding?
Date: Sunday, January 29 @ 15:29:40 CET
Topic: (Private) Life

Guy Ritchie has reportedly agreed to be best man at Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie's wedding, according to a published report. The arrangements were reportedly made during a dinner at the Locanda Locatelli in Mayfair on Wednesday night.

Pitt apparently admitted that he hopes to marry Angelina as early as next month. "All the talk was of the forthcoming nuptials," a source told the Sunday Mirror. "And then Brad said to Guy he would like him to be best man. Guy was absolutely thrilled. He thinks it's a real honor."There has been mounting speculation that the pending - and depending on who you talk to - imminent nuptials, might actually occur outside the US. Recent reports indicate that London could be the place and with the recent news that Madonna's Guy would be best man, it only further the theory
