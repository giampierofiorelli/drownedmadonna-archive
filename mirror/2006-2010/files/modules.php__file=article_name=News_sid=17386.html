''Hung Up'' sung at the Enfoirés yesterday evening

French singers Patricia Kaas and Francis Cabrel sang some lines from "Hung Up" in a medley at the Enfoirés, "La caravane des Enfoirés 2007", broadcasted yesterday evening on French channel TF1. This medley is already available on sale today in CD here and DVD here. "Les Enfoirés" give million meals for free each year and since more than 20 years to people in need. So please do not share the song but buy it.
