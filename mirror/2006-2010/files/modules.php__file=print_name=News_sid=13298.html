As we already reported, our friend Val, along with Kat and ATC, had the chance to see yesterday's rehearsals at Los Angeles Forum.

Paradise [Not For Me] was sung yesterday as a DUET with Yitzhak Sinwani, the guy who sings on "Isaac". He sang in a different language (it wasn't the usual French part that is in the actual song.. it could be French, but it was definitely different lyrics) to one part, Madonna sang her own part, then they sang together at one point with one doing the "I've... been so high" part and the other singing the "I can't remember.. when I was young". it was very acoustic and sounded REALLY really good!
