Exclusive interview with Moto Blanco.
Date: Friday, October 19 @ 17:37:31 CEST
Topic: Interviews & Statements

After holding an interview with Dean Coleman, we also chat with Moto Blanco about their remix of Annie Lennox "Sing" featuring female vocalis such as Madonna. Click the banner below to read the interview.

"She (Annie Lennox) asked for the spoken vocal parts to be included in our versions," Moto Blanco tell Drowned Madonna. "When we started the mix we didn’t know Madonna was involved until we played her verse...... We both went WOW!! We’ve never remixed her before, this is the closest we’ve got!!"
