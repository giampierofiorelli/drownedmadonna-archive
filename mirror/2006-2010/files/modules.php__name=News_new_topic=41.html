A-ROD WENT OVER THE 'BORDERLINE'

By CAROLYN SALAZAR in Miami and SAMUEL GOLDSMITH and DAN MANGAN in NY

July 8, 2008 -- Sexy singing siren Madonna stole A-Rod's heart, fueling the obsessive infatuation that helped doom his marriage, a lawyer for his wife said yesterday upon filing divorce papers.

The married Madonna - famous for a long list of lovers in her wild, younger years - captivated the Yankee star to the point that their "relationship" was "the last straw" for his long-suffering wife, Cynthia, said C-Rod's lawyer, Earle Lilly.

Cynthia Rodriguez filed for divorce on the grounds that "the marriage of the parties is irretrievably broken because of the husband's extramarital affairs and other marital misconduct," the lawsuit states.

That "misconduct" presumably includes Alex Rodriguez's relationship with Madge, which his wife says involves the Jewish mystic philosophy kabbalism - and the pop star playing the role of teacher to A-Rod, her doe-eyed, "brainwashed" student.

Lilly told People.com that that oddball matchup between the singer and slugger "is an affair of the heart."

He insisted his side isn't claiming that Madonna and A-Rod have had a sexual fling.

"The correct analysis is a 'relationship,' " Lilly said. "Some people categorize an affair as something as sexual infidelity. We're not claiming that."

A-Rod's obsession with all things Madonna includes listening only to her music while driving, dumping his longtime sports agent for her manager - a kabbalah practitioner - and the three of them enjoying private Friday-night Sabbath dinners together, sources have said.

All of these things were the final insult for his wife, who has suffered through a string of extramarital affairs by her All-Star hubby during their 5½ years of marriage, Lilly said.

"She's just a Greek Orthodox lady who has had as much as she can take," Lilly said.

Cynthia's lawsuit demands she get the couple's $12 million mansion in Coral Gables, Fla., as well as its contents, plus alimony, custody of their two young daughters, child support, her Maybach luxury car and lawyers' fees.

The suit, filed in Miami-Dade County court, suggests that C-Rod also should land up to half of the more than $140 million Alex has earned during their six-year marriage.

"The petitioner has exhausted every effort to salvage the marriage," the suit said. "However, Alex has emotionally abandoned his wife and children and has left her with no choice but to divorce him."

Cynthia, 35, revealed in the divorce complaint that she and Alex signed a prenuptial agreement on Oct. 3, 2002, a month before their marriage.

But the suit suggests that Cynthia wants that prenup tossed.

Philadelphia divorce lawyer Lynne Gold-Bikin, who is not involved in the case, said it is significant that Cynthia filed for divorce in Florida, because that state's judges are more apt to toss out prenuptial agreements than those elsewhere. A prenup can be ruled invalid in Florida if a judge decides its terms are not "fair or reasonable," she said.

The couple has two daughters, 3-year-old Natasha and 2-month-old Ella.

Last night, A-Rod seemed to be reveling in his impending bachelorhood as he held court with two male pals at the Four Seasons Hotel in Midtown. A steady stream of beautiful young women approached the table to wish him well.

"He was very friendly. It's like he didn't have a care in the world," said Wendy Diamond, a judge on the upcoming CBS-TV show "Greatest American Dog" who witnessed the exchanges.

One spy said the athlete and his pals sipped tea and discussed women and possible new residences for him.

His lawyer, Ira Elegant, did not return a phone call.

Elegant told the Miami Herald that he plans to file a counter-petition after he returns from a trip to Turkey next week.

Madonna, 49, had no comment as she walked out of her Central Park West building, where actress Gwyneth Paltrow paid a visit during the day.

She has said she did not have a romance with 32-year-old slugger.

Madonna is not mentioned by name in C-Rod's suit.

Also left unnamed were any other women with whom A-Rod had a relationship - including busty blond stripper Joslyn Morse, who last year was sharing hotel rooms around North America with the Yankee third basemen.

TMZ.com reported Madonna and her hubby, director Guy Ritchie, have been planning the media frenzy surrounding her purported affair with A-Rod for months in a bid to raise her profile and sell more tickets for her upcoming "Sticky and Sweet" world tour.

Those sources also said that Madonna's marriage is for all intents over and that the couple has agreed to an "official split" in November of December, after her big-bucks concert tour.
