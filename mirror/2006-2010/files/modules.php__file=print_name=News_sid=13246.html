More on Coachella.
Date: Sunday, April 30 @ 02:03:32 CEST
Topic: Live

Today Madonna has rehearsed at Los Angeles Forum the numbers she is going to perform tomorrow at Coachella Festival.

As you can see from the setlist we previously posted, Madonna will do all of KoKo/G-A-Y plus "Ray Of Light".

"I Love New York" will be longer and "Ray of Light" will be performed in a very rocky version... totally f*cking cool. In the middle of "Everybody" she says "your E should be taking effect right now".
