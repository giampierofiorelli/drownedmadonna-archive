''Alone Again'' written by Madonna and sung by Kylie appears in Kylie new Documentary!
Date: Wednesday, October 17 @ 02:37:57 CEST
Topic: Music

There were a lot of rumours about a song called "Alone Again" written by Madonna and Rick Nowels in the "Ray of Light" sessions which was scrapped and given to Kylie Minogue for her "Fever" album but it didn’t make the album because it was most likely too late to add the song, or Kylie or her label didn’t want it on the album. Then, there were rumours about "Alone Again" being planned to be the double a-side along "Come Into My World" or a b-side to the single but it didn’t happen.

Anyway, the song exists and it's not official that it has been given to Kylie and she recorded it.

In fact, "Alone Again" appears in Kylie’s new documentary, "White Diamond" and Madonna is also thanked in the credits of the film for the song.

Here's what our reader and Community member Dhp23 report about a special screening of the Kylie documentary:

"I’m just back from a sceening of the documentary in Edinburgh. A small part of the song was played over a scene where Kylie was talking about her ex, Olivier Martinez.

Can’t remember much of the lyrics to be honest, but it sounded nice. It wasn’t till I saw the end credits that I realised it was written by Madonna and Rick Nowels. 

Kylie also gave a special thank you in the credits to Madonna and a few other poeple."
