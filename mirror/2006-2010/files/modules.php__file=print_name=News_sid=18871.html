Madonna in ''Hits de Diamant'' with ''Ray of Light''
Date: Tuesday, October 30 @ 00:00:09 CET
Topic: TV

The TV show "Hits de Diamant" ("Diamond Hits") on French channel M6 revealed the TOP 100 of the best selling albums of the last 20 years in France. This chart was broadcast in 2 parts on M6 on October 13 and 19 and is broadcast again in one part on October 31. Madonna is just at the beginning of the show with "Ray of Light" at 98. "Hung Up" is also in the music opening and closing this TV show.

N°98 - MADONNA - "Ray of Light" with 925 400 albums sold in France

M6 - ''Hits de Diamant'' - Again on October 31 - 08:50pm
