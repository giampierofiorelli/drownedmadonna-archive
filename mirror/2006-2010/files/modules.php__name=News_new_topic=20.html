Madonna's Sticky & Sweet Tour to be released on DVD, Blu-Ray and CD on March 30th!
Category: Madonna live.

This is an official press release from Live Nations:

Madonna’s ‘Sticky & Sweet’ Concert To Be Released March 30th On DVD, Blu-Ray And CD

LOS ANGELES, CA -- January 12, 2009 -- Madonna’s most recent stage extravaganza, the “Sticky & Sweet” concert, will be released on March 30th, it was announced today by Live Nation, the world’s largest live music company. “Sticky & Sweet”, which was produced by Live Nation, was the most successful tour by a solo artist in history and was seen by over 3.5 million fans in 32 countries around the world.

The show, which will be distributed by Warner Bros Records, will be available on DVD, Blu-Ray and CD and will include many of the Material Girl’s hits from the course of her unprecedented career including, “4 Minutes,” “Like a Prayer,” “Hung Up” and “Ray of Light”.

Filmed in Buenos Aires, over four days to a crowd exceeding 256,000 fans, the concert also includes a show stopping moment when Madonna performs a historic “Don’t Cry For Me Argentina” to a thunderous audience response from her Argentine fans. Also included are several hits from the multi-Grammy winner’s most recent CD “Hard Candy” which debuted at No. 1 in 37 countries.

The DVD also includes 30 minutes of exclusive footage filmed behind the scenes during the course of the tour.

“Part concert, part block party and all fun, there is still only one Madonna,” raved the Oakland Press in their review of the “Sticky & Sweet” Tour. “A night of triumph and defiance… A two hour multi-media juggernaut,” glowed the LA Times. “Our lady of perpetual motion…This may be the best tour she’s ever stitched together,” claimed the NY Post.

MADONNA wraps the most successful solo tour of all time
Category: Madonna live.
from MADONNA.COM LOS ANGELES – September 2, 2009 – Madonna’s “Sticky & Sweet” Tour made history as it wrapped up last night with two final shows in Tel Aviv, Israel at Yehoshua Gardens (HaYarkon Park, September 1 and 2). The Material Girl more than lives up to her name, as her tour becomes the most successful tour ever by a solo artist. After playing 85 shows since its launch in August 2008, Madonna’s “Sticky & Sweet” has drawn more than 3.5 million fans in 32 countries and has grossed $408 million, it was announced today by Live Nation, the tour’s global producer. “Sticky & Sweet” is also the second highest grossing tour of all time. Madonna set an attendance record for the biggest crowds in history in Zurich, Switzerland, where she drew the largest audience ever assembled for a show in that country (72,000). Her Gothenburg, Sweden show, seen by 119,000 fans, broke the record for highest grossing multiple shows in the country’s history. In Helsinki, Finland, “Sticky & Sweet” sold more than 85,000 tickets, making it the biggest single show by an artist in history for all of Scandinavia. Other highlights on the tour included stops in Buenos Aires, Argentina (256,000), Santiago, Chile (146,000), Sao Paulo, Brazil (203,000), Tallinn, Estonia (72,000), Warsaw, Poland (79,000), Bucharest, Romania (69,000), and Sofia, Bulgaria (54,000). “With ‘Sticky & Sweet’ Madonna has proven yet again that there is virtually no limit to what she can accomplish both creatively and commercially,” said Arthur Fogel, Live Nation’s Chairman of Global Music and CEO of Global Touring. “Sticky & Sweet” broke Madonna’s previous record of most successful tour by a solo artist with her 2006 Confessions Tour, which grossed $194 million. Madonna’s upcoming CD, “CELEBRATION” – the definitive Madonna collection – is scheduled to be released at the end of September.

Madonna speaked out against discrimination in Bucharest. And she was booed!
Category: Madonna live.

Thousands of fans in Bucharest react after Madonna speaked out at concert against discrimination of people traditionally known as Gypsies.

At first, fans politely applauded the Roma performers sharing a stage with Madonna. Then the pop star condemned widespread discrimination against Roma, or Gypsies, and the cheers gave way to jeers.

The sharp mood-change that swept the crowd of 60,000, who had packed a park for Wednesday night's concert, underscores how prejudice against Roma remains deeply entrenched across Eastern Europe.

“It has been brought to my attention ... that there is a lot of discrimination against Romanies and Gypsies in general in Eastern Europe,” Madonna said. “It made me feel very sad.”

Thousands booed and jeered her.

A few cheered when she added: “We don't believe in discrimination ... we believe in freedom and equal rights for everyone.”

But she got more boos when she mentioned discrimination against homosexuals and others.

Source: The Globe and Mail.

Scandinavia ready for Madonna!!!
Category: Madonna live.

Here is a huge bulk of information from some of the biggest papers in Scandinavia. Norway is a goldmine when it comes to details about the tour and things around it.

Madonna in Norway, Sweden and Denmark

Madonna is going to play in Oslo on Tuesday and Thursday, and is expected to fly in from London on Tuesday afternoon with a private Jet-plane. She was supposed to stay in Oslo for five days, but canceled her reservations at Grand Hotel last week, and will fly back to London after the show, and return on Thursday. Source: http://www.vg.no/rampelys/artikkel.php?artid=564323.

50 people from Oslo started the hard work on building Madonna's gigantic stage last Thursday evening when 15 trailers arrived with the stage. Over the weekend an additional 36 trailers arrived with sound and lightning. According to VG, only Rollng Stones, U2 and Pink Floyd has brought such huge productions to Norway before Madonna. 130 Norwegians are working on building the stage on Monday and Tuesday. The stage is 65 meters wide, and has a weight of about 400 tons! 25 of the 350 people in Madonna's entourage are stage builders.

Verdens Gang also tells about the tragic accident in France, where two men died after Madonna's stage collapsed. "The death stage was going to Oslo after Marsielle!" http://www.vg.no/musikk/artikkel.php?artid=564039.

Unfortunately, they read too much crap fron Swedish tabloids. Aftonbladet in Sweden has published new pictures of Madonna's frightening arms! They talk about anirexia nervosa, and as always, a doctor has spoken after ´looking at the picture, saying Madonna could suffer from Anorexia, an infection, poison struma (Hyperthyroidism) or cancer! Anyway, Verdens Gang has all about it in today's net edition. http://www.vg.no/musikk/artikkel.php?artid=564863

About 80.000 people will attend at Madonna's two concerts in Oslo, the first ones for her in Norway. And those of you who don't want to see Madonna tonight, you can also choose between Lady Gaga and Metallica. Tonight, Oslo is the city of stars... Source: http://www.vg.no/musikk/artikkel.php?artid=564467

Fans in Denmark can fight over 1000 extra tickets! The arranger of the concert has recieved all details about the stage, and come to the conclusion that 1000 more people can come to the show. All standing room only. The tickets were made available at 11.00 tuesday, and are most probably gone by now. Source: http://www.billetlugen.dk/musik/7335/

And what about Sweden? Well, if you live in Stockholm, you can catch a special Madonna train! Old t-shirts, party and lots of Madonna music is promised. Trains are scheduled for both the 8th and 9th of August. See link for details http://www.sjevent.se/.

After the accident in Marsielle, some people were worried about the effect it might have in Gothenburg, but the concerts will go on as promised. The gruond under Ullevi stadium will be reinfirced with steel pellars, as Madonna's stage is quite heavy. Ullevi's staff takes safety very seriously after the near collapse in 1985, when Bruce Springsteen and a very enthusiastic crowd almost rocked the stadium to pieces over two nights.

Furthermore, Swedish papers reports about the video for Celebration, which will be directed by long time collaborator Jonas Åkerlund. They also mention the Greatest Hits collection due for release in late September.

More to come over the following couple of weeks...
