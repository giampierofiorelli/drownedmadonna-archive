ALL ABOUT MADONNA IN PARIS
 
 
- Télé Loisirs - Cheeses and red wine for Madonna in Paris
French magazine Télé Loisirs talked about the promotion Madonna made in Paris : Star Academy, interviews for TF1, France 2, NRJ Radio, M6 and also her video chat with fans on Wanadoo website.
 
November 10 
- 03:00 PM
Madonna arrived in Paris without her family and went to Crillon hotel.
The first thing she did was to record there the video chat for Orange/Wanadoo and then the FRANCE 2 interview.
The interviews for M6 and TF1 were recorded before in London.
- 09:00 PM
She went to the VIP Room for the NRJ interview with Manos.
450 persons were there, including the writer Paul-Loup Sulitzer and the actress Charlotte de Turckheim.
After this interview, she stayed for a while, listening to the DJ Archigram. She specially wanted him to be there.
She danced too. Then she went to the restaurant quai Voltaire with her dancers and manager.
 
November 11
- 03:30 PM
She went to La Plaine-Saint-Denis to record the Star Academy show.
Her only one request was to have French cheeses and red wine.
During her performance, she went twice close to the student Jean-Luc. She talked to him after : she asked him who he was, if he was a singer, and what kind of music he was doing.
Madonna's effect to the show : more than 8,3 millions of persons watched it !
 
- Aujourd'hui en France - Behind the scenes of the recording of Star Academy
Friday, November 11th - Studio Carrère, la Pleine-Saint-Denis, Paris
 
- 03:30 PM
Madonna didn't accept to be live on the show in the evening but has accepted that 600 persons attended to the recordings of her songs earlier in the afternoon. It was a special public, mostly fans, not the one in the evening.
- 06:45 PM
Madonna was there, rehearsing "Hung Up" and not in silence... And Madonna encouraged it.
She did 3 rehearsals and 3 recordings. Each recordings were cut by long talks between Madonna's staff and the Star Academy's staff. All in a crazy mess... But Madonna was there, only this counted, and she stayed later than it was envisaged at first. She was smiling more and more as time went by, happy to stay longer here. She even went close to the student Jean-Luc during the 2 the last recordings... and looked at him carefully when he sang "Like A Prayer" for her... She even said goodbye to him when she left the studio.

LE FIGARO TV Magazine
LE FIGARO :
- Four years to wait for Madonna and 1 000 mails sent and phone calls since last May to make Madonna come to Star Academy.
- Record companies have understood that this is the only show for a phenomenal promotion for an artist.
- She recorded her performance and has left for Germany.
- In this newspaper features also a picture of Luc Besson (currently in Marseille) wearing a t-shirt promoting Arthur.
TELE Z : still mentionning this show
 
Note : Madonna has even accepted to record several ads speaking in French for the show. It was unexpected even 15 days ago that she would have done it because she never did it before.
