Madonna upcoming World Tour.
Date: Tuesday, November 08 @ 13:28:02 CET
Topic: Live Performances & Tours

Drowned Madonna can reveal few info regarding Madonna's upcoming world tour.

As for the 2004 re-Invention Tour, Madonna will rehearse from late February 2006 to May 2006. The first show is set to take place at the end of May 2006.

As already rumoured, Madonna would REALLY like to tour Japan.

Keep in mind that this is not confirmed yet, and the agenda is subjected to change.
