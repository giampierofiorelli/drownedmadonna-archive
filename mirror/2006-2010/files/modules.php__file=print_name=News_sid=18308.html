More on the duet with Annie Lennox.
Date: Saturday, June 30 @ 01:56:39 CEST
Topic: Music

On May the first, Matt at www.poolparty.com chose Drowned Madonna as the only Madonna's website to spread the world about the rumours he heard regarding an Annie Lennox duet with Madonna for Annie upcoming album.

Yesterday, Annie Lennox revealed the title of her new album: "Songs of Mass Destruction".

Today, once again Matt has chosen Drowned Madonna as Madonna's news-site to add more... Here's what he has to report:

"Annie Lennox plans to release her new album "Songs of Mass Destruction" this fall. The track "Sing" calls for the implementation of a program to stop the transmission of mother-to-baby HIV.

She asked 23 of the world’s best known female artists to sing with her and every one said yes, including Madonna, Celine Dion, Gladys Knight, Joss Stone, Shakira, Fergie, P!nk, Faith Hill, Melissa Etheridge and K.D. Lang.

Annie will make the track available on the Internet, with all download profits going to support Treatment Action Initiatives (TAC)."

Matt has more info for our readers... The inspiration for the track was Annie’s trip to Africa and her involvement with TAC. Working with such high profile artists would bring added publicity to the cause. The track "Sing" includes:

Madonna, Joss Stone, Celine Dion, Dido, Gladys Knight, k.d. Lang, Angelique Kidjo, Bonnie Raitt, Shakira, Melissa Ethridge, Sarah McLachlan, Fergie, Anastasia, Beverly Knight, Faith Hill, Pink, Sugababes, Martha Wainwright, Isobel Campbell, Beth Gibbons, KT Tunstall, Shingai Shoniwa and Beth Orton.

We remind you that at the moment there's not any official confirmation. Let's wait and see! Anyway, we at Drowned Madonna would be very happy if this rumour proves to be true!
