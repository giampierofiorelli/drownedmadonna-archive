H&M Profit Rises 31 Percent in 2nd Quarter

    Hennes & Mauritz, Europe's second-largest clothing retailer, said Wednesday that its profit rose 31 percent in the second quarter as the company opened new stores and the Madonna-branded collection was well-received by customers.

The company also announced a collaboration with Italian designer Roberto Cavalli.

Net income for the three months through May 31 rose to 3.47 billion Swedish kronor ($503 million) from 2.65 billion kronor a year earlier, H&M said. Sales gained 18 percent to 20.05 billion kronor ($2.91 billion).

"The spring collections have been well-received by the customers during the whole first half-year," the company said.

H&M shares dipped 1.54 percent to 416 kronor ($60.32) Wednesday in Stockholm, however.

Hennes & Mauritz has been opening new stores -- bringing the total to 1,420 -- at the same time it has been expanding its shoes business. The company also introduced a range of clothes branded with pop singer Madonna's name.

"The expansion of online and catalog sales in the Netherlands and of shoes has surpassed expectations," the company said.

H&M noted a number of stores opened in new markets, including Hong Kong, Shanghai, Slovakia, Greece and Qatar.

The company said the Cavalli collection of menswear and womenswear will be available in about 200 of H&M's stores starting Nov. 8.

For the first six months of the year, net income rose to 5.1 billion Swedish kronor ($740 million), from 3.92 billion kronor in the same period a year earlier, the company said. Sales gained 15 percent to 36.8 billion kronor ($5.34 billion).

Source: Associated Press via Yahoo! Finance
