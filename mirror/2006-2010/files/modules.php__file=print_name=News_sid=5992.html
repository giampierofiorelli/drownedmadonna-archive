Madonna asks Bloodshy for a song?
Date: Tuesday, February 15 @ 12:56:40 CET
Topic: Music

Our friend QueenBitch at MadonnaNation Board brought the news that an article at Expressen.se states that Madonna called Bloodshy up herself asking for a song.

"Madonna called me when I was standing on the beach in Thailand," states Bloodshy, "I can’t even begin to explain how unreal it felt standing there talking to Madonna!  We have just started the work so we don't really know when the song will be released. However we are aiming to give her a REAL DISCO_STOMPER".*
