One day with RAISTALLA!
Date: Saturday, October 27 @ 02:41:10 CEST
Topic: Music

Our dear friend Raistalla is currently in Florence, Italy, to receive her award at this year DigiFestival. She will leave Italy and fly back to Los Angeles tomorrow, Sunday 28 October.

Yesterday we at Drowned Madonna had the honour to meet Raistalla and her mother and spend one day with them, walking in Florence and having a dinner with them and the other artists.

Raistalla is an incredible artist and human being! We wish you all the best.
