Stuart Price to perform Down Under.
Date: Friday, February 11 @ 21:16:01 CET
Topic: Music

Stuart Price aka Jacques Lu Cont plays the Good Vibrations Festival this Sunday at the Sidney Myer Music Bowl.

"She’s kind of famed for using people who are good or in vogue at the time," says Price about Madonna, "but she drives that. If you’re working with her, she can really push you to make something that you just wouldn’t have bothered going that far with if you’d been on your own."

Source: The Age.
