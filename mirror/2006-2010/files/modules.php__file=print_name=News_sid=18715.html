Debbie Harry: ''She's such a big star!''
Date: Tuesday, October 02 @ 19:53:54 CEST
Topic: Interviews & Statements

An excerpt of an interview with Blondie's Debbie Harry published on "Steppin’ Out" magazine (12 september 2007): 


Steppin' Out: Like you, Madonna has kept her career going well into her 40s.What are you thoughts about her?

Debbie Harry: We’ve met, but we kind of travel in different worlds. She’s such a big star. I realy do like of her music. I think she’s had some great hits... some really great songs.
