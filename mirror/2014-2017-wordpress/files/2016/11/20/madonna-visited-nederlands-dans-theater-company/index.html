Madonna visited the Nederlands Dans Theater company

Giampiero
on November 20, 2016 at 5:29 pm

Madonna visited the Netherlands Dans Theater company in New York City yesterday.

The NTD is a very talented Dutch modern dance company.
They performed in New York this week as part of their world tour.
Madonna went to see their show and met the crew backstage as you can see from these photos.
