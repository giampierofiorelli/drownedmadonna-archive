Charli XCX on Madonna: “She was someone who didn’t get put in a box”

giampiero
on July 14, 2014 at 8:51 pm
  • 2
  • 0
    Shares

Charli XCX talked about Madonna. Compared to the early Madonna by the New York Times, the I Love It singer and writer, is glad of the comparison because, like Madonna, she is not “a pop star with the conventional idea people have.”

Madonna was someone who didn’t get put in a box. She changed and evolved.

Charli XCX

“They say that?” asked Charli XCX regarding the comparison with the early Madonna. “That’s cool. I don’t think that I’m a pop star. On paper, I’m bad at being a pop star with the conventional idea people have. Madonna was someone who didn’t get put in a box. She changed and evolved. The second someone tries to put me in a box, I will do everything to rebel against that. One thing I don’t understand is how people want you to replicate your past successes. Being an artist should be about freedom and not just becoming one thing, because I think that’s terrible and boring.”
