“Bitch I’m Madonna” Remixes EP available on iTunes.

Dexter
on June 15, 2015 at 11:21 pm
  • 0
  • 0
    Shares

Madonna‘s “Bitch I’m Madonna” Remixes EP is now available on some iTunes stores, including Italy, Australia, Russia, France and Honk Hong.

In the next hours the EP will be available worldwide.

Here’s the tracklist:

1. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Fedde Le Grand Remix] [Explicit]

2. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Rosabel’s Bitch Move Mix] [Explicit]

3. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Sander Kleinenberg Remix] [Explicit]

4. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Junior Sanchez Remix] [Explicit]

5. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Oscar G 305 Dub] [Explicit]

6. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Sick Individuals Remix] [Explicit]

7. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Dirty Pop Remix] [Explicit]

8. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Flechette Remix] [Explicit]

9. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Oscar G Bitch Beats] [Explicit]

10. Bitch I’m Madonna (feat. Nicki Minaj) [Rosabel’s Bitch Move Dub] [Explicit]
