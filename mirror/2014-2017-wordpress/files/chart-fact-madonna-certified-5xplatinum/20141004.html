Chart fact: MADONNA certified 5xPlatinum in USA!

Dexter
on October 4, 2014 at 10:26 am
  • 2
  • 0
    Shares

On October 3rd, 2000, Madonna’s eponymous album MADONNA was certified 5xPlatinum (5.000.000 copies sold) in USA.

It has sold more than 10.000.000 copies worldwide so far.

MADONNA was released on July 27, 1983 by Sire Records. The album was re-released in 1985 for the European market with a new cover and style, and it was renamed Madonna: The First Album.

Madonna released five singles from this album:

Everybody (360.000)
Burning Up (300.000)
Holiday (3.000.000)
Lucky Star (1.100.000)
Borderline (1.550.000).
