All Saints recorded a cover of Madonna’s Like A Prayer.

Dexter
on April 29, 2016 at 5:59 pm
  • 0
  • 0
    Shares

Mel, Nat, Nic and Shaz told the fans they recorded one 1980’s hit for the new BBC Radio 2 selection!
Well, All Saints recorded a cover of Madonna‘s Like A Prayer! Can’t wait to listen to it!

On the second CD there’s a song called Borderline sung by Steve Mason. Probably it is Madonna’s. We will find out on May 6, when the box will be released.

BBC Radio 2 Sounds Of The 80’s, Vol. 2 is in pre-sale on Amazon UK.
