giampiero

    Shares

Boy George chooses Madonna‘s Borderline as one of the best singles of 1984 and talks about the phenomenal appeal of Madonna.

While the Rolling Stone was working on their list of the 100 best singles of 1984, Boy George popped by and offered his thoughts on a few of our selections. Watch the video below.

Boy George hums Madonna‘s Borderline and talks about the time he went to New York City’s Radio City Music Hall to check out her Virgin Tour.

Who knew what she was gonna become? You couldn’t ever imagine how formidable she was gonna be. It was just a phenomenon.

Boy George
