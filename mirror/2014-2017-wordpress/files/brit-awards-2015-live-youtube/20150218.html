The Brit Awards 2015 live on YouTube.

Dexter
on February 18, 2015 at 5:45 pm
  • 0
  • 0
    Shares

The 2015 Brit Awards will be streamed live on YouTube. Click HERE to watch Madonna performing live at the Brits!

Radio 1 stars Dan and Phil will be presenting the official BRITs YouTube live stream. They’ll be joined by fashion and beauty vlogger Patricia Bright. The event will also engage Twitter users as the winner of British Artist Video will be determined by a social media vote.

The Brit Awards 2015 takes over London’s O2 Arena on February 25. The event will be hosted by Ant and Dec. Performing on the night will be Madonna, Paloma Faith, George Ezra, Royal Blood, Taylor Swift, Ed Sheeran and Sam Smith.
