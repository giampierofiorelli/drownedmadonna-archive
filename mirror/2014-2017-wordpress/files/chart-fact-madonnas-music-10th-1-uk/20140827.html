Chart fact: Madonna’s Music 10th #1 in the UK!

Dexter
on August 27, 2014 at 3:12 pm
  • 3
  • 0
    Shares

On this day, August 27, 2000, MUSIC became Madonna‘s 10th song to hit the #1 in the UK singles chart! According to The Official Charts Company, the song has sold 410,000 copies there.

Music is also the 12th (and the last so far) #1 on the US Billboard Hot 100 chart. 

By reaching the top, Music made Madonna the second artist to achieve #1 spot on the Hot 100 in three different decades, the 80s, 90s and 00s, and became the 2nd most-successful dance song of the 2000s in the States, reaching #2 on the Hot Dance Club Play Decade-end Chart, behind Hung Up.
