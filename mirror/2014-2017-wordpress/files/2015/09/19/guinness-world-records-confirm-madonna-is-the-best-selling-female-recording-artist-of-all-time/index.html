Guinness World Records confirm: Madonna is the best selling female recording artist of all time.

Dexter
on September 19, 2015 at 2:35 pm

The new 2016 Edition of the Guinness Book of World Records confirms what we already know: Madonna is the best selling female recording artist of all time.

According to the Guinness Book of World Records

Madonna (USA, b. Madonna Ciccone) has sold more than 300 million albums in her career since debuting with her self-titled release in 1983.
