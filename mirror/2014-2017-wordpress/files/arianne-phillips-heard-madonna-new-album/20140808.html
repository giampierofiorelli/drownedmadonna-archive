Arianne Phillips heard Madonna new album: “It’s great, you will love it!”

giampiero
on August 8, 2014 at 11:31 am
  • 6
  • 0
    Shares

We at DrownedMadonna.com can reveal that Arianne Phillips heard some of the tracks on Madonna new album and she really loves what she heard.

Our reader Craig Casteel (on behalf of Geeky Castillo Productions) met the gorgeous Arianne Phillips – longtime Madonna‘s stylist – at this years San Diego Comic Con on Friday 25 July. Arianne was there to promote Kingsmen. Craig was able to catch up with her and ask a few questions.

Craig asked Arianne Phillips if she “heard anything on Madonna new album yet.”

“Oh yeah, it’s great!” Arianne Phillips said. She was with Madonna in London the week before and heard some of it.

I really like it, and I think the fans will too!

Arianne Phillips

We are going to tell you more very soon. Stay tuned on DrownedMadonna.com to find out what else Arianne told.

Arianne Phillips also took the time to sign some items to Craig.
