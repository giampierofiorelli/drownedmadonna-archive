Arianne Phillips confessed that there are no plans for a Madonna new tour yet, but

giampiero
on August 27, 2014 at 6:06 pm
  • 3
  • 0
    Shares

Madonna‘s long-time stylist Arianne Phillips confirmed what we at DrownedMadonna.com told you at the end of July.

When asked by our reader Craig Casteel, Arianne Phillips confessed that there are no plans for a Madonna new tour yet, but she guesses there will be a tour in 2015.

“So, will there be a tour in 2015?” Craig asked her. Arianne Phillips looked at him, winked, and quietly mouthed “I think so.”

It’s now clear that the rumours of an imminent tour announcement were absolutely untrue.

Arianne asked Craig: “So what has been your favorite tour so far?” He just had to tell her without hesitation… “re-Invention of course!”

“Oh so you like the hits huh?” Arianne said with a smile. And then she added that the re-Invention tour is also her favourite Madonna’s tour.

Thanks to Craig Casteel on behalf of Geeky Castillo Productions.
