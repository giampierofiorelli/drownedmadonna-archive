Madonna & M.I.A. Baby unreleased demo

Giampiero
on August 25, 2014 at 2:35 pm

You may have heard of a song called Baby written by M.I.A. for Madonna‘s MDNA album. M.I.A. demo version leaked back in 2012. We at DrownedMadonna.com can now tell you that Madonna recorded a demo of Baby with M.I.A.

Despite the M.I.A. version – that you can listen above – Madonna and M.I.A. Baby demo version is 1:40 long. You can hear Madonna singing with M.I.A. The two versions are pretty similar.

M.I.A. vocals are quite the same to the leaked demo and louder than Madonna‘s. Even though Baby sounds very minimal, it’s better than Bday Song, but Madonna‘s voice on the yet-unreleased demo of Baby sounds quite similar to her Bday Song vocals.

When M.I.A. demo version leaked online, she tweeted:

Whoever leaked that Baby song should know that I’ve never heard this version before. It sounds crazy different to what I wrote for Madonna. This song wasn’t recorded for my LP, so it’s not complete… besides I now have way more to say to my man.

M.I.A.
